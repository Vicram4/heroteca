Kamar-Taj is where people go once they have lost all hope. It looks like a simple monastery, but it trains the sorcerers who protect the world from threats in other dimensions without almost anyone knowing.

## Comics origin

It appears in *Strange Tales* #110 (July 1963), by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko, though the name only arrives later, as the character's past is fleshed out. Tucked away among the peaks of the Himalayas, it is where the Ancient One, Strange's mentor, lives.

## On screen

[Doctor Strange](/marvel/doctor-strange/) (2016) moves it to Kathmandu, Nepal: Stephen Strange arrives seeking a cure for his hands and meets the Ancient One, Wong and Mordo. The film adds three sanctums around the world (New York, London and Hong Kong) that depend on it.

[Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness](/marvel/doctor-strange-en-el-multiverso-de-la-locura/) (2022) puts it in danger. ||The Scarlet Witch lays waste to it in search of a girl who can travel between worlds.||

It also appears in the animated film [Doctor Strange: The Sorcerer Supreme](/marvel/doctor-strange-el-hechicero-supremo/) (2007), as a school for sorcerers.
