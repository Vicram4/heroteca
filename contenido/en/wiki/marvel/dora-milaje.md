The Dora Milaje protect Wakanda's king with vibranium spears and absolute discipline. Their loyalty is to the throne, not to a person, and that difference creates some of the tensest dilemmas in their story.

## Comics origin

Christopher Priest and Mark Texeira introduce them in *Black Panther* vol. 3 #1 (November 1998). Their name means "adored ones". They are chosen from rival tribes to keep the peace in the country, and in that first version they also have a ceremonial role as the king's future wives, a trait later versions drop.

## On screen

They show up in [Captain America: Civil War](/marvel/capitan-america-civil-war/) and become famous in [Black Panther](/marvel/black-panther/) (2018), where Okoye is head of the guard and one of Wakanda's finest warriors. ||Her dilemma comes when Killmonger seizes the throne: Okoye, faithful to the law, stays at his side until everything falls apart.||

They continue in [Black Panther: Wakanda Forever](/marvel/black-panther-wakanda-forever/) and fight in the great battle of [Avengers: Infinity War](/marvel/vengadores-infinity-war/). Ayo has a fine moment in [The Falcon and the Winter Soldier](/marvel/the-falcon-and-the-winter-soldier-t1/), chasing an old acquaintance.
