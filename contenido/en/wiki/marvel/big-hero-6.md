Big Hero 6 is probably the Marvel adaptation least like its source. An obscure group of Japanese superheroes became a family film about grief, friendship and a nurse robot who has turned into one of Disney's best-loved characters.

## Comics origin

Created by Steven T. Seagle and Duncan Rouleau, the team arrives in a 1998 miniseries as an official superhero group serving the Japanese government, recruited by a secret consortium of politicians and corporations.

## On screen

[Big Hero 6](/marvel/big-hero-6/) (2014), from Walt Disney Animation Studios, keeps the names and little else. It moves the story to San Fransokyo, an imaginary blend of San Francisco and Tokyo, and turns the group into science students around Hiro and his robot Baymax. ||What drives Hiro to form the team is the death of his brother Tadashi, Baymax's creator.||

The story continues in [Big Hero 6: The Series](/marvel/big-hero-6-la-serie/), and Baymax stars in the [Baymax!](/marvel/baymax/) shorts (2022).
