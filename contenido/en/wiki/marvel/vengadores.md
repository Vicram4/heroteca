The Avengers are neither a family nor a group of friends. They are an alliance of heroes with clashing egos and methods who come together when the danger is too great. That tension explains both their victories and their worst crises.

## Comics origin

The team was born in *The Avengers* #1 (September 1963), by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby. Its founders are [Iron Man](/marvel/personajes/iron-man/), Thor, the Hulk, Ant-Man and the Wasp; [Captain America](/marvel/personajes/capitan-america/), often mistaken for a founder, joins shortly afterward in issue 4, once he is rescued from the ice. The lineup has been changing ever since.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Nick Fury assembles the team in [The Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores/), which builds to the battle of New York against Loki. Ant-Man and the Wasp are not among the founders here: the group is Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, the Hulk, Black Widow and Hawkeye.

[Avengers: Age of Ultron](/marvel/vengadores-la-era-de-ultron/) expands the roster, and accords meant to regulate heroes split it in two in [Captain America: Civil War](/marvel/capitan-america-civil-war/). ||Divided, they fail to stop Thanos in Avengers: Infinity War, and the survivors spend Avengers: Endgame undoing his victory.||

### Animation

The collection includes several unconnected versions, from [The Avengers: United They Stand](/marvel/the-avengers-united-they-stand/) (1999) to the [Ultimate Avengers](/marvel/ultimate-avengers/) films and the series [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/).
