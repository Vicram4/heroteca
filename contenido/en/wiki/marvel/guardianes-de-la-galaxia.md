The Guardians are the opposite of a model team: a thief, an assassin, a warrior with no sense of irony, a raccoon who hates being called one and a tree who says three words. What holds them together is that nobody else wants them, and their blend of comedy and feeling grows out of that.

## Comics origin

The name dates from 1969, from a group of future heroes created by Arnold Drake, Roy Thomas and Stan Lee. The team everyone knows is a different one: Dan Abnett and Andy Lanning put it together in *Annihilation: Conquest* #6 (2008) from characters who had no prior connection, Star-Lord, Rocket, Groot, Gamora and Drax among them.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

James Gunn made them famous in [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2014/) (2014), where they land in the same prison and end up fighting Ronan over an orb. [Vol. 2](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-vol-2/) looks into Star-Lord's past and [Vol. 3](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-volumen-3/) into Rocket's. ||That last film closes the era: the group breaks up and Rocket ends up leading a new lineup.||

### Animation

The [2015 series](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2015/) tells its own adventures, with no direct link to the films. [I Am Groot](/marvel/yo-soy-groot-t1/) and [Rocket & Groot](/marvel/rocket-y-groot/) are short pieces built around the duo.
