Pym Particles are a perfect invention for comedy and action: they let a man turn tiny or gigantic in an instant, or an entire building fit in a suitcase. They are also a dangerous technology their creator prefers to keep under lock and key.

## Comics origin

Hank Pym discovers them in his earliest stories, which begin with a seven-page adventure in *Tales to Astonish* #27 (January 1962), by Stan Lee, Larry Lieber and Jack Kirby. With them he becomes Ant-Man and later Goliath and other heroes. He also uses them to give wings to Janet van Dyne, the Wasp.

## On screen

In [Ant-Man](/marvel/ant-man-2015/) (2015), Pym hides them from everyone, especially S.H.I.E.L.D., and a former protégé wants to copy them to make weapons. [Captain America: Civil War](/marvel/capitan-america-civil-war/) offers the surprise of a giant Scott Lang in the middle of the fight. [Ant-Man and the Wasp](/marvel/ant-man-y-la-avispa/) puts them to use on cars and buildings.
