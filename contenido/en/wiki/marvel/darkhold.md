The Darkhold is the kind of object sensible characters ought to burn, and never do. It promises enormous knowledge in exchange for sanity, and whoever opens it tends to believe they will be the one to control it.

## Comics origin

It belongs to the horror vein Marvel explored in the seventies. It is a grimoire with the secrets of Chthon, an ancient dark god who mastered these arts before anyone else and was left bound to Mount Wundagore.

## On screen

It first surfaces in [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-t4/), in the fourth season, as a book granting terribly costly knowledge to its reader, in a plot with Ghost Rider and robots.

[WandaVision](/marvel/wandavision-t1/) shows it in Agatha Harkness's basement. ||It is the series' final clue: in the post-credits scene, Wanda studies it alone.||

[Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness](/marvel/doctor-strange-en-el-multiverso-de-la-locura/) (2022) gives it its biggest role. ||It corrupts Wanda until she becomes the villain, and it also corrupts a version of Strange from another universe.||
