The Quantum Realm is what lies beneath everything: a world so small that time and space stop working normally. It starts out as a danger no one returns from and ends up as the key to several of Marvel's biggest stories.

## Comics origin

It is based on the comics' Microverse, but got a different name because that one was associated with the Micronauts, characters whose rights are held by Hasbro.

## On screen

In [Ant-Man](/marvel/ant-man-2015/) (2015) it is where Janet van Dyne was lost decades ago, and [Ant-Man and the Wasp](/marvel/ant-man-y-la-avispa/) tells of the attempt to rescue her. ||Scott Lang gets trapped inside for five years, and on coming out, in Avengers: Endgame, he realizes it may hold a way of traveling through time.||

[Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania](/marvel/ant-man-y-la-avispa-quantumania/) (2023) turns it into an inhabited world, with cities and strange beings, where Janet hides a secret and [Kang](/marvel/personajes/kang/) lives in exile.
