Mutants don't choose their fate: they are born with a mutation that usually shows itself in adolescence, and from then on people fear them. Marvel found here a simple way to create heroes with no need to invent accidents, and at the same time a powerful way to talk about discrimination.

## Comics origin

Mutants arrive with the [X-Men](/marvel/wiki/x-men/) in 1963, though Marvel considers Namor (1939) the first, something not stated until decades later, in *Fantastic Four Annual* #1. The trait is explained by a gene, the X-gene, which ordinary humans lack.

## On screen

Films like [X-Men](/marvel/x-men/) and [The New Mutants](/marvel/los-nuevos-mutantes/), with its touch of horror, treat mutation as either a curse or a promise, depending on who tells it. On television, [The Gifted](/marvel/the-gifted-los-elegidos/) follows a family on the run, and [Legion](/marvel/legion/) blurs powers with mental illness. [X-Men '97](/marvel/x-men-97/) takes on organized hatred.
