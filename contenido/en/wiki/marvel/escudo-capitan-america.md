It is a defensive weapon turned symbol. More than the costume, it is the shield that marks who Captain America is, which is why the question of who deserves to carry it drives so much of the story.

## Comics origin

The first shield, shaped like a heraldic crest, appears in *Captain America Comics* #1 (March 1941). The round one everyone remembers arrives in the next issue. Later stories explain that it is made from an unrepeatable material containing vibranium, a gift from T'Chaka, king of Wakanda.

## On screen

[Captain America: The First Avenger](/marvel/capitan-america-el-primer-vengador/) mirrors that evolution: Steve first uses a crest-shaped prop for his propaganda shows, then Howard Stark gives him the round vibranium one. He throws it, it bounces and it comes back, making his fight scenes unmistakable.
