Stark Tower sums up its owner: huge, flashy and with his name at the top. For years it is the most recognizable building in Marvel's New York, as much for its silhouette as for the disasters it attracts.

## Comics origin

In the comics it is a Midtown Manhattan complex, with a 93-story main tower and two flanking buildings of 35 and 55 stories. After several eras it comes to serve as the Avengers' base.

## On screen

[The Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores/) (2012) presents it as a building that powers itself with Stark's reactor, and makes it the center of the battle of New York. The letter "A" left on its facade announces its new purpose, and by [Avengers: Age of Ultron](/marvel/vengadores-la-era-de-ultron/) it is the team's headquarters.

||In Spider-Man: Homecoming, Stark has sold it and is moving its contents to another base, which gives the Vulture his plan. In Thunderbolts* it has a new owner and a new name, the Watchtower.||
