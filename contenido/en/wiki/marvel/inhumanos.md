The Inhumans are humanity's distant cousins: a separate society with its own royal family and a ceremony in which each youngster finds out, with no say in it, what they will become. They live apart from the world and distrust it.

## Comics origin

They appear as a group in *Fantastic Four* #45 (December 1965), though Medusa and Gorgon had turned up earlier in the same series. They are the result of Kree experiments on primitive humans. Their powers come from the Terrigen Mist, and their city, Attilan, has a curious precedent: the name already appears in a 1941 Jack Kirby story.

## On screen

They reach television through [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-t2/), where they become central to the plot from the second season on. ||Quake then discovers she is one of them.||

The series [Inhumans](/marvel/inhumans-t1/) (2017), with the royal family in Attilan, located on the Moon, lasted a single season. Their leader, Black Bolt, whose voice can destroy, returns in [Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness](/marvel/doctor-strange-en-el-multiverso-de-la-locura/) with the same actor, Anson Mount, as a version from another universe.
