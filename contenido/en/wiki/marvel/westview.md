Westview is the perfect town, with trimmed lawns, friendly neighbors and plenty of studio-audience humor. That is exactly why it is frightening. Beneath the cheerful surface something doesn't fit, and that tension drives one of Marvel's most original series.

## Comics origin

It doesn't come from any one story: it is the place imagined for the series, a New Jersey town that takes on the atmosphere of different decades of American comedy. Even so, the tone of the couple owes much to several Scarlet Witch comics that inspired its creators.

## On screen

Wanda and Vision lead a happy life here in [WandaVision](/marvel/wandavision-t1/) (2021), which opens as a tribute to fifties sitcoms and moves forward decade by decade. ||None of it is real: Wanda, sunk in grief, has sealed the town in a magic bubble, the Hex, and controls its inhabitants. Monica Rambeau crosses in from outside, and Agatha, the nosy neighbor, turns out to be a witch.||

[Agatha All Along](/marvel/agatha-quien-si-no-t1/) starts in the same town, before its heroine sets out along another road.
