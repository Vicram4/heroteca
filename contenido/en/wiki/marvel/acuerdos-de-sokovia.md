The Sokovia Accords answer a fair question: who watches the heroes? The fallout is so serious because nobody is entirely wrong. Iron Man believes they need oversight; Captain America, that nobody should decide for them.

## Comics origin

The idea comes from *Civil War* (2006), in which a Registration Act forces heroes with powers to reveal their identities to the government and work for it. It is a war between heroes with the same leaders, though under different rules. The Sokovia name belongs to the films.

## On screen

[Captain America: Civil War](/marvel/capitan-america-civil-war/) (2016) revolves around them. After the disaster in [Sokovia](/marvel/wiki/sokovia/), Thaddeus Ross puts the Accords on the table and the Avengers split into two sides. ||Those who refuse end up in a special prison, the Raft, or on the run for years.||

[Black Widow](/marvel/viuda-negra/) shows Natasha as a fugitive for this reason. In [Ant-Man and the Wasp](/marvel/ant-man-y-la-avispa/), Scott Lang is under house arrest for breaking them, trying to lead a normal life.
