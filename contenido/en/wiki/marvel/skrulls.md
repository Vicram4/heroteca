Skrulls can turn into anyone, which makes them the perfect threat for stories of paranoia: your best friend, the president or the hero next to you might not be who they claim. Marvel has used that idea many times.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby introduce them in *Fantastic Four* #2 (1962), making them among the oldest villains of this Marvel era. Their most famous moment is *Secret Invasion* (2008), which reveals they have spent years replacing Earth's heroes.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

They make their live-action debut in [Captain Marvel](/marvel/capitana-marvel/) (2019). ||The film upends the comic: Talos's Skrulls are refugees, not invaders. Spider-Man: Far From Home then reveals that Fury and Hill had been replaced by Talos and his wife.||

[Secret Invasion](/marvel/invasion-secreta-t1/) (2023) takes the comic's name but tells another story, in which Nick Fury has promised the Skrulls a home and a group of them grows tired of waiting. They also appear in [The Marvels](/marvel/the-marvels/).

### Animation

The second season of [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/) adapts the comics' invasion.
