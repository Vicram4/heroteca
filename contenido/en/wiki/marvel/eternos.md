The Eternals have been at our side throughout human history, and many of the gods and heroes of legend could have been them. Theirs is one of Marvel's most cosmic stories: beings with a mission they never chose, handed down by colossal makers.

## Comics origin

Jack Kirby creates them on his own in *The Eternals* #1 (July 1976). In this mythology, the Celestials experiment on early humans a million years ago, giving rise to two branches: the Eternals and their enemies, the monstrous Deviants. [Thanos](/marvel/personajes/thanos/) belongs to this family too in the comics, born among the Eternals of Titan.

## On screen

[Eternals](/marvel/eternals/) (2021), by Chloé Zhao, spans seven thousand years of history with ten members, including Sersi, Ikaris and Thena, apart for centuries and forced to reunite. ||They discover their real mission was never to protect humanity, but to allow the birth of a Celestial inside the Earth, which splits the group in two.||
