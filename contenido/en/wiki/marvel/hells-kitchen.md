Hell's Kitchen is a real neighborhood on Manhattan's West Side, and Marvel has turned it into the darkest corner of its New York. Far from the Avengers' skyscrapers, its problems are corruption, extortion and neighbors no one defends.

## Comics origin

Matt Murdock is born and raised here, and nearly the whole [Daredevil](/marvel/personajes/daredevil/) series takes place on these streets. The neighborhood is both his strength and his weakness, because everyone he cares about lives there, and his enemies know it.

## On screen

The film [Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil/) (2003) already uses it as a setting, but the Netflix series gets the most out of it. [Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil-t1/) shows a neighborhood damaged by the Incident in New York, being rebuilt and coveted by Wilson Fisk. [Jessica Jones](/marvel/jessica-jones-t1/) lives here too, and [The Defenders](/marvel/the-defenders-t1/) ends up turning it into a battlefield.

[Daredevil: Born Again](/marvel/daredevil-born-again-t1/) returns to the same streets, now with Fisk in politics.
