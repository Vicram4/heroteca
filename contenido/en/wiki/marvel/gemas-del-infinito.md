The Infinity Stones are six stones, each tied to one aspect of existence: Space, Mind, Reality, Power, Time and Soul. They are dangerous on their own; together they let whoever gathers them impose their will on the entire universe. They are the thread running through much of Marvel's film output.

## Comics origin

The first turns up in *Marvel Premiere* #1 (1972), when it is called a "Soul Gem". A 1976 Captain Marvel story later establishes that there are six, each with a different power. They became famous through *The Infinity Gauntlet* (1991), in which Thanos gathers them to seize absolute power.

## On screen

For years the Cinematic Universe introduces them without saying what they are, hidden inside objects that seem unrelated:

- **Space:** the Tesseract in [Captain America: The First Avenger](/marvel/capitan-america-el-primer-vengador/).
- **Mind:** Loki's scepter in [The Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores/).
- **Reality:** the Aether in [Thor: The Dark World](/marvel/thor-el-mundo-oscuro/).
- **Power:** the orb in [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2014/).
- **Time:** the Eye of Agamotto in [Doctor Strange](/marvel/doctor-strange/).
- **Soul:** the last to appear, hidden on Vormir until [Avengers: Infinity War](/marvel/vengadores-infinity-war/).
