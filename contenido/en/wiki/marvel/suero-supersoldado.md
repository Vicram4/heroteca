The serum is Marvel's great scientific obsession. It brings a person to the peak of their physical abilities, and it also amplifies what lies within: the good become better and the bad become worse. Nearly every attempt to copy it has ended badly.

## Comics origin

It arrives with Captain America in 1941. Steve Rogers, a frail young man rejected by the army, is chosen for a secret government program, Project Rebirth, and is the serum's first test subject.

## On screen

[Captain America: The First Avenger](/marvel/capitan-america-el-primer-vengador/) presents it as the work of Dr. Erskine, who gives it to Steve. The Red Skull took it too, in an earlier version that left him with that face. In [The Incredible Hulk](/marvel/el-increible-hulk-2008/) (2008), General Ross has spent years trying to recover the program: both the Hulk and the Abomination come out of it.

[The Falcon and the Winter Soldier](/marvel/the-falcon-and-the-winter-soldier-t1/) shows its darkest side through Isaiah Bradley, a Black soldier who was experimented on in secret and locked away for decades. ||John Walker also takes it in a moment of desperation, with disastrous results.|| Bradley returns in [Captain America: Brave New World](/marvel/capitan-america-brave-new-world/).
