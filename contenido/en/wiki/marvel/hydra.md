Hydra is summed up by its motto: cut off one head and two more grow back. It is S.H.I.E.L.D.'s archenemy, a clandestine organization that wants to impose order by force and survives every defeat.

## Comics origin

It debuts in the same *Strange Tales* #135 (August 1965) as S.H.I.E.L.D., so the two are born at war. It soon becomes tied to Baron Strucker, a former Nazi officer and an enemy of Nick Fury since the war.

## On screen

[Captain America: The First Avenger](/marvel/capitan-america-el-primer-vengador/) presents it as the Nazis' science division under the Red Skull, before it breaks with them to pursue its own goals. Arnim Zola supplies its brains and the [Winter Soldier](/marvel/personajes/soldado-de-invierno/) its enforcer.
