Kingsman is the sharpest and also the fondest parody of the British secret agent: tailored suits, good manners and over-the-top violence. It is an agency that answers to no government and recruits on merit, not family name, or at least tries to.

## Comics origin

It comes from the comic *The Secret Service* (2012), by Mark Millar and Dave Gibbons, published by Marvel within the independent Millarworld universe, from an idea by Millar and director Matthew Vaughn. That is why it belongs to this collection, though nothing connects it to the superheroes.

## On screen

In [Kingsman: The Secret Service](/marvel/kingsman-servicio-secreto/) (2014), Harry Hart (Colin Firth) recruits a young man with no prospects, Eggsy (Taron Egerton), to take on a billionaire with a mad plan. [Kingsman: The Golden Circle](/marvel/kingsman-el-circulo-de-oro/) (2017) takes them to the United States. ||Early in the film the agency's headquarters are destroyed, and the survivors seek help from their American cousins, the Statesman.||

[The King's Man](/marvel/the-king-s-man-la-primera-mision/) (2021) goes backward, to World War I, and tells the organization's birth with Ralph Fiennes.
