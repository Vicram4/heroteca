Mjolnir is probably Marvel's most famous object, and it all revolves around one rule: only those who are worthy can lift it. It is a weapon, but also a moral test that has prompted arguments, jokes and the occasional moving moment.

## Comics origin

It arrives with Thor in *Journey into Mystery* #83 (1962), by Stan Lee, Larry Lieber and Jack Kirby. In that first version a doctor, Donald Blake, finds a cane in a Norwegian cave that turns into the hammer when struck on the ground. The inscription says it plainly: whoever holds it, if worthy, shall have the god's power. Others have lifted it over time, such as Beta Ray Bill, an alien whom Odin recognizes as worthy.

## On screen

In [Thor](/marvel/thor/) (2011), Odin sends it to Earth along with his banished son, and the short [A Funny Thing Happened on the Way to Thor's Hammer](/marvel/algo-divertido-sucedio-de-camino-al-martillo-de-thor/) pokes fun at the situation. The party in [Avengers: Age of Ultron](/marvel/vengadores-la-era-de-ultron/) turns the matter into a game among the heroes.
