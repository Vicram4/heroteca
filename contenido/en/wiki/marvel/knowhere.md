Knowhere is where you end up when you have nowhere else to go: a seedy port at the end of everything, raised inside a colossal skull. The name is a pun on "nowhere", and it sounds exactly the same.

## Comics origin

It comes from Marvel's cosmic era of the 2000s, in the pages of *Nova*. It is the severed head of a Celestial floating at the very edge of space and time, a stopover for travelers with a view of the end of everything.

## On screen

It is first seen in the post-credits scene of [Thor: The Dark World](/marvel/thor-el-mundo-oscuro/), and in [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2014/) it is where the group tries to sell the orb and meets the Collector. ||In Avengers: Infinity War, Thanos ravages it in search of the Reality Stone.||

The [Guardians](/marvel/wiki/guardianes-de-la-galaxia/) eventually rebuild it and settle there, as seen in the [Holiday Special](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-especial-felices-fiestas/) and [Vol. 3](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-volumen-3/).
