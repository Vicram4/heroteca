The TVA is time travel at its drabbest. It has no starships or heroes, only forms, elevators and a seventies office look. Its employees decide, without batting an eye, which realities deserve to go on existing.

## Comics origin

It debuts in *Thor* #372 (October 1986), by Walt Simonson and Sal Buscema, with an in-joke: its employees are all clones of Mark Gruenwald, the Marvel editor obsessed with continuity who helped define how its alternate realities are classified.

## On screen

[Loki](/marvel/loki-t1/) makes it central: the god of mischief is arrested and put to work with an agent named Mobius while he discovers who is really in charge. ||The staff turn out to be variants from other worlds with their memories wiped, and at the top is He Who Remains, a version of Kang.|| The [second season](/marvel/loki-t2/) follows the organization's collapse.

[Deadpool & Wolverine](/marvel/deadpool-y-wolverine/) plays the idea for comedy: an unscrupulous agent uses it for his own plans, and whatever gets in the way ends up in the Void, a dumping ground for time.
