Asgard is the golden realm of Odin and his family: a place between legend and science fiction, with palaces, eternal warriors and a bridge of light leading to other worlds. It is as grand as it is unable to escape its own family betrayals.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee, Larry Lieber and Jack Kirby introduce it in *Journey into Mystery* #85 (October 1962), borrowing nearly everything from the Norse sagas. Later, after the comics' Ragnarok, Thor rebuilds it on Earth, near Broxton, Oklahoma, a contrast that yields plenty of fun.

## On screen

[Thor](/marvel/thor/) (2011) presents it as a civilization so advanced it can be mistaken for magic. [Thor: The Dark World](/marvel/thor-el-mundo-oscuro/) puts it under attack, and [Thor: Ragnarok](/marvel/thor-ragnarok/) pushes it to the limit. ||Thor realizes that Asgard is its people and not a place, and lets it be destroyed to save them. The survivors settle in a Norwegian fishing village, the New Asgard seen in Avengers: Endgame.||

It also features in [Loki](/marvel/loki-t1/), and [Thor: Tales of Asgard](/marvel/thor-tales-of-asgard/) (2011) is an animated film, unrelated to the others, with Thor and Loki as youngsters.
