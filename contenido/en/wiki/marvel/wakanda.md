Wakanda seems to be a poor rural country, but it is really the most advanced nation on Earth. It was never colonized and lives off a secret: the vibranium beneath its soil. Its great dilemma is whether to stay hidden or help everyone else.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby introduce it in *Fantastic Four* #52 (July 1966), the same issue that presents Black Panther. It is a pioneering idea for its time: an independent, prosperous African country, ahead of the West in science.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

It surfaces in [Captain America: Civil War](/marvel/capitan-america-civil-war/), its first film appearance, and is shown in all its splendor in [Black Panther](/marvel/black-panther/) (2018): a futuristic city hidden behind a fake jungle. ||At the end of the film, T'Challa decides to open the country to the world.|| It hosts the great battle of [Avengers: Infinity War](/marvel/vengadores-infinity-war/), and [Black Panther: Wakanda Forever](/marvel/black-panther-wakanda-forever/) sets it against another secret nation, Talokan.

### Animation

It already mattered before the films in [Ultimate Avengers 2](/marvel/ultimate-avengers-2-rise-of-the-panther/) (2006), and later turns up in a [LEGO](/marvel/lego-marvel-super-heroes-pantera-negra-problemas-en-wakanda/) short.
