The Defenders don't want to be a team. Each has their own problems and their own streets, and they join up only when there is no other way. Their appeal lies in that uneasy coexistence, in comics and on television alike.

## Comics origin

They appear as the Defenders in *Marvel Feature* #1 (December 1971), with Doctor Strange, the Hulk and Namor, later joined by the Silver Surfer. They are known as a "non-team": no charter, no fixed base and hardly any desire to stay together.

## On screen

The collection has them only in the Netflix version, which changes the lineup entirely and brings the scale down to the streets of New York. After the solo series [Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil-t1/), [Jessica Jones](/marvel/jessica-jones-t1/), [Luke Cage](/marvel/luke-cage-t1/) and [Iron Fist](/marvel/iron-fist-t1/), the four come together in the miniseries [The Defenders](/marvel/the-defenders-t1/) (2017) to face [the Hand](/marvel/wiki/la-mano/). ||Along the way Elektra returns as the organization's weapon, and Matt Murdock ends up giving everything to stop her.||
