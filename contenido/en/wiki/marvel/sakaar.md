Sakaar is a planet ringed by wormholes where everything the universe throws away ends up, and sometimes people too. It is a lawless world where you survive by fighting in the arena, and for the Hulk it is the first place that greets him with applause instead of fear.

## Comics origin

It is the setting of the "Planet Hulk" saga (2006), in which some of Earth's heroes banish the Hulk to space, judging him too dangerous. There he ends up a slave, a gladiator and, later, a king.

## On screen

The animated film [Planet Hulk](/marvel/planet-hulk/) (2010) adapts that story quite faithfully, with the Hulk in the arena and a revolution under way.

[Thor: Ragnarok](/marvel/thor-ragnarok/) (2017) changes it completely. The tone is now a colorful, zany comedy, with a Grandmaster who runs the fights for sheer amusement. Thor lands there and finds out who the local champion is. ||The rival he must face is the Hulk, who has gone two years without turning back into Bruce Banner.||
