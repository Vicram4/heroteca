The Phoenix is the X-Men's most famous tragedy: absolute power joined to an ordinary woman, and the question of whether anyone can carry such a thing without getting lost. For decades it has stood as the high point of their history.

## Comics origin

Chris Claremont and Dave Cockrum introduce it in *Uncanny X-Men* #101 (October 1976). It unfolds in "The Dark Phoenix Saga", one of Marvel's best-loved stories, in which Jean Grey, manipulated and overcome by her power, turns destructive. Its ending is still remembered today. It represents the cosmic force of destruction and rebirth, like the mythological bird.

## On screen

### Films

Two adaptations. [X-Men: The Last Stand](/marvel/x-men-la-decision-final/) (2006) removes the cosmic element and treats it as a second personality of Jean's. [Dark Phoenix](/marvel/x-men-fenix-oscura/) (2019) retells it with the younger cast, this time with a force from space. ||In both, Jean ends up dying, just as in the original comic.||

### Animation

[X-Men: The Animated Series](/marvel/x-men-la-serie-animada/) gives it one of its finest arcs, very faithful to the comics, and for many the best adaptation of the story.
