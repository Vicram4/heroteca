The Hand is a secret society of ninjas, as ancient as it is dangerous, moving between organized crime and mysticism. It tends to lurk behind what happens on the streets of New York, with almost nobody aware it exists.

## Comics origin

Frank Miller creates it for his run on Daredevil, and it debuts in *Daredevil* #174 (September 1981), shortly after Elektra herself, who had infiltrated its ranks. Its warriors often come back from death to serve it.

## On screen

### Netflix series

It is the great enemy of this corner of Marvel. [Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil-t1/) introduces it gradually, [the second season](/marvel/daredevil-t2/) brings it to the fore and [Iron Fist](/marvel/iron-fist-t1/) shows another of its branches. ||The Defenders reveals that a few leaders hold the organization together, and that their immortality is what drives them to risk everything.||

### Film

In [Elektra](/marvel/elektra/) (2005), a version unrelated to the above, the heroine faces these assassins to protect a young girl and her father.
