S.H.I.E.L.D. is where classic espionage meets superheroes: agents in suits, secret bases and files on anyone with powers. It is the piece that connects stories and characters who would otherwise never meet.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby create it in *Strange Tales* #135 (August 1965), at the height of the spy-movie craze, and put Nick Fury in charge, until then a World War II sergeant. The name is an acronym whose meaning has changed several times over the years.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Coulson quietly introduces it in [Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-2008/), and Fury uses it to bring the [Avengers](/marvel/wiki/vengadores/) together. [Captain Marvel](/marvel/capitana-marvel/) shows its nineties. ||Captain America: The Winter Soldier reveals that Hydra has spent decades embedded in the agency, and it collapses.||

[Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-t1/) follows Coulson's team for seven seasons, and shorts like [Item 47](/marvel/articulo-47/) and [The Consultant](/marvel/the-consultant/) add small stories about its agents.

### Other versions

[Nick Fury: Agent of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/nick-furia-agente-de-shield/) (1998), with David Hasselhoff, is a standalone TV movie, long before the Cinematic Universe.
