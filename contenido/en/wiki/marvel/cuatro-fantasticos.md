The Fantastic Four are more a family than a team: they argue, they love each other and they keep no secret identities. Reed Richards stretches, Sue Storm turns invisible, her brother Johnny burns, and Ben Grimm is stuck in a stone body he never asked for.

## Comics origin

*The Fantastic Four* #1 (November 1961), by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby, launches modern Marvel: heroes with ordinary problems and a tone far closer to real life than was usual at the time. The series introduces many of the universe's big names, such as Doctor Doom, the Silver Surfer and the Skrulls.

## On screen

### Films

Three unconnected attempts came before the Cinematic Universe: [Fantastic Four](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-2005/) (2005) and [its Silver Surfer sequel](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-y-silver-surfer/), then [Fantastic Four](/marvel/cuatro-fantasticos/) (2015). [The Fantastic Four: First Steps](/marvel/los-cuatro-fantasticos-primeros-pasos/) (2025) sets them in a retro-futurist sixties world, separate from the rest of the saga, against Galactus.

### Animation

They have had their own series since [1967](/marvel/the-fantastic-four-1967/), including one from [2006](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-2006/). The oddest is [Fred and Barney Meet the Thing](/marvel/fred-and-barney-meet-the-thing/), which pairs the Thing with the Flintstones.
