Weapon X is the answer to the question that haunts Wolverine for decades: who did this to him. It is a clandestine government program that treats mutants as weaponry and leaves its victims with no memories and wounds that never heal.

## Comics origin

The oldest mention comes from Wolverine's first appearance, in *The Incredible Hulk* #180 (October 1974). The program takes shape in "Weapon X", Barry Windsor-Smith's story in *Marvel Comics Presents* (1991), which shows in full detail the painful process that bonds adamantium to his skeleton.

## On screen

### Films

[X2](/marvel/x-men-2/) shows the Alkali Lake base and William Stryker, the man who ran the experiment. [X-Men Origins: Wolverine](/marvel/x-men-origenes-lobezno/) tells the whole process and puts an early version of Deadpool in the same program. [X-Men: Apocalypse](/marvel/x-men-apocalipsis/) includes a brief visit to the facility. [Logan](/marvel/logan/) picks up the idea through a modern heir: Transigen. ||This company breeds children from mutant DNA to repeat the formula.||

### Animation

[Hulk Vs.](/marvel/hulk-vs/) (2009) sets Wolverine against his former program colleagues.
