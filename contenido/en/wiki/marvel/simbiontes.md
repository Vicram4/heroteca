A symbiote looks at first like a black suit. It is actually alive: it clings to someone, gives them great strength, and in return amplifies the worst in them. That mix of power and addiction has made Venom one of Marvel's most popular characters.

## Comics origin

They arrive in May 1984, in *The Amazing Spider-Man* #252 and two other titles that month, when Spider-Man returns home with a black suit brought from space after *Secret Wars*. It later turns out to be alive. Much later comes their deepest origin: they were created by an evil primordial god, Knull.

## On screen

### Films

[Spider-Man 3](/marvel/spider-man-3/) (2007) follows that story closely, with the black suit and Eddie Brock turned into Venom. [Venom](/marvel/venom/) (2018) gives it its own saga and shifts the tone toward a strange couples comedy, [Venom: Let There Be Carnage](/marvel/venom-habra-matanza/) brings in Carnage, and [Venom: The Last Dance](/marvel/venom-el-ultimo-baile/) adds Knull and more symbiotes.

### Animation

[The Spectacular Spider-Man](/marvel/el-espectacular-spider-man/) tells the suit's arrival very well, and there is a lighter version in [LEGO](/marvel/lego-marvel-spider-man-vexed-by-venom/).
