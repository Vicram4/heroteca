The Ten Rings is the Cinematic Universe's longest thread: they are there from the first scene of the first film, though their real story isn't told until thirteen years later.

## Comics origin

The organization is an invention of the films. In the comics, the ten rings are the weapons of the Mandarin, one of Iron Man's great enemies. The [Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-la-serie-animada/) series of the nineties makes him its main villain.

## On screen

They kidnap Tony Stark at the start of [Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-2008/), in Afghanistan, which gives rise to the hero. [Iron Man 3](/marvel/iron-man-3/) introduces the Mandarin as their leader. ||He turns out to be an actor hired to frighten people, and the short All Hail the King hints that the real one is still out there.||

[Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings](/marvel/shang-chi-y-la-leyenda-de-los-diez-anillos/) (2021) explains it all: an immortal warlord, Xu Wenwu, founded the organization a thousand years ago and has carried the rings ever since. He is also the hero's father.
