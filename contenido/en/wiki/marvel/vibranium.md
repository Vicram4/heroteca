Vibranium is Marvel's most famous metal: light, extremely tough and able to absorb vibrations. Whoever controls it holds a huge advantage, which is the basis of Wakanda's power and of why so many people want to take it.

## Comics origin

It is first mentioned in *Daredevil* #13 (February 1966), in a version that would come to be called "anti-metal". A few months later, *Fantastic Four* #53 (August 1966) presents the variety everyone knows, mined in Wakanda. That is where Black Panther's suit and part of Captain America's shield come from.

## On screen

[Captain America: The First Avenger](/marvel/capitan-america-el-primer-vengador/) explains that Howard Stark has used all he had to make a shield. In [Avengers: Age of Ultron](/marvel/vengadores-la-era-de-ultron/) it is what Ultron steals from a smuggler to build himself a perfect body.

[Black Panther](/marvel/black-panther/) shows its origin in a meteorite and the culture built around it. [Black Panther: Wakanda Forever](/marvel/black-panther-wakanda-forever/) ends the idea that Wakanda is the only place it exists: Talokan has it too.
