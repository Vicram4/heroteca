The Kree are one of Marvel's great space powers: a militaristic, disciplined empire ruled by an artificial intelligence, the Supreme Intelligence. They consider themselves superior to others and tend to treat those unlike them as pawns.

## Comics origin

Jack Kirby and Stan Lee introduce them in *Fantastic Four* #65 (August 1967), with Ronan the Accuser dispatched to Earth after one of their Sentries is destroyed there. Their long war with the Skrulls provides several of Marvel's great stories, and they are also the makers of the [Inhumans](/marvel/wiki/inhumanos/).

## On screen

Ronan is the villain of [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2014/) (2014), a Kree who breaks with his own empire. [Captain Marvel](/marvel/capitana-marvel/) (2019) first presents them as heroes at war. ||The film turns it all around: the Kree are the invaders, and Carol Danvers has been manipulated with a false past.|| That war continues in [The Marvels](/marvel/the-marvels/) (2023), on their planet, Hala, now in ruins.

In [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-t2/) they are the makers of the Inhumans, and in [the fifth season](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-t5/) they keep humanity enslaved in the future.
