Adamantium is the hardest thing in the Marvel universe, and its fame is tied to one particular character. Wolverine's claws wouldn't be the same without it, nor would his tragedy: the metal makes him nearly invincible, but it was put in him without his consent.

## Comics origin

It is first mentioned in *The Avengers* #66 (July 1969), in a story by Roy Thomas and Barry Windsor-Smith. It comes up there in connection with Ultron, who ends up coated in it, and only later reaches Wolverine, who makes it famous. There is also a less resistant "secondary" variant, to explain why it sometimes gets damaged.

## On screen

### X-Men films

It is part of Wolverine from the first film, and [X-Men Origins: Wolverine](/marvel/x-men-origenes-lobezno/) shows how it was implanted. It plays an important role in [The Wolverine](/marvel/lobezno-inmortal/). ||In Logan it turns against him: the metal slowly poisons him, now that his healing factor can no longer keep up.||

### Cinematic Universe

[Captain America: Brave New World](/marvel/capitan-america-brave-new-world/) (2025) finally brings it into the saga. ||It is found in the Celestial remains left in the ocean after Eternals, sparking an international race to obtain it.||
