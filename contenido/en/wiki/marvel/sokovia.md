Sokovia is the country nobody had heard of until it was destroyed. What happens there changes the Cinematic Universe, because for the first time citizens ask who pays for the wreckage when heroes save the world.

## Comics origin

It belongs to the Cinematic Universe, with no direct counterpart in the comics: a landlocked territory in Eastern Europe, home of the Maximoff twins and Helmut Zemo.

## On screen

[Avengers: Age of Ultron](/marvel/vengadores-la-era-de-ultron/) (2015) opens with an assault on a Hydra base in this country, where the experiments on the twins took place. ||The film ends there too: Ultron lifts the capital, Novi Grad, to smash it into the Earth. The Avengers destroy it in midair and save most of the population, but Quicksilver dies.||

[Captain America: Civil War](/marvel/capitan-america-civil-war/) deals with the fallout: a Sokovian seeking revenge and [accords](/marvel/wiki/acuerdos-de-sokovia/) named after the country. [WandaVision](/marvel/wandavision-t1/) shows Wanda's childhood there, and [The Falcon and the Winter Soldier](/marvel/the-falcon-and-the-winter-soldier-t1/) returns to visit the memorial to its dead.
