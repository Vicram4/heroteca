Xavier's School is a boarding school with a very particular basement. Upstairs are classrooms and gardens where young mutants learn to live with what they are; below are the X-Men's jet, the Danger Room and Cerebro, the machine Xavier uses to locate other mutants.

## Comics origin

It stands at 1407 Graymalkin Lane, in Salem Center (Westchester County, New York), and has been the center of the series from the start. The first two generations of the team train there, and its later history is full of rebuilds, destructions and changes of name.

## On screen

The first time Rogue enters it, in [X-Men](/marvel/x-men/) (2000), viewers discover it along with her. It lives through its worst moment in [X2](/marvel/x-men-2/), during the assault by Stryker's soldiers. [X-Men: First Class](/marvel/x-men-primera-generacion/) shows Xavier opening the family home to his first students.

In a different tone, Deadpool sneaks in during [Deadpool](/marvel/deadpool/) and jokes about how empty it seems. It is also a fixture of [X-Men: The Animated Series](/marvel/x-men-la-serie-animada/) and [X-Men: Evolution](/marvel/x-men-evolucion/).
