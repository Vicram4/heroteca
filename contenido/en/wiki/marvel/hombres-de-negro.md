The Men in Black work so that nobody knows they work. They watch over aliens settled on Earth as if running an immigration office, and they wipe the memory of anyone who sees too much. The premise is a simple joke: half of what we can't explain has a ridiculous explanation.

## Comics origin

Many see them as something outside Marvel, but the saga comes from a comic by Lowell Cunningham published at Malibu, an imprint Marvel ended up buying. That is why it belongs to this collection. Those pages had a darker, more conspiratorial tone than the films.

## On screen

[Men in Black](/marvel/men-in-black/) (1997), by Barry Sonnenfeld, pairs veteran Agent K (Tommy Lee Jones) with rookie Agent J (Will Smith) and hits on a comedy formula repeated in [Men in Black II](/marvel/men-in-black-ii/). In [Men in Black 3](/marvel/men-in-black-3/), J travels to 1969. ||There he meets a young K and discovers what happened between them before he could remember it.||

[Men in Black: International](/marvel/men-in-black-international/) (2019) widens the world with new leads and the London branch.
