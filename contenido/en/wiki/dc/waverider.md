The Waverider is the [Legends](/dc/wiki/leyendas-del-manana/)' floating home: bedrooms, a sick bay and a cockpit from which any century can be reached. It sits halfway between a spaceship and a shared apartment.

## Origin

The name comes from the comics, but there it wasn't a ship but a character, a time traveler from the nineties. The series borrows it to name Rip Hunter's vehicle.

## On screen

It appears in [DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t1/), where Rip takes it without permission from the Time Masters to recruit his team and chase Vandal Savage. It is run by Gideon, its artificial intelligence, who knows everything that can happen along the timeline and tends to comment on it with some irony.
