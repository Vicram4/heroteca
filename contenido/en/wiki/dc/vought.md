Vought is the real enemy in [The Boys](/dc/wiki/the-boys/): a big corporation to which superheroes are a product. It owns the Seven, its star group led by [Homelander](/dc/personajes/patriota/), and it sells everything from movies to security for cities.

## Comics origin

In Ennis and Robertson's comics it is called Vought-American and has made weapons for decades. Supers bring it its biggest profits, and its history is full of covered-up failures. Its secret is Compound V, created by Jonah Vogelbaum.

## On screen

The series [The Boys](/dc/the-boys-t1/) presents it as a mix of movie studio and big business, with executives like Madelyn Stillwell. ||The heroes were not chosen: Vought injected babies with Compound V.|| ||The second season introduces Stormfront, a new member of the Seven who turns out to be a former Nazi connected to the company's founder.||
