In Watchmen almost nobody has powers: the masked heroes are ordinary people in costumes, and many have less than admirable reasons for putting them on. The Minutemen are the first generation, in the forties. Their successors, in the thick of the Cold War, try to form another group, the Crimebusters, which never gets going.

## Comics origin

Alan Moore, Dave Gibbons and John Higgins publish *Watchmen* between 1986 and 1987, in twelve issues. Its story is set in an alternate 1985 where masked heroes have been banned, and only [Doctor Manhattan](/dc/personajes/doctor-manhattan/) has real powers.

## On screen

- **[Watchmen](/dc/watchmen/) (2009):** Zack Snyder's film stays close to the comic, with [Rorschach](/dc/personajes/rorschach/) investigating the Comedian's death. ||Ozymandias sacrifices millions of people to stop a nuclear war; the comic uses a giant squid and the film blames Doctor Manhattan.||
- **[Watchmen](/dc/watchmen-2019/) (2019):** the HBO series is a sequel set in Tulsa, decades later. ||It reveals that Hooded Justice, the first masked hero, was a Black man hiding his identity.||
