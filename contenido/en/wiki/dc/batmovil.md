The Batmobile is almost another of [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/)'s trademarks. It is a car, but also a weapon, a statement of intent and a toy, and each director gives it a shape of their own.

## Comics origin

The idea of a special car comes from *Detective Comics* #27 (May 1939), but the name doesn't appear until issue 48. For years, it is just a red car, with little to set it apart.

## On screen

- **[Batman](/dc/batman/) (1966):** George Barris adapted a concept car from the previous decade, the Lincoln Futura. Its design is among the best remembered.
- **[Batman](/dc/batman-1989/) (1989):** Tim Burton turns it into a long, black, elegant machine.
- **[Batman Forever](/dc/batman-forever/):** with very flashy lines, it lights up from within.
- **[Batman Begins](/dc/batman-begins/):** Christopher Nolan reinvents it as a military vehicle, the Tumbler.
- **[Batman v Superman](/dc/batman-vs-superman-el-amanecer-de-la-justicia/):** a heavy tank for a more brutal Batman.
- **[The Batman](/dc/the-batman/):** now a powerful classic car with no frills, almost a horror-movie monster.
