The Legion of Doom answers an obvious question: if the heroes team up, why wouldn't the villains? It nearly always appears with [Lex Luthor](/dc/personajes/lex-luthor/) at the head or very close to it.

## Origin

It comes not from the comics but from the animated series *Challenge of the Superfriends* (1978), by Hanna-Barbera. It later entered DC's publications.

## On screen

- **[Doom](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-perdicion/):** Vandal Savage gathers a group of villains to turn the League's own weaknesses against it.
- **[Attack of the Legion of Doom](/dc/lego-dc-comics-super-heroes-la-liga-de-la-justicia-el-ataque-de-la-legion-del-mal/):** the LEGO version, with far more humor.
- **[DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t2/):** in the second season, it is made up of [Reverse-Flash](/dc/personajes/flash-reverso/), Damien Darhk and Malcolm Merlyn.
