"Metahuman" is the DC universe's word for people with superpowers. It works as a scientific term or as an uncomfortable label: it is used both by those who want to understand them and those who want them under control.

## Comics origin

It is first used by the Dominators, aliens who attack Earth in the saga *Invasion!* (1988), and gradually becomes widespread. It also explains that some humans are born with a genetic variant, the metagene, which can be activated.

## On screen

- **[The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/):** it is the keyword of the series. The [S.T.A.R. Labs](/dc/wiki/star-labs/) explosion creates almost all the villains of its early seasons, whom the team calls "metas".
- **[Black Lightning](/dc/black-lightning-t1/):** the powers of [Jefferson Pierce](/dc/personajes/black-lightning/) and others are tied to a secret government experiment.
- **[Batman v Superman](/dc/batman-vs-superman-el-amanecer-de-la-justicia/):** [Lex Luthor](/dc/personajes/lex-luthor/) gathers files on metahumans, offering a first look at other members of the League.
