The Justice League brings DC's best-known heroes together when a threat is too big for them separately. Its appeal lies in the clash of personalities: Superman's idealism, Batman's distrust and Wonder Woman's experience around one table.

## Comics origin

It debuts in *The Brave and the Bold* #28 (March 1960). The idea was mostly commercial, since each character promoted the others, but it also helped DC's universe feel like one shared world.

## On screen

### Live-action films

[Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/) (2017) was finished by Joss Whedon after Zack Snyder left the shoot, and came out far shorter than planned. [Zack Snyder's Justice League](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-de-zack-snyder/) (2021) restores his cut, about four hours long. In both, Batman assembles the group against [Steppenwolf](/dc/personajes/steppenwolf/). ||Both versions bring back Superman, who died in Batman v Superman, with the help of a Mother Box.||

### Animated films

The collection holds many standalone versions. [The New Frontier](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-la-nueva-frontera/) sets it in the fifties, [War](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-guerra/) tells how it forms during the invasion from [Apokolips](/dc/wiki/apokolips/), and [Gods and Monsters](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-dioses-y-monstruos/) imagines a much darker League. In [Doom](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-perdicion/), Batman's plans for stopping his teammates fall into the wrong hands.
