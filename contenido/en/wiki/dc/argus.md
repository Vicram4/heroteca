A.R.G.U.S. is the bureaucratic, less heroic side of the superhero world: offices, files and missions that are never on record anywhere. If there is dirty work involved, [Amanda Waller](/dc/personajes/amanda-waller/) is almost always behind it.

## Comics origin

It is a recent creation: Geoff Johns and Gene Ha introduce it in *Justice League* vol. 2 #7, during DC's 2011 comics relaunch.

## On screen

- **[Arrow](/dc/arrow-t2/):** here it is a major piece, with Waller at the head and Lyla Michaels, John Diggle's wife, among its agents. Oliver Queen deals with it for years, and in his past as well. ||Lyla ends up running the agency.||
- **[Suicide Squad](/dc/wiki/escuadron-suicida/) films:** Waller's people control the prisoners in [Suicide Squad](/dc/escuadron-suicida/) and [The Suicide Squad](/dc/el-escuadron-suicida/), and that connection carries into [Peacemaker](/dc/el-pacificador-t1/).
