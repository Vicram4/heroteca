The League of Assassins is an ancient order that trains warriors to eliminate whoever it considers a blight on the world. Its leader, [Ra's al Ghul](/dc/personajes/ras-al-ghul/), truly believes he is doing good, which makes it more unsettling than a mere band of criminals.

## Comics origin

It appears in *Strange Adventures* #215 (December 1968), though it does not get this name until *Detective Comics* #405 (November 1970). Later it becomes tied to Ra's al Ghul and his daughter [Talia](/dc/personajes/talia-al-ghul/).

## On screen

- **Nolan trilogy:** in [Batman Begins](/dc/batman-begins/) it is called the League of Shadows and trains Bruce Wayne. ||The mentor he knows as Ducard turns out to be the real Ra's.|| Its shadow returns in [The Dark Knight Rises](/dc/el-caballero-oscuro-la-leyenda-renace/).
- **[Arrow](/dc/arrow-t2/):** it is part of the past of Sara Lance, the future [White Canary](/dc/personajes/white-canary/). ||In the third season, Ra's wants Oliver Queen as his successor.||
- **[Gotham](/dc/gotham-t4/):** it returns in the fourth season with a Ra's interested in Bruce.
- **[Son of Batman](/dc/el-hijo-de-batman/):** [Damian](/dc/personajes/damian-wayne/) grows up among them before meeting his father.
