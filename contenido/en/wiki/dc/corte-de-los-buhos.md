The Court of Owls calls into question what Batman thinks he knows about his city: a network of powerful families has always controlled Gotham, without him even noticing. To act, it uses the Talons, killers trained from childhood.

## Comics origin

Scott Snyder and Greg Capullo create it for their Batman run, which began in 2011. One of their most unsettling ideas is that the group is largely a city legend, from a nursery rhyme nobody takes seriously.

- **[Batman vs. Robin](/dc/batman-contra-robin/):** this is its first screen appearance, in animation. A Talon tries to win [Damian](/dc/personajes/damian-wayne/) over to its side.
- **[Gotham](/dc/gotham-t3/):** in the third season, its members pull the strings. ||Bruce is kidnapped and a double takes his place in the city.||
