Apokolips is an entire planet in the service of [Darkseid](/dc/personajes/darkseid/): factories, fire and a population living in terror. It is the source of the parademons and the greatest threat the Justice League has ever faced.

## Comics origin

Jack Kirby creates it for the Fourth World, the line of connected titles he draws for DC between 1970 and 1973. Its opposite is New Genesis, home of the good gods.
