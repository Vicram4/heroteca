The Green Lantern Corps watches over the universe by sectors, with officers from thousands of different species. It is overseen by the Guardians, very ancient beings, and Earth falls within the area of [Green Lantern](/dc/personajes/linterna-verde/).

## Comics origin

John Broome and Gil Kane introduce it alongside Hal Jordan in *Showcase* #22 (1959). Jordan, a test pilot, receives the ring from a dying alien, Abin Sur, and with it becomes the first human in the Corps.

## On screen

[Green Lantern](/dc/linterna-verde/) (2011), with Ryan Reynolds, shows the planet Oa and his fellow officers in live action. ||A scene during the credits shows Sinestro putting on a yellow ring.|| The animated films make better use of it: [First Flight](/dc/linterna-verde-primer-vuelo/) tells Hal's first missions alongside [Sinestro](/dc/personajes/sinestro/), and [Emerald Knights](/dc/linterna-verde-caballeros-esmeralda/) links several stories about other members.
