The power ring is one of DC's most potent weapons and also one of the easiest to understand: it turns willpower into green energy in whatever shape its wearer thinks of. The limit lies with whoever wears it, which is why it picks people able to overcome fear.

## Comics origin

It arrives with Hal Jordan in *Showcase* #22 (1959), when a dying alien, Abin Sur, hands him his own. It must be recharged with a lantern and, for a long time, it doesn't work against yellow. The forties Green Lantern, Alan Scott, had a magic ring of a different origin.

## On screen

- **[Green Lantern](/dc/linterna-verde/) (2011):** digital effects show what Hal creates with it, and fear becomes the great enemy.
- **[First Flight](/dc/linterna-verde-primer-vuelo/) (2009):** ||Sinestro switches to yellow power, fed by fear.||
- **[Emerald Knights](/dc/linterna-verde-caballeros-esmeralda/) (2011):** several stories show how each wearer uses the ring.
