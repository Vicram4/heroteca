Metropolis is the optimistic face of DC's cities: skyscrapers, newspapers and a hero in plain view. It is where [Clark Kent](/dc/personajes/superman/) works at the Daily Planet and where [Lex Luthor](/dc/personajes/lex-luthor/) builds his empire.

## Comics origin

It is first named in *Action Comics* #16 (September 1939). Joe Shuster, Superman's co-creator, based its buildings on Toronto, the city where he was born.

## On screen

- **Richard Donner films:** in [Superman](/dc/superman/) (1978), New York plays Metropolis, with the Daily Planet right in the center. It continues in [Superman II](/dc/superman-ii-la-aventura-continua/).
- **[Superman Returns](/dc/superman-returns/):** returns to that style with a somewhat timeless city, shot largely in Australia.
- **Zack Snyder films:** ||Man of Steel ends with the city half destroyed by the battle with Zod,|| and [Batman v Superman](/dc/batman-vs-superman-el-amanecer-de-la-justicia/) opens precisely from the viewpoint of those on the ground.
- **[Smallville](/dc/smallville-t10/):** it gains weight as Clark moves closer to his future.
