Team Arrow is the big change that [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t1/) brings to the myth of [Green Arrow](/dc/personajes/flecha-verde/), a hero who in the comics usually works alone. Here Oliver Queen would not get far without someone watching his back or giving him orders over the earpiece.

## Origin

The group was created for the series, though it later influenced the comics. John Diggle, its first member, is a television character who later moved to the page; Felicity Smoak comes from the Firestorm comics, where she had a very different role.

## On screen

It starts with Oliver and Diggle in a club basement, and Felicity joins them soon after. Over the years they are joined by Roy Harper, Laurel Lance as [Black Canary](/dc/personajes/black-canary/), Thea and, in the fifth season, a new generation of recruits. ||In the final season, Oliver gives his life during the Crisis, and the others must carry on without him.||
