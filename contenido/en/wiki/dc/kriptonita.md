Kryptonite is the radioactive remnant of a destroyed planet, and the one thing that makes [Superman](/dc/personajes/superman/) vulnerable. Without it, tension would be hard to create with such a powerful character, which is why it comes up so often.

## Comics origin

Curiously, it isn't born in the comics: it first turns up on the radio, in the 1943 serial *The Adventures of Superman*. It reaches the page in *Superman* #61 (November 1949). Variants in other colors, with different effects, come later.

## On screen

- **[Superman](/dc/superman/) (1978):** [Lex Luthor](/dc/personajes/lex-luthor/) hangs it around Clark's neck and throws him into a pool.
- **[Superman III](/dc/superman-iii/):** a synthetic version turns the hero evil, and he fights himself in a junkyard.
- **[Superman Returns](/dc/superman-returns/):** ||Luthor builds an entire island out of it.||
- **[Smallville](/dc/smallville-t1/):** it falls along with Clark's ship and gives powers to many townspeople. The red kind also appears here, stripping away his inhibitions.
- **[Batman v Superman](/dc/batman-vs-superman-el-amanecer-de-la-justicia/):** [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/) uses it as a weapon. ||The spear tipped with it kills Doomsday and also Superman.||
- **[Superman/Batman: Public Enemies](/dc/superman-batman-enemigos-publicos/):** a huge meteor of this mineral heads for Earth.
