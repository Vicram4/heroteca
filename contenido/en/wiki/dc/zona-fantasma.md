The Phantom Zone is the Kryptonians' solution for their worst criminals: exile in another dimension, where time works differently and inmates exist like ghosts. Of course, as in all fictional prisons, someone gets out sooner or later.

## Comics origin

Robert Bernstein and George Papp create it in *Adventure Comics* #283 (April 1961). It gives Superman enemies from his own planet who survived its destruction.

## On screen

- **[Superman](/dc/superman/) (1978):** it appears as a flat mirror that traps [Zod](/dc/personajes/zod/) and his followers, then drifts off into space. ||In Superman II a nuclear explosion frees them.||
- **[Supergirl](/dc/supergirl/) (1984):** ||the villain Selena banishes Kara there, and she must escape.||
- **[Man of Steel](/dc/el-hombre-de-acero/):** ||Superman sends almost all of Zod's men back to the Zone, using the Kryptonian ships.||
- **Series:** [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t6/) and [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t1/) use it to bring Krypton's villains to Earth.
