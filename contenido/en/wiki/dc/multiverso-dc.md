DC's multiverse is a simple idea with enormous consequences: many Earths exist at once, each with its own versions of the heroes. That way, the same character can be very different without contradicting the others.

## Comics origin

It is born in *The Flash* #123 (September 1961), with the story "Flash of Two Worlds", in which Barry Allen meets the forties Flash, who lives on Earth-Two. The idea at a stroke explained all the old heroes DC had in its archive.

## On screen

- **[The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t2/):** the second season opens the way to Earth-2, and from then on the device is routine in its series. ||Crisis on Infinite Earths reshuffles those worlds.||
- **[The Flash](/dc/flash/) (2023):** ||Barry meets another Batman, played by Michael Keaton, after changing the past.||
- **[Crisis on Two Earths](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-crisis-en-dos-tierras/):** the League faces its evil doubles from another Earth.
- **Standalone stories:** [Gods and Monsters](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-dioses-y-monstruos/) or [Superman: Red Son](/dc/superman-hijo-rojo/) imagine different worlds with no need to cross them.
