The Batcave is where Bruce Wayne stops being a millionaire and becomes [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/). It is also where [Alfred](/dc/personajes/alfred/) reminds him he has a life beyond the cape. Each version changes it, and its look says a lot about each era's Batman.

## Origin

It is born outside the comics: it appears in the 1943 film serial *Batman*, in the chapter "The Electrical Brain". Bob Kane, who visited the set, soon carried it over to the newspaper strip as "The Bat Cave".

## On screen

- **[Batman](/dc/batman/) (1966):** everything is labeled, and the heroes slide down poles to get there.
- **[Batman](/dc/batman-1989/) (1989):** a huge, dark, more serious place.
- **[Batman Begins](/dc/batman-begins/):** Bruce discovers it as a boy by falling into a well, and his symbol is born from that fear. During [The Dark Knight](/dc/el-caballero-oscuro/) it moves to a bunker under the city, because the manor is under construction. ||The Dark Knight Rises ends with an heir finding the cave.||
- **[Batman v Superman](/dc/batman-vs-superman-el-amanecer-de-la-justicia/):** a glass base on the shore of a lake.
- **[The Batman](/dc/the-batman/):** set up in an abandoned subway station.
