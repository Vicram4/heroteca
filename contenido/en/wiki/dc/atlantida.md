Atlantis is the sunken kingdom to which [Aquaman](/dc/personajes/aquaman/) half belongs. Son of a queen and a human, Arthur Curry moves between two worlds that don't understand each other, and that tension lies behind nearly all his stories.

## Comics origin

DC draws on the old legend and changes details from era to era. For years, Aquaman rules the kingdom, loses it and wins it back. In one stretch of the comics he even gives up the throne, deeply affected by what he sees in the seas.

## On screen

- **[Aquaman](/dc/aquaman/) (2018):** an undersea city full of light and creatures, where Arthur fights his brother Orm for the throne with help from [Mera](/dc/personajes/mera/).
- **[Aquaman and the Lost Kingdom](/dc/aquaman-y-el-reino-perdido/) (2023):** ||Arthur and Orm have to team up against an ancient threat from the lost kingdom of Necrus.||
- **[Throne of Atlantis](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-el-trono-de-atlantis/) (2015):** the animated version takes the conflict straight to the surface.
- **[Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/) (2017):** the Atlanteans guard one of the [Mother Boxes](/dc/wiki/cajas-madre/).
