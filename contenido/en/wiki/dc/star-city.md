Star City is to [Green Arrow](/dc/personajes/flecha-verde/) what Gotham is to Batman: a city with huge gaps between rich and poor, which gives his fight its meaning. Oliver Queen is heir to one of its largest fortunes.

## Comics origin

It is the character's city in DC's comics, on the American coast, and it has shifted with each writer and era.

## On screen

In [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t1/) it is first called Starling City, and it features a run-down district, the Glades. ||At the end of the first season, an attack devastates part of that district.|| The name changes to Star City in the [fourth season](/dc/arrow-t4/). The [seventh season](/dc/arrow-t7/) also shows its future, as a divided and very different city.
