Justice League Dark deals with the supernatural: demons, curses and everything Superman doesn't know how to handle. Its members are disreputable people who work together grudgingly, starting with [Constantine](/dc/personajes/constantine/).

## Comics origin

It debuts in *Justice League Dark* #1 (September 2011), with characters from DC's darkest corners, such as Constantine, Zatanna and Deadman.

## On screen

It only appears in animation. [Justice League Dark](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-oscura/) (2017) begins with [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/) turning to Constantine over some unexplained events, and [Swamp Thing](/dc/personajes/swamp-thing/) joins in too. [Apokolips War](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia-oscura-la-guerra-apokolips/) (2020) is much harsher: it closes a whole line of animated films. ||Darkseid has won, and the survivors make one last desperate attempt.||
