The Speed Force is the explanation for why [The Flash](/dc/personajes/flash/) runs so fast: an energy present throughout the universe, to which a few connect. It is almost religious for speedsters, but also dangerous, because running too fast can make them get lost in it.

## Comics origin

Mark Waid introduces it in the nineties, during his run with Wally West. Before, it was just about speed; since then, the power has a source, rules and, above all, a risk.

## On screen

- **[The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/):** it gradually becomes a central element of the series, almost with a will of its own. ||At the end of the third season, Barry enters it as a penance, and his friends must get him out in the next one.||
- **[The Flash](/dc/flash/) (2023):** Barry discovers that running very fast lets him view the past as in a movie theater, and makes the mistake of touching it.
