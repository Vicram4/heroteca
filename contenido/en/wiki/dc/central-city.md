Central City is home to [The Flash](/dc/personajes/flash/): an ordinary mid-sized American city, far from Gotham's drama and Metropolis's shine. That lets the extraordinary stand out all the more.

## Comics origin

It is Barry Allen's city, and the comics themselves have never settled its location: Ohio, Illinois, Missouri and Florida have all been named at one time or another. Next door is Keystone City, the home of another Flash.

## On screen

- **[The Flash](/dc/the-flash/) (1990):** a city with a retro, somber look, strongly influenced by Tim Burton's Batman.
- **[The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/) (2014):** here it is almost one more character. The explosion of the [S.T.A.R. Labs](/dc/wiki/star-labs/) accelerator fills the city with metahumans, such as [Captain Cold](/dc/personajes/capitan-frio/) and other villains who stick around for years.
- **[The Flash](/dc/flash/) (2023):** it barely appears here, as the story soon moves to other realities.
