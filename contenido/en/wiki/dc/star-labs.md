S.T.A.R. Labs is the DC universe's great network of laboratories, the place to go when something impossible needs studying. The initials stand for Scientific and Technological Advanced Research Laboratories.

## Comics origin

It emerges in the Superman comics of the seventies and soon spreads to several cities. Its role is usually secondary: it is where heroes bring what they can't explain.

## On screen

- **[The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/):** [Harrison Wells](/dc/personajes/harrison-wells/)'s lab, through the explosion of its accelerator, causes the accident that creates the Flash and other metahumans. [Cisco](/dc/personajes/cisco-ramon/) and [Caitlin](/dc/personajes/caitlin-snow/) work there, and it becomes their base. ||Wells is really Eobard Thawne, the enemy who killed Barry's mother.||
- **[Chronicles of Cisco](/dc/the-flash-chronicles-of-cisco-t1/):** a short web series that takes place within its walls.
- **[Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/):** Silas Stone, the father of [Cyborg](/dc/personajes/cyborg/), works there.
