The Teen Titans are where the great heroes' sidekicks stop being sidekicks. That is why their stories talk so much about growing up, the family you choose and stepping out of your mentor's shadow.

## Comics origin

Bob Haney and Bruno Premiani introduce them in *The Brave and the Bold* #54 (1964) with Robin, Kid Flash and Aqualad, before the group even had a name. Their best-remembered run begins in 1980 with Marv Wolfman and George Pérez, who add [Cyborg](/dc/personajes/cyborg/), [Raven](/dc/personajes/raven/) and [Starfire](/dc/personajes/starfire/).

## On screen

### Live-action series

[Titans](/dc/titans-t1/) offers an adult, violent version, with Dick Grayson trying to leave Batman behind. ||Its second season revolves around what the team once did with Deathstroke and his son.||

### Animated films

[Justice League vs. Teen Titans](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-contra-los-jovenes-titanes/) centers the group on [Damian Wayne](/dc/personajes/damian-wayne/) and Raven. [The Judas Contract](/dc/jovenes-titanes-el-contrato-de-judas/) adapts one of their most famous comics, with [Deathstroke](/dc/personajes/deathstroke/) as the enemy. ||The new member, Terra, is spying on them for him.||
