Themyscira is an island inhabited only by women warriors, hidden from the rest of the world for centuries. There, [Diana](/dc/personajes/wonder-woman/) grows up between peace and training, which is why men's wars shock her so much when she leaves.

## Comics origin

William Moulton Marston and Harry G. Peter introduce it in *All Star Comics* #8 (1941) under the name Paradise Island. It has been called Themyscira since the 1987 relaunch, taking the name of the Amazons' city in Greek mythology.

## On screen

- **[The New Original Wonder Woman](/dc/the-new-original-wonder-woman/) (1975):** it still appears under the name Paradise Island.
- **[Wonder Woman](/dc/wonder-woman-2017/) (2017):** its great live-action debut, followed by a spectacular prologue in [Wonder Woman 1984](/dc/wonder-woman-1984/).
- **[Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/) (2017):** [Steppenwolf](/dc/personajes/steppenwolf/) attacks the island to steal the [Mother Box](/dc/wiki/cajas-madre/) kept there.
- **Animation:** Diana's story in [Wonder Woman](/dc/wonder-woman-la-mujer-maravilla/) and in [Bloodlines](/dc/wonder-woman-linaje/) also starts from the island.
