Nth metal is what allows the Hawk family to fly. It is a material with almost magical properties that cancels gravity, and it gives their weapons unusual force.

## Comics origin

It is born with Hawkman in the forties. Carter Hall discovers a "ninth metal" that lets him fly. Over time it changes its name and is given an origin on the planet Thanagar, the home of another version of the character, Katar Hol.

## On screen

- **[DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t1/):** it carries real weight in the first season, with [Hawkgirl](/dc/personajes/hawkgirl/) as a lead. ||A dagger of this metal is the only thing that can finish off Vandal Savage.||
- **[Black Adam](/dc/black-adam/):** Hawkman's wings and mace are made of this metal.
