The Birds of Prey are a team made up almost always of women alone, working the street-level side of the DC universe. They lack the Justice League's powers, but they have contacts, information and very little patience.

## Comics origin

They are born in the nineties in scripts by Chuck Dixon, around the alliance between [Black Canary](/dc/personajes/black-canary/) and Oracle, the name Barbara Gordon takes after she stops being [Batgirl](/dc/personajes/batgirl/). Gail Simone later writes one of their longest runs.

## On screen

[Birds of Prey (and the Fantabulous Emancipation of One Harley Quinn)](/dc/aves-de-presa-y-la-fantabulosa-emancipacion-de-harley-quinn/) (2020) shifts the focus: the narrator is [Harley Quinn](/dc/personajes/harley-quinn/), newly split from the Joker, and the team takes shape over the story itself with Black Canary, Huntress, Renee Montoya and young Cassandra Cain. The enemy is Black Mask. Oracle does not appear.
