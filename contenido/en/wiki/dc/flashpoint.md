Flashpoint poses every time traveler's dilemma: what can you fix without ruining everything else? What starts with an understandable decision ends with a whole world changed, and [The Flash](/dc/personajes/flash/) as the only one remembering how it was.

## Comics origin

It is a big 2011 crossover, with many parallel titles, leading into the general relaunch of DC's comics known as The New 52. In its alternate world, Bruce's father wears the cowl, while [Wonder Woman](/dc/personajes/wonder-woman/) and [Aquaman](/dc/personajes/aquaman/) have laid waste to Western Europe with their fighting.

## On screen

- **[The Flashpoint Paradox](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-la-paradoja-del-tiempo/) (2013):** the most faithful adaptation, animated and for adults. ||Barry saved his mother, and that is the origin of the whole disaster.||
- **[The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t3/):** the third season opens with the same idea, but resolves it quickly and spends the rest of the season paying the price.
- **[The Flash](/dc/flash/) (2023):** uses the same premise. ||Barry prevents his mother's death, and this creates a reality without Superman or the League.||
