Smallville explains why Superman is good: a farm, adoptive parents and a small town. It is the rural counterpoint to [Metropolis](/dc/wiki/metropolis/), the place Clark returns to when he needs to remember who he is.

## Comics origin

The name first appears in *Superboy* #2 (May 1949), and for years it isn't even said which state it is in. It was the 1978 film that placed it in Kansas, and that idea has stuck.

## On screen

- **[Smallville](/dc/smallville-t1/):** ten seasons about Clark's youth, in a town marked by the meteor shower his ship arrived with, and by [Lex Luthor](/dc/personajes/lex-luthor/) as a friend.
- **[Superman](/dc/superman/) (1978):** the farm scenes, with their golden light, shape every later version.
- **[Superman III](/dc/superman-iii/):** Clark returns to his town for a class reunion.
- **[Man of Steel](/dc/el-hombre-de-acero/):** ||Jonathan Kent dies in a tornado, and the town suffers a violent battle with Zod's men.||
