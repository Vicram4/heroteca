Krypton is a highly advanced civilization that refused to listen to the one warning of its end. Almost everything that defines Superman comes from that loss: his father [Jor-El](/dc/personajes/jor-el/), the ship and the loneliness of being the last of his people.

## Comics origin

Jerry Siegel and Joe Shuster mention it in *Action Comics* #1 (June 1938) and show it in *Superman* #1 (June 1939). Its name comes from the chemical element.

## On screen

- **[Superman](/dc/superman/) (1978):** a white world of crystal, with Marlon Brando as Jor-El.
- **[Man of Steel](/dc/el-hombre-de-acero/):** a darker, more technological image, with Russell Crowe as Jor-El and [Zod](/dc/personajes/zod/) staging a coup.
- **[Krypton](/dc/krypton-t1/):** the series takes place two generations earlier, with Seg-El, Superman's grandfather. ||A warrior arriving from the future turns out to be Zod himself, Seg-El's son.||
- **[Supergirl](/dc/supergirl/) (1984):** the survivors of Argo City are the film's starting point. The [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t1/) series does it through the memories of Kara, who does remember the planet.
