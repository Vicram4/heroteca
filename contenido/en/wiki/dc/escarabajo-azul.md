The scarab looks like an ancient relic, but it is an alien weapon with its own consciousness. It attaches to the spine of [Jaime Reyes](/dc/personajes/blue-beetle/) and gives him armor, in exchange for a far from easy cohabitation.

## Comics origin

Blue Beetle is a name with a long history: Dan Garret has carried it since the late thirties, and later Ted Kord takes it up. The scarab was at first only a magic amulet for Garret. Years later, when Jaime Reyes arrives, a young man from El Paso, its true origin is revealed, as technology from an alien race, the Reach.

## On screen

[Blue Beetle](/dc/blue-beetle/) (2023) calls it Khaji-Da and presents it as an object of desire for Victoria Kord's company. It leads Jaime toward both comedy and family drama, as part of the fun is that the scarab offers opinions and speaks inside his head.
