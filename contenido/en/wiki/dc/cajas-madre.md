Mother Boxes are small devices almost no one understands, and they solve nearly impossible problems. Those who use them treat them with affection, as if they were alive, and in part they are.

## Comics origin

Jack Kirby invents them for the Fourth World, in the early seventies. Only someone born on New Genesis or [Apokolips](/dc/wiki/apokolips/) can build one, and many fail, because the maker's character affects the result.

## On screen

In [Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/) and [Zack Snyder's version](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-de-zack-snyder/) there are three, hidden millennia ago by the Amazons, the Atlanteans and the humans. [Steppenwolf](/dc/personajes/steppenwolf/) needs them to finish off Earth. [Cyborg](/dc/personajes/cyborg/) owes his body to the third. ||The heroes use its power to bring Superman back to life.||

In the animated [Justice League: War](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-guerra/), Victor Stone also becomes Cyborg thanks to such technology.
