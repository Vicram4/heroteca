The Lasso of Truth is [Wonder Woman](/dc/personajes/wonder-woman/)'s most unusual weapon, because it is less about causing harm than about disarming: whoever is tied up ends up telling the truth. It suits a character who would rather persuade than destroy.

## Comics origin

It is the creation of William Moulton Marston, the psychologist who invented Wonder Woman and also worked on the lie detector. At first it served to make people obey; only later did it become an instrument of truth. Later versions say Hephaestus forged it.

## On screen

- **[The New Original Wonder Woman](/dc/the-new-original-wonder-woman/):** the seventies series uses it to draw out confessions, with its distinctive glow.
- **[Wonder Woman](/dc/wonder-woman-2017/) (2017):** Steve Trevor is one of the first to feel its effect, when the Amazons question him.
- **[Wonder Woman 1984](/dc/wonder-woman-1984/):** ||Diana hooks it onto lightning to move through the air, and employs it in her final confrontation with Max Lord.||
- **[Professor Marston and the Wonder Women](/dc/el-profesor-marston-y-wonder-women/):** this film tells who invented it, and links the idea to his experiments.
