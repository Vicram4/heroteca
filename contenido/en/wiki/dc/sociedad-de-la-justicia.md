The Justice Society has the air of an older generation: heroes from the forties passing their legacy on to those who come after. That is why on screen it usually appears as something from the past that someone has to pick up.

## Comics origin

Gardner Fox and Sheldon Mayer create it in *All Star Comics* #3 (Winter 1940-1941), and it is the first superhero team in comics history. The [Justice League](/dc/wiki/liga-de-la-justicia/) would come almost twenty years later.

## On screen

- **[Stargirl](/dc/stargirl-t1/):** Courtney Whitmore finds a fallen hero's staff and assembles a new generation. ||The original Society died at the hands of the Injustice Society, which is still hiding in Courtney's town.||
- **[Black Adam](/dc/black-adam/):** Hawkman, Doctor Fate, Atom Smasher and Cyclone face [Black Adam](/dc/personajes/black-adam/). ||Doctor Fate dies sacrificing himself for his teammates.||
- **[DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t2/):** the second season visits the group in the forties, with that era's [Vixen](/dc/personajes/vixen/) among its members.
- **[Smallville](/dc/smallville-t9/):** a two-part episode in the ninth season brings the group into Clark's life.
