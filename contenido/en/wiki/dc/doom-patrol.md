The Doom Patrol is the opposite of the admired superhero: people scarred by an accident, whom the world would rather not look at. Their stories move between personal drama and some of the most absurd things ever seen in comics.

## Comics origin

Arnold Drake, Bob Haney and Bruno Premiani create it in *My Greatest Adventure* #80 (June 1963), with the Chief, Robotman, Elasti-Girl and Negative Man. Its creator suspected that Marvel had drawn on it for the X-Men, who came out a few months later.

## On screen

The group first appears in an episode of [Titans](/dc/titans-t1/), where [Beast Boy](/dc/personajes/chico-bestia/) has ties to them. Then comes its own series, [Doom Patrol](/dc/doom-patrol-t1/), which adds [Cyborg](/dc/personajes/cyborg/) and Crazy Jane and embraces the strangest humor. Brendan Fraser voices Robotman and Timothy Dalton is the Chief. ||The team learns that the Chief had a hand in the accidents that gave them their powers.||
