The Boys starts from an uncomfortable question: what if superheroes were famous and powerful, but also spoiled, with no real oversight? [Billy Butcher](/dc/personajes/billy-butcher/)'s group is the only thing standing in their way, with methods as dubious as their enemies'.

## Comics origin

Garth Ennis and Darick Robertson publish the story between 2006 and 2012, in 72 issues. The first volume came out from WildStorm, a DC imprint, which canceled it after six issues; Dynamite published the rest. It sits under DC here because that is how the collection is organized.

## On screen

[The Boys](/dc/the-boys-t1/) (2019), the Amazon series, begins when Hughie loses his girlfriend to a superhero moving too fast, and Butcher recruits him. With them are Mother's Milk, Frenchie and Kimiko. ||Butcher is hunting Homelander because he believes he raped his wife, Becca, whom he thinks is dead; in fact, she is alive and secretly raising their son.||
