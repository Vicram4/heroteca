National City is a bright, modern coastal city, made to measure for the friendly tone of [Supergirl](/dc/personajes/supergirl/). It is what Metropolis is to Superman, and it gives the heroine a place of her own, out of her cousin's shadow.

## Origin

Its fame comes from television. In the comics, Supergirl has mostly moved around other places, such as the town of Midvale, which the series also keeps as her childhood home.

## On screen

[Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t1/) fills it with regular settings: CatCo, Cat Grant's media company where Kara works, and the facilities of the [D.E.O.](/dc/wiki/deo/) The first season kicks off when Kara reveals herself to the city by saving a plane her sister is on.
