Crisis on Infinite Earths is the moment to put the house in order: an event in which the fate of all the Earths is decided at once. At DC, the word "Crisis" has been tied forever to its biggest sagas.

## Comics origin

Marv Wolfman and George Pérez publish it in twelve issues from April 1985 to March 1986. Wolfman wanted to simplify a multiverse that he felt confused readers, and in the end only one Earth remains. ||Along the way Supergirl and Barry Allen die, unthinkable at the time.||

## On screen

The television series adapt it in a big 2019 and 2020 crossover, split among [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t5/), [Batwoman](/dc/batwoman-t1/), [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t6/), [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t8/) and [DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t5/). [Black Lightning](/dc/black-lightning-t3/) also joins with an episode of its own. It is well known for its surprise appearances by characters from other eras. ||Oliver Queen sacrifices himself, and in the end the shows' worlds are united into a single Earth-Prime.||
