The Legends are the people nobody would pick to save the world, and that is exactly why the show works. What begins as a serious adventure ends up as the most lighthearted and absurd corner of DC's television series.

## Origin

The team was invented for television, bringing together characters first seen in [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t1/) and [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/). Rip Hunter does come from the comics, where he has been a time traveler since the late fifties.

## On screen

[DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t1/) starts when Rip Hunter recruits [White Canary](/dc/personajes/white-canary/), [The Atom](/dc/personajes/the-atom/), [Firestorm](/dc/personajes/firestorm/), [Hawkgirl](/dc/personajes/hawkgirl/) and [Captain Cold](/dc/personajes/capitan-frio/) to stop Vandal Savage aboard the [Waverider](/dc/wiki/waverider/). ||Rip is not out to save the world but to avenge his family, and Snart sacrifices himself near the end of the season.||

Later they are joined by a forties [Vixen](/dc/personajes/vixen/) and [Constantine](/dc/personajes/constantine/), while Sara Lance becomes the captain.
