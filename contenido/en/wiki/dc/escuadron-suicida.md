The Suicide Squad starts from a simple idea: if a mission is so dangerous that nobody would return, let expendable criminals do it. [Amanda Waller](/dc/personajes/amanda-waller/) controls them remotely, and anyone who disobeys can die on the spot.

## Comics origin

The first group with that name appears in *The Brave and the Bold* #25 (September 1959), and was a unit of adventurers with no villains. The current version, with supervillains under government orders, arrives with John Ostrander in *Legends* #3 (January 1987).

## On screen

### Live-action films

[Suicide Squad](/dc/escuadron-suicida/) (2016), by David Ayer, teams up [Harley Quinn](/dc/personajes/harley-quinn/), [Deadshot](/dc/personajes/deadshot/) and [Killer Croc](/dc/personajes/killer-croc/). [The Suicide Squad](/dc/el-escuadron-suicida/) (2021), by James Gunn, is far more irreverent and bloody. ||It opens with a massacre on the beach that wipes out almost an entire team.|| The series [Peacemaker](/dc/el-pacificador-t1/) grows out of it.

### Other versions

[Batman: Assault on Arkham](/dc/batman-el-asalto-de-arkham/) and [Hell to Pay](/dc/escuadron-suicida-consecuencias-infernales/) are animated films for adults. [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t2/) also gives it an episode in its second season.
