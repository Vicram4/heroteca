The Lazarus Pit explains how [Ra's al Ghul](/dc/personajes/ras-al-ghul/) is centuries old: waters that heal any wound and even bring the dead back to life. The name refers to the biblical Lazarus, raised by Jesus, and its price is a passing madness that doesn't always go away.

## Comics origin

It arrives with Ra's al Ghul, created by Julius Schwartz, Dennis O'Neil and Neal Adams in *Batman* #232 (June 1971). According to the character's story, he discovered it long ago and has been using it ever since.

## On screen

- **[Batman: Under the Red Hood](/dc/batman-capucha-roja/):** ||it is what brings back Jason Todd, the former Robin murdered by the Joker.||
- **[Arrow](/dc/arrow-t3/):** ||in the third season it saves Thea Queen, who is left with a thirst for blood. In the fourth season they bring back Sara Lance, who goes on to lead Legends of Tomorrow.||
- **[Gotham](/dc/gotham-t4/):** it is part of the story of the show's Ra's, who here is very long-lived.
