The D.E.O. is a government agency that deals with those who are not entirely human. Depending on the story it appears as a threat to heroes or as their main support, and on television it is the latter.

## Comics origin

Dan Curtis Johnson and J. H. Williams III introduce it in *Batman* #550 (1998). Over time it becomes tied above all to [Batwoman](/dc/personajes/batwoman/), whom it pressures into working for it.

## On screen

The series [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t1/) makes it a regular setting: Alex, Kara's sister, works at its facilities, and the agency goes from distrusting [Supergirl](/dc/personajes/supergirl/) to working with her from early in the show. ||Its chief, Hank Henshaw, is really J'onn J'onzz, the Martian Manhunter, in disguise.||
