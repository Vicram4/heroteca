Stephen Strange is a brilliant, very self-satisfied surgeon whose world collapses when an accident ruins his hands. He looks for a cure until he finds a mystic teacher, and he ends up as Sorcerer Supreme, charged with defending Earth from threats almost nobody ever sees. He keeps the ego but learns to use it for more than himself.

## Comics origin

Steve Ditko draws him and Stan Lee writes the words in *Strange Tales* #110, from July 1963. His origin does not arrive until issue 115, and Ditko's impossible dimensions draw in readers, college students of the period among them, who saw something psychedelic in the art.

## On screen

### Live action

Peter Hooten plays him in [Dr. Strange](/marvel/dr-strange/), a 1978 TV film meant as the pilot for a series that never happened.

### Cinematic Universe

Benedict Cumberbatch embodies him in [Doctor Strange](/marvel/doctor-strange/), with visual magic that folds entire cities. He later helps [Thor](/marvel/personajes/thor/) and [Spider-Man](/marvel/personajes/spider-man/) and leads [Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness](/marvel/doctor-strange-en-el-multiverso-de-la-locura/). ||In Avengers: Infinity War he hands the Time Stone to Thanos because it is the only route to victory he has seen among millions of possible futures.||

### Animation

He stars in the 2007 film [Doctor Strange](/marvel/doctor-strange-el-hechicero-supremo/), an animated take on his origin. [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/) gives him one of its darkest episodes, with Cumberbatch's voice.
