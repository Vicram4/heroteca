Logan is a Canadian mutant with retractable claws, an adamantium skeleton and the ability to heal from almost any wound. He has lived far longer than he remembers, since much of his past has been erased, and his foul temper hides someone who always ends up protecting the youngest. He is the loner of a team he cannot leave.

## Comics origin

He first peeks in on the last pages of *The Incredible Hulk* #180, in 1974, created by Len Wein and John Romita Sr. and drawn by Herb Trimpe. The next year he joins the X-Men in *Giant-Size X-Men* #1, and his popularity takes off with the 1982 miniseries by Chris Claremont and Frank Miller, set in Japan.

## On screen

### Live action

Hugh Jackman plays him for almost twenty-five years, starting with [X-Men](/marvel/x-men/) in 2000, in one of the longest runs in superhero cinema. He has his own films: [X-Men Origins: Wolverine](/marvel/x-men-origenes-lobezno/), [The Wolverine](/marvel/lobezno-inmortal/) and [Logan](/marvel/logan/), a violent twilight western with an aged Logan. ||Logan dies at the end of that film, saving his daughter.|| [Deadpool & Wolverine](/marvel/deadpool-y-wolverine/) brings Jackman back as a variant from another universe and, at last, in the yellow suit from the comics.

### Animation

Cal Dodd gives him his best-remembered voice in [X-Men](/marvel/x-men-la-serie-animada/), the animated series, and returns in [X-Men '97](/marvel/x-men-97/). He also leads [Wolverine and the X-Men](/marvel/lobezno-y-los-x-men/) and stars in the 2011 anime [Wolverine](/marvel/wolverine/).
