Miles Morales is a Brooklyn teenager, the son of a Black father and a Puerto Rican mother, who gets bitten by a spider just like Peter Parker. He has the same school and family troubles, plus an electric sting and the ability to turn invisible. His story is that of someone unsure he can live up to so big a name.

## Comics origin

Brian Michael Bendis and Sara Pichelli create him for *Ultimate Fallout* #4, from August 2011, in the Ultimate line, after the death of that universe's [Peter Parker](/marvel/personajes/spider-man/). The news drew wide attention and the character eventually moved into Marvel's main universe.

## On screen

### Animation

Shameik Moore voices him in [Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse](/marvel/spider-man-un-nuevo-universo/), from 2018, which made him famous worldwide and won the Oscar for best animated feature, and in [Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse](/marvel/spider-man-cruzando-el-multiverso/). Before that, Nadji Jeter voiced him in the 2017 [Spider-Man](/marvel/marvel-spider-man/) series. Young children can find him in [Spidey and His Amazing Friends](/marvel/spidey-y-su-superequipo/).

### Live action

Moore can be heard in a preview of *Into the Spider-Verse* included after the credits of [Venom](/marvel/venom/).
