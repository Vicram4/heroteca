Peter Quill is abducted as a boy by a gang of space pirates on the very day his mother dies. As an adult he is a joking thief who calls himself Star-Lord even though nobody knows who that is, and who plays his mother's old tapes. Beneath the façade is someone who never got over that loss.

## Comics origin

Steve Englehart and Steve Gan create him for the black-and-white magazine *Marvel Preview* #4, from January 1976, with a very different tone. The character is reinvented in 2008, when he founds the spacefaring team of Guardians the films draw on.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Chris Pratt plays him in [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2014/), with his joking mix of swagger and clumsiness. ||In Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 he finds out that his father, Ego, is a living planet, and that Ego killed his mother.|| [Avengers: Infinity War](/marvel/vengadores-infinity-war/) and [Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-volumen-3/) test him with the loss of [Gamora](/marvel/personajes/gamora/).

### Animation

Will Friedle voices him in [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2015/). [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/) plays with the idea of a different Peter Quill in a story where the pirates abduct another boy.
