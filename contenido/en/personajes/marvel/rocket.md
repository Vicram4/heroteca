Rocket looks like a joke, a talking raccoon who handles enormous guns, and even his teammates sometimes treat him as one. He answers with insults and impossible plans. Underneath is an animal who was hurt in a lab and trusts very few, above all [Groot](/marvel/personajes/groot/).

## Comics origin

Bill Mantlo and Keith Giffen create him for *Marvel Preview* #7, in 1976, calling him Rocky, a name taken from a Beatles track. He spends years almost forgotten until, in 2008, he becomes a member of the modern Guardians of the Galaxy.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Bradley Cooper has voiced him since [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2014/), where he is one of the film's great successes. He joins the fight against Thanos in [Avengers: Infinity War](/marvel/vengadores-infinity-war/), forming an unexpected comic duo with Thor. [Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-volumen-3/) finally gives him the spotlight. ||It reveals his origin as an experiment of the High Evolutionary, alongside the animals who were his first family.||

### Animation

Trevor Devall voices him in [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2015/) and other productions, and he stars with Groot in the shorts [Rocket & Groot](/marvel/rocket-y-groot/).
