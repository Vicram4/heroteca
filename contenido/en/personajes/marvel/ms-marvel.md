Kamala Khan is a New Jersey girl who writes fan fiction about her favorite heroes and suddenly finds herself with powers. Her Pakistani family, her faith and her high school matter as much as the fights, and her fan enthusiasm rids the genre of its habit of taking itself so seriously. She admires [Carol Danvers](/marvel/personajes/capitana-marvel/) above all, which is where her name comes from.

## Comics origin

She comes from 2013 work by writer G. Willow Wilson, artists Jamie McKelvie and Adrian Alphona, and editors Stephen Wacker and Sana Amanat. After a cameo in *Captain Marvel* #14, she headlines *Ms. Marvel* from 2014, the first Marvel title led by a Muslim protagonist. It was very warmly received.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Iman Vellani, a true fan herself, plays her in [Ms. Marvel](/marvel/ms-marvel-t1/), a brightly colored series with animation layered over the image, where her powers come from an inherited bangle. She joins Carol and [Monica Rambeau](/marvel/personajes/monica-rambeau/) in [The Marvels](/marvel/the-marvels/). ||The end of her series suggests she carries a mutant gene, a first for this continuity.||

### Animation

Kathreen Khavari voices her in [Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores-unidos/) and [Marvel Rising: Secret Warriors](/marvel/marvel-rising-secret-warriors/), and Sandra Saad in [Spidey and His Amazing Friends](/marvel/spidey-y-su-superequipo/).
