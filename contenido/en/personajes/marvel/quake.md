Quake can generate vibrations strong enough to bring down a building, but her real story is that of someone who has never had a family and gradually finds one. On screen she is above all the girl who started out breaking into S.H.I.E.L.D.'s networks and ends up as one of its finest agents.

## Comics origin

Brian Michael Bendis and Gabriele Dell'Otto introduce her in *Secret War* #2, from July 2004, as a young agent recruited by [Nick Fury](/marvel/personajes/nick-fury/). She is the daughter of the villain Mister Hyde, a detail the comic uses to raise doubts about how far she can be trusted. In time she comes to run S.H.I.E.L.D.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Chloe Bennet plays her over the seven seasons of [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-t1/), where she first goes by Skye. She is the emotional core of the series and the character who changes most over its run. ||The second season reveals that she is an Inhuman named Daisy Johnson, daughter of Jiaying and Cal, and turns her into the Quake of the comics.||

### Animation

Bennet also voices her in [Marvel Rising: Secret Warriors](/marvel/marvel-rising-secret-warriors/). Before that, Lacey Chabert played her in [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/).
