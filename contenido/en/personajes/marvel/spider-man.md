Peter Parker is a smart, unlucky Queens student who gains a spider's powers from a bite. One bad decision costs the life of someone he loves, and from then on he carries the idea that not helping has consequences too. His charm lies in the fact that superpowers do not pay the rent: he runs late, he is short of money and he cracks jokes in a fight to hide his fear.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Steve Ditko introduce him in *Amazing Fantasy* #15, in August 1962, in a magazine on the verge of cancellation. A teenager as the hero rather than the sidekick was unusual then. His success led the following year to *The Amazing Spider-Man*, where Ditko shaped much of his world and his villains.

## On screen

### Live action

His first steps are the sketches of [Spidey Super Stories](/marvel/spidey-super-stories/) and the 1977 TV film [Spider-Man](/marvel/spider-man-1977/), with Nicholas Hammond, which continued in [the series](/marvel/spiderman/).

Tobey Maguire leads Sam Raimi's trilogy, which begins with [Spider-Man](/marvel/spider-man-2002/) and hits its most celebrated moment with [Spider-Man 2](/marvel/spider-man-2/). Andrew Garfield brings a more restless energy to [The Amazing Spider-Man](/marvel/the-amazing-spider-man/) and its sequel.

### Cinematic Universe

Tom Holland debuts in [Captain America: Civil War](/marvel/capitan-america-civil-war/) and has his first solo adventures in [Spider-Man: Homecoming](/marvel/spider-man-homecoming/), a much younger Peter with Tony Stark looking out for him. ||Spider-Man: No Way Home brings him together with the Maguire and Garfield versions and ends with a spell that erases Peter Parker from everyone's memory.|| His story continues in [Spider-Man: Brand New Day](/marvel/spider-man-brand-new-day/).

### Animation

Few characters have had so many series: the [1967](/marvel/spider-man-1967/) one, [Spider-Man and His Amazing Friends](/marvel/spider-man-y-sus-sorprendentes-amigos/), [The Spectacular Spider-Man](/marvel/el-espectacular-spider-man/), much loved for its writing, and [Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man](/marvel/tu-amigo-y-vecino-spider-man/), among others. In [Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse](/marvel/spider-man-un-nuevo-universo/) he shares the lead with [Miles Morales](/marvel/personajes/miles-morales/) and several Peters from other worlds.
