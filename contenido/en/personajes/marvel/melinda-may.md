Melinda May speaks little, fights better than almost anyone and is the one truly in charge when things turn ugly. In [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-t1/) she is the right hand and close friend of [Phil Coulson](/marvel/personajes/phil-coulson/). Her colleagues call her The Cavalry, a nickname she hates.

## Comics origin

She was born on television: Joss Whedon, Jed Whedon and Maurissa Tancharoen create her for the 2013 pilot. Mark Waid and Carlos Pacheco bring her into the comics in *S.H.I.E.L.D.*, in 2015.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Ming-Na Wen plays her across the seven seasons and in [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.: Slingshot](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-slingshot/). Almost all the mystery of the first season revolves around her nickname. ||The second season tells what happened in Bahrain, where she had to kill a girl who turned out to be the one controlling the others, which is why she shut down. In the final season she bows out with a very typical decision: staying on to teach the next generation.||
