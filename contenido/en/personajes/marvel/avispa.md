This entry gathers the Hope van Dyne of the films and the animated Wasps, although many of those are actually her mother, [Janet van Dyne](/marvel/personajes/janet-van-dyne/). Hope is a brilliant, tough executive who knows how to use the suit better than anyone. Her stories spring from the wound of losing her mother as a child and the tense relationship with her father, [Hank Pym](/marvel/personajes/hank-pym/).

## Comics origin

The comics' original Wasp is Janet van Dyne, from 1963. Hope is loosely based on Hope Pym, Hank Pym's daughter in an alternate future. That makes her more of a film creation than a direct adaptation.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Evangeline Lilly plays her in [Ant-Man](/marvel/ant-man-2015/), where she trains Scott Lang and makes it plain she would be better prepared for the mission. She finally dons the suit in [Ant-Man and the Wasp](/marvel/ant-man-y-la-avispa/), an adventure focused on rescuing her mother, and [Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania](/marvel/ant-man-y-la-avispa-quantumania/) takes her to the Quantum Realm.

### Animation

In most animated titles the Wasp is Janet, as in [Ultimate Avengers: The Movie](/marvel/ultimate-avengers/) or [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/). Lilly voices Hope in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
