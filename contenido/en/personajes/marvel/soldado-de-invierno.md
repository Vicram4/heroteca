Bucky Barnes is Steve Rogers's childhood friend. When he vanishes in the war, everyone assumes he is dead, but he resurfaces decades later as the Winter Soldier, an assassin whose memory has been wiped and whose metal arm makes him even more fearsome. His story follows someone trying to live with what he did when he was not his own master.

## Comics origin

Joe Simon and Jack Kirby create him as [Captain America](/marvel/personajes/capitan-america/)'s teenage sidekick in *Captain America Comics* #1, in 1941. For decades he was held to be dead, one of the few deaths comics honored. In 2005, Ed Brubaker and Steve Epting bring him back as the Winter Soldier, in one of the character's most celebrated stories.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Sebastian Stan appears in [Captain America: The First Avenger](/marvel/capitan-america-el-primer-vengador/) and is the great reveal of [Captain America: The Winter Soldier](/marvel/capitan-america-el-soldado-de-invierno/). He remains a driver of the plot in [Captain America: Civil War](/marvel/capitan-america-civil-war/), and his recovery is the theme of [The Falcon and the Winter Soldier](/marvel/the-falcon-and-the-winter-soldier-t1/), alongside [Sam Wilson](/marvel/personajes/falcon/). [Thunderbolts\*](/marvel/thunderbolts/) pulls him out of politics to lead a group of antiheroes.

### Animation

His presence is smaller than in live action: he appears in [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/) and [Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores-unidos/), the latter with several voices.
