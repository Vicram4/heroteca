Sharon Carter is a highly capable field agent with a heavy surname to carry, that of [Peggy Carter](/marvel/personajes/peggy-carter/). Her job is to watch, infiltrate and hold up under pressure, and it is rarely clear which side she will land on when her bosses get it wrong.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee, Jack Kirby and Dick Ayers introduce her in *Tales of Suspense* #75, from March 1966, as S.H.I.E.L.D.'s Agent 13. She soon becomes the great love of [Steve Rogers](/marvel/personajes/capitan-america/), a complicated romance because she belongs to the family of the woman he left behind in the war.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Emily VanCamp plays her in [Captain America: The Winter Soldier](/marvel/capitan-america-el-soldado-de-invierno/), posing as Steve's neighbor, and in [Captain America: Civil War](/marvel/capitan-america-civil-war/), where helping him costs her the job. She ends up a fugitive, and [The Falcon and the Winter Soldier](/marvel/the-falcon-and-the-winter-soldier-t1/) finds her again in Madripoor, far more cynical. ||The end of that series reveals that she is the Power Broker, and that she gets her government post back to keep selling secrets.||

### Animation

VanCamp voices a variant of her in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
