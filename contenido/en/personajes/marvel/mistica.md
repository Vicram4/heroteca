Raven Darkhölme changes shape at will, so nobody ever knows who she really is. She is a very long-lived mutant and a ruthless spy, and her loyalties always depend on what she thinks is best for her own. In the comics she is also Nightcrawler's mother and [Rogue](/marvel/personajes/picara/)'s adoptive mother.

## Comics origin

Chris Claremont and Dave Cockrum create her in the late seventies, and she debuts in the pages of *Ms. Marvel*. Soon after, heading a new Brotherhood of Evil Mutants, she becomes a regular enemy of the X-Men during Claremont's run.

## On screen

### Live action

Rebecca Romijn plays her in [X-Men](/marvel/x-men/) and [X2](/marvel/x-men-2/), as [Magneto](/marvel/personajes/magneto/)'s right hand, with her famous blue, scaly look. Jennifer Lawrence makes her a more central figure in [X-Men: First Class](/marvel/x-men-primera-generacion/) and [X-Men: Days of Future Past](/marvel/x-men-dias-del-futuro-pasado/), where her actions decide everyone's future.

### Animation

She is a recurring villain in [X-Men](/marvel/x-men-la-serie-animada/), the animated series, and [X-Men '97](/marvel/x-men-97/). In [X-Men: Evolution](/marvel/x-men-evolucion/), she poses as a high school principal.
