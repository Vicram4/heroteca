Wade Wilson is a mercenary with a wrecked body, a healing factor that will not let him die and a mouth that never stops. He knows he is a fictional character and speaks directly to the audience, which lets him mock his own genre. Behind the nonstop gag is a broken man who knows perfectly well what he has lost.

## Comics origin

Rob Liefeld and Fabian Nicieza create him for *The New Mutants* #98, from February 1991, as a hired killer and rival to Cable. His name is a nod to DC's Deathstroke, Slade Wilson. Later writers, especially in the late nineties, built up the humor and fourth-wall breaks that define him today.

## On screen

### X-Men saga

Ryan Reynolds first plays him in [X-Men Origins: Wolverine](/marvel/x-men-origenes-lobezno/), where his mouth is sewn shut, a mistake he later took pleasure in ridiculing. He gets his own back in [Deadpool](/marvel/deadpool/), from 2016, a huge R-rated hit, followed by [Deadpool 2](/marvel/deadpool-2/) and its family cut, [Once Upon a Deadpool](/marvel/erase-una-vez-deadpool/).

### Cinematic Universe

[Deadpool & Wolverine](/marvel/deadpool-y-wolverine/) brings him into the Marvel universe alongside [Wolverine](/marvel/personajes/lobezno/), in a film packed with jokes about the studio itself.

### Animation

Nolan North voices him in [Hulk Vs.](/marvel/hulk-vs/), in the part devoted to Wolverine, and Jason Mantzoukas in [LEGO Marvel Avengers: Mission Demolition](/marvel/lego-marvel-avengers-mision-demolicion/).
