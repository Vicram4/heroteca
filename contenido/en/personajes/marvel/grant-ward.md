Grant Ward is the field operative on [Phil Coulson](/marvel/personajes/phil-coulson/)'s team in [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-t1/), the tough, quiet guy who looks like the show's classic hero. His bond with his teammates, especially [Skye](/marvel/personajes/quake/), grows steadily. What happens to him next is one of the reasons the first season ends so well.

## Comics origin

He was born on television: Joss Whedon, Jed Whedon and Maurissa Tancharoen create him for the 2013 pilot.
