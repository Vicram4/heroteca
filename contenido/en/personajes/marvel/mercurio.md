Pietro Maximoff moves at superhuman speed and lives with the feeling that the world runs too slowly, which makes him arrogant and impatient. Twin brother of the [Scarlet Witch](/marvel/personajes/bruja-escarlata/), he is also very protective of her. He is one of the few characters to have two film versions at once, because of a rights matter between studios.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby introduce him with his sister in *The X-Men* #4, from March 1964, fighting on the side of [Magneto](/marvel/personajes/magneto/) and his Brotherhood. They soon join the [Avengers](/marvel/wiki/vengadores/), and for years they were regarded as Magneto's own children.

## On screen

### X-Men saga

Evan Peters plays a teenage, mocking Pietro in [X-Men: Days of Future Past](/marvel/x-men-dias-del-futuro-pasado/), with a slow-motion kitchen scene that earned much of the film's praise. The formula is repeated in [X-Men: Apocalypse](/marvel/x-men-apocalipsis/).

### Cinematic Universe

Aaron Taylor-Johnson gives him a different tone in [Avengers: Age of Ultron](/marvel/vengadores-la-era-de-ultron/). ||He dies at the end of that film saving Hawkeye.|| ||In WandaVision Evan Peters shows up posing as him, a nod to the other Pietro that turns out to be a trick.||

### Animation

He appears as a supporting character in [X-Men](/marvel/x-men-la-serie-animada/), the animated series, and is a rebellious teenager in [X-Men: Evolution](/marvel/x-men-evolucion/).
