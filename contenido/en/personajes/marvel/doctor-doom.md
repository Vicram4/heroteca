Doctor Doom rules a small European country, Latveria, and combines science and sorcery like nobody else. What makes him fascinating is his pride: he considers himself the most capable man on the planet, has his own code of honor and cannot accept that [Reed Richards](/marvel/personajes/mr-fantastico/) might be better than him. Many regard him as Marvel's greatest villain.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby create him for *The Fantastic Four* #5, from July 1962. He is a genius from humble beginnings, a university classmate of Reed's, who is scarred by a failed experiment and hides behind an iron mask. His mother, a Romani sorceress he wants to rescue from hell, explains much of his obsession.

## On screen

### Live action

Joseph Culp plays him in the unreleased [The Fantastic Four](/marvel/the-fantastic-four-1994/), from 1994. Julian McMahon makes him a businessman gradually turning to metal in [Fantastic Four](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-2005/) and its sequel, [Fantastic Four: Rise of the Silver Surfer](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-y-silver-surfer/). Toby Kebbell takes the part in [Fantastic Four](/marvel/cuatro-fantasticos/), from 2015.

### Animation

He appears regularly in [Fantastic Four: World's Greatest Heroes](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-2006/), from 2006, voiced by Paul Dobson, and in team series such as [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/). Charlie Adler makes him a comic figure in [The Super Hero Squad Show](/marvel/el-escuadron-de-superheroes/).
