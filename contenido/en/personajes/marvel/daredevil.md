Matt Murdock went blind as a child in a chemical accident, which in return sharpened his other senses to superhuman levels. He is a lawyer by day and a vigilante by night in Hell's Kitchen, and his Catholic faith sets up a constant struggle with the violence he deals out. His stories tend to be grimier and more down to earth than those of other heroes.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and artist Bill Everett create him for *Daredevil* #1, from April 1964. The big change comes with Frank Miller, who from 1979 darkens the series, introduces [Elektra](/marvel/personajes/elektra/) and turns [Kingpin](/marvel/personajes/kingpin/) into his quintessential enemy.

## On screen

### Live action

Rex Smith plays him in the 1989 TV film [The Trial of the Incredible Hulk](/marvel/el-juicio-del-increible-hulk/), and Ben Affleck leads the 2003 film [Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil/).

### Cinematic Universe

Charlie Cox finds the right tone in the Netflix series [Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil-t1/), with very physical fights and dry violence, and continues in [The Defenders](/marvel/the-defenders-t1/). After a brief cameo in [Spider-Man: No Way Home](/marvel/spider-man-no-way-home/), he returns in [She-Hulk: Attorney at Law](/marvel/she-hulk-abogada-hulka-t1/), [Echo](/marvel/echo-t1/) and [Daredevil: Born Again](/marvel/daredevil-born-again-t1/).

### Animation

Cox also voices him in [Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man](/marvel/tu-amigo-y-vecino-spider-man/).
