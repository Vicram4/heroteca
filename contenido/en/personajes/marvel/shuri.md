Shuri is the sister of [T'Challa](/marvel/personajes/black-panther/), and she likes to remind him that she is the smart one in the family. She moves just as easily between royal court and laboratory, and her humor takes the solemnity out of everything she touches. Behind that is somebody who has had to grow up very fast.

## Comics origin

Reginald Hudlin and John Romita Jr. create her for *Black Panther* #2, from 2005, and she herself later becomes the new Black Panther. She has been queen and warrior, and the comics place her among the smartest people in the Marvel universe.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Letitia Wright steals plenty of scenes in [Black Panther](/marvel/black-panther/), where she designs all of her brother's technology, and returns in [Avengers: Infinity War](/marvel/vengadores-infinity-war/) and [Avengers: Endgame](/marvel/vengadores-endgame/). ||In Black Panther: Wakanda Forever she loses T'Challa and her mother and takes up the mantle of Black Panther, a story about grief born of Chadwick Boseman's death.||

### Animation

Ozioma Akagha voices her in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/), and she appears in [Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores-unidos/) before her jump to the big screen.
