Wilson Fisk runs New York's organized crime from behind the façade of a respectable businessman. He is enormous, and it is hard to believe how fast he moves, though his real weapon is patience. The unsettling part is that he tends to have his own idea of order and a carefully tended personal life, centered on his wife Vanessa.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and John Romita Sr. create him for *The Amazing Spider-Man* #50, from July 1967. He starts as an enemy of Spider-Man, but Frank Miller brings him into the pages of *Daredevil* from 1981 and turns him into the great rival of [Daredevil](/marvel/personajes/daredevil/), which is how he is best remembered today.

## On screen

### Live action

John Rhys-Davies plays him in the 1989 [The Trial of the Incredible Hulk](/marvel/el-juicio-del-increible-hulk/). Michael Clarke Duncan brings huge physical presence to the film [Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil/).

### Cinematic Universe

Vincent D'Onofrio redefines the character in the series [Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil-t1/), with a clumsy, tender, terrifying Fisk. ||He reappears by surprise at the end of Hawkeye.|| He continues in [Echo](/marvel/echo-t1/) and [Daredevil: Born Again](/marvel/daredevil-born-again-t1/).

### Animation

Duncan voices him again in [Spider-Man: The New Animated Series](/marvel/spider-man-la-nueva-serie-animada/). In [Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse](/marvel/spider-man-un-nuevo-universo/), Liev Schreiber makes him a gigantic man driven by a family loss.
