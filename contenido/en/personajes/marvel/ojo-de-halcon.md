Clint Barton is an exceptional archer who moves among heroes able to lift cars. He has no powers or armor, just a bow, arrows of every kind and plenty of pride, and much of his charm comes from knowing it. Under the humor is a tired man trying to do right despite his mistakes.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Don Heck introduce him in *Tales of Suspense* #57, in September 1964, as a rival of Iron Man, deceived along the way by [Black Widow](/marvel/personajes/viuda-negra/). In May 1965, in *The Avengers* #16, he reforms and joins the [Avengers](/marvel/wiki/vengadores/), where he is a loudmouth and a headache for [Captain America](/marvel/personajes/capitan-america/).

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Jeremy Renner has played him since a brief cameo in [Thor](/marvel/thor/). He goes from being under Loki's control in [The Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores/) to revealing a secret family in [Avengers: Age of Ultron](/marvel/vengadores-la-era-de-ultron/), a twist that finally gives him some weight of his own. ||In Avengers: Endgame, shattered by the loss of his family, he works as a bloodthirsty vigilante under the name Ronin.|| The series [Hawkeye](/marvel/ojo-de-halcon-t1/) gives him a Christmas story alongside [Kate Bishop](/marvel/personajes/kate-bishop/), who is positioned as his successor.

### Animation

He has been around since [Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-la-serie-animada/), the animated series, and [The Avengers: United They Stand](/marvel/the-avengers-united-they-stand/), and [Next Avengers: Heroes of Tomorrow](/marvel/nuevos-vengadores-heroes-del-manana/) shows him as an older survivor training the next generation.
