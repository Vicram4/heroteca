Marc Spector is a former mercenary who serves Khonshu, the Egyptian moon god, in exchange for having come back from the dead. His mind is split among several personalities, and his story plays on that: at times not even he knows which one is in charge. He is one of Marvel's most unsettling heroes, with his white suit and mask.

## Comics origin

Doug Moench and Don Perlin create him for *Werewolf by Night* #32, from August 1975, as a hired villain. He soon turns into a Batman-like hero, with identities such as Steven Grant and Jake Lockley, and over the years his disorder becomes the key to his best stories.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Oscar Isaac plays Marc and the British Steven Grant in [Moon Knight](/marvel/caballero-luna-t1/), from 2022, an Egyptian adventure that moves from humor to horror, with Ethan Hawke as the villain. ||The post-credits scene confirms a third identity, Jake Lockley, whom Marc knows nothing about either.||

### Animation

Gideon Emery voices him in [Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores-unidos/), and Scott Porter in [LEGO Marvel Avengers: Mission Demolition](/marvel/lego-marvel-avengers-mision-demolicion/).
