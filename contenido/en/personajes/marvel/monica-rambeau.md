Monica Rambeau can turn herself into any form of energy, and for years she was one of the most powerful and least remembered characters in Marvel comics. On screen it is a different story: the girl who looked up to [Carol Danvers](/marvel/personajes/capitana-marvel/) grows into an agent with powers of her own and good reasons to feel hurt.

## Comics origin

Roger Stern and John Romita Jr. create her for *The Amazing Spider-Man Annual* #16, from October 1982, as a New Orleans harbor patrol lieutenant who becomes the new Captain Marvel. She goes on to lead the Avengers. Later she changes name several times: Photon, Pulsar and Spectrum.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Akira and Azari Akbar play the child in [Captain Marvel](/marvel/capitana-marvel/). Teyonah Parris plays her as an adult in [WandaVision](/marvel/wandavision-t1/), where she is one of the few who treat Wanda with humanity. ||By crossing the Westview barrier several times, she gains her powers.|| She forms a trio with Carol and [Kamala Khan](/marvel/personajes/ms-marvel/) in [The Marvels](/marvel/the-marvels/). ||She ends up trapped in another reality alongside Beast.||

### Animation

Parris voices her in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
