Carol Danvers is an air force pilot who, through an accident with alien technology, gains one of the greatest reserves of power in the universe. Her stories revolve around pride and getting back up after each fall. She often appears as the heroine who stays away because she has bigger problems to handle elsewhere in the galaxy.

## Comics origin

Roy Thomas and Gene Colan introduce her in *Marvel Super-Heroes* #13, from March 1968, as an Air Force officer working with the Kree hero Mar-Vell. In the late seventies she has her own series as Ms. Marvel, and in 2012 she takes the name Captain Marvel.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Brie Larson plays her in [Captain Marvel](/marvel/capitana-marvel/), set in the nineties, a story in which she does not remember who she was and that also features a young [Nick Fury](/marvel/personajes/nick-fury/). [The Marvels](/marvel/the-marvels/) brings her together with [Monica Rambeau](/marvel/personajes/monica-rambeau/) and with [Ms. Marvel](/marvel/personajes/ms-marvel/), who admires her. ||In Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, Lashana Lynch plays the Captain Marvel of another universe, who there is Maria Rambeau.||

### Animation

She appears in [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/), still under the name Ms. Marvel, and is a reference point in [Marvel Rising: Secret Warriors](/marvel/marvel-rising-secret-warriors/).
