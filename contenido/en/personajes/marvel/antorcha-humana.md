Johnny Storm flies and burns at will, and he knows it. He is the group's youngster, vain and fearless, who spends his time winding up [Ben Grimm](/marvel/personajes/la-cosa/) and enjoying being famous. Beneath that pose is a very loyal brother and teammate, who grows up the hard way over the course of his stories.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby create him for *Fantastic Four* #1, from November 1961, reviving the name of the flaming android Carl Burgos had created in 1939. The younger brother of [Sue Storm](/marvel/personajes/mujer-invisible/), he soon has solo adventures and strikes up a long friendship with [Spider-Man](/marvel/personajes/spider-man/).

## On screen

### Live action

Jay Underwood plays him in the unreleased [The Fantastic Four](/marvel/the-fantastic-four-1994/). Chris Evans, before becoming Captain America, nails him in [Fantastic Four](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-2005/) and [Fantastic Four: Rise of the Silver Surfer](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-y-silver-surfer/). Michael B. Jordan gives him a different feel in [Fantastic Four](/marvel/cuatro-fantasticos/), from 2015.

### Cinematic Universe

Joseph Quinn is the Johnny of [The Fantastic 4: First Steps](/marvel/los-cuatro-fantasticos-primeros-pasos/), where he deciphers Galactus's signals. ||Chris Evans returns to the role by surprise in Deadpool & Wolverine, as a variant from another universe, in a nod to an audience expecting to see Captain America.||

### Animation

Christopher Jacot voices him in [Fantastic Four: World's Greatest Heroes](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-2006/), from 2006.
