Hank Pym invented the particles that let a person shrink or grow, and he was the first Ant-Man. He has enormous talent and a temper that makes him unbearable at times. His stories tend to revolve around guilt: what he has created, who he has lost, and who he dares to hand his invention to.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee, Larry Lieber and Jack Kirby introduce him in *Tales to Astonish* #27, from January 1962, in a horror tale about a man trapped in an anthill. A few months later, in #35, he returns in costume as Ant-Man. He helps to found the Avengers, changes identity several times and is also the creator of [Ultron](/marvel/personajes/ultron/).

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Michael Douglas makes him a jaded veteran in [Ant-Man](/marvel/ant-man-2015/): a retired man, at odds with his daughter [Hope](/marvel/personajes/avispa/), who picks [Scott Lang](/marvel/personajes/ant-man/) to wear the suit. [Ant-Man and the Wasp](/marvel/ant-man-y-la-avispa/) centers on his search for [Janet van Dyne](/marvel/personajes/janet-van-dyne/), lost in the Quantum Realm. ||He vanishes in Thanos's snap at the end of that film and returns in Avengers: Endgame.|| He continues in [Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania](/marvel/ant-man-y-la-avispa-quantumania/).

### Animation

Nolan North voices him in [Ultimate Avengers: The Movie](/marvel/ultimate-avengers/) and its sequel, and he appears in the [Ant-Man](/marvel/ant-man-2017/) shorts. ||In What If...?, Douglas plays a Pym who, shattered by his daughter's death, turns killer.||
