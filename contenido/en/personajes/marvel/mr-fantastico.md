Reed Richards is a scientist of near-limitless talent and leader of the Fantastic Four, able to stretch his body at will after a trip into space went wrong. He often gets lost in giant problems and neglects small ones, including his family, starting with [Sue Storm](/marvel/personajes/mujer-invisible/). His mind is both his greatest virtue and his great flaw.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby introduce him in *The Fantastic Four* #1, from November 1961, the issue that kicks off modern Marvel. The group works more as a family in an argument than as a team, a novelty at the time.

## On screen

### Live action

Alex Hyde-White is Reed in [The Fantastic Four](/marvel/the-fantastic-four-1994/), a low-budget 1994 film that was never officially released. Ioan Gruffudd plays him in [Fantastic Four](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-2005/) and [Fantastic Four: Rise of the Silver Surfer](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-y-silver-surfer/), and Miles Teller in the 2015 attempt, [Fantastic Four](/marvel/cuatro-fantasticos/).

### Cinematic Universe

||John Krasinski plays him briefly in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, as a member of another universe's Illuminati.|| Pedro Pascal takes the role in [The Fantastic 4: First Steps](/marvel/los-cuatro-fantasticos-primeros-pasos/), set in its own retro-futuristic universe.

### Animation

He has had series since the 1967 [The Fantastic Four](/marvel/the-fantastic-four-1967/) and is a regular presence in Marvel productions such as [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/).
