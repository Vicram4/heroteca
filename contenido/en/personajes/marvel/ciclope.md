Scott Summers fires beams from his eyes that he cannot shut off, so he lives behind a visor or ruby-quartz glasses. That forced control suits his nature: disciplined, serious and the favorite student of [Charles Xavier](/marvel/personajes/profesor-x/), with the job of leading the X-Men even if it costs him his popularity.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby create him for *The X-Men* #1, from September 1963, as leader of the founding group. His relationship with [Jean Grey](/marvel/personajes/jean-grey/) and his rivalry with [Wolverine](/marvel/personajes/lobezno/) drive many of the team's stories for decades.

## On screen

### Live action

James Marsden is Cyclops in [X-Men](/marvel/x-men/) and [X2](/marvel/x-men-2/), always in Logan's shadow. ||In X-Men: The Last Stand he dies early at the hands of Jean, by then transformed into Phoenix.|| The new timeline makes him younger: Tye Sheridan plays a teenage Scott in [X-Men: Apocalypse](/marvel/x-men-apocalipsis/) and [Dark Phoenix](/marvel/x-men-fenix-oscura/).

### Animation

He fares better here than in the films. He is the undisputed leader in [X-Men](/marvel/x-men-la-serie-animada/), the animated series, in [X-Men: Evolution](/marvel/x-men-evolucion/) and in [X-Men '97](/marvel/x-men-97/), the last with Ray Chase, where his family drama sits at the center of the story.
