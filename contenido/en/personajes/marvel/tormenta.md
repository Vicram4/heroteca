Ororo Munroe controls the weather, from wind to lightning, and moves through the world with an almost royal serenity. Her past gives her more shades than it seems: she grew up picking pockets in Cairo, came to be worshipped as a goddess in Africa, and has a claustrophobia that even her enemies exploit. Among the X-Men she is one of the most respected leaders.

## Comics origin

Len Wein and Dave Cockrum create her for *Giant-Size X-Men* #1, in May 1975, when the team is renewed with mutants from different countries. Under Chris Claremont's pen she came to lead the group, and in 2006 she married [Black Panther](/marvel/personajes/black-panther/).

## On screen

### Live action

Halle Berry plays her in [X-Men](/marvel/x-men/) and the sequels in that line, gaining weight in [X-Men: The Last Stand](/marvel/x-men-la-decision-final/). Alexandra Shipp plays her young in [X-Men: Apocalypse](/marvel/x-men-apocalipsis/), where she initially serves the villain, and in [Dark Phoenix](/marvel/x-men-fenix-oscura/).

### Animation

Alison Sealy-Smith gives her a solemn, unforgettable voice in [X-Men](/marvel/x-men-la-serie-animada/), the animated series, and returns in [X-Men '97](/marvel/x-men-97/), which gives her one of its finest arcs. She also stands out in [X-Men: Evolution](/marvel/x-men-evolucion/), as an adult figure in the team.
