Red Skull is the enemy in its purest form: a fanatic who embodies everything [Captain America](/marvel/personajes/capitan-america/) fights against. His skull face, sometimes a mask and sometimes flesh, makes him instantly recognizable. What does not change over the decades is his obsession with ruling the world and destroying Steve Rogers.

## Comics origin

Joe Simon, Jack Kirby and France Herron create him for *Captain America Comics* #1, from March 1941, even before the United States entered the war. That first Skull turns out to be an impostor, and the real one, Johann Schmidt, takes hold in the sixties. Later stories explain how he survives the passage of time through means such as a chemical sleep or moving into other bodies.

## On screen

### Live action

Scott Paulin plays him in [Captain America](/marvel/capitan-america-1990/), from 1990, which makes him Italian.

### Cinematic Universe

Hugo Weaving gives him an aristocratic bearing in [Captain America: The First Avenger](/marvel/capitan-america-el-primer-vengador/), where he leads Hydra and splits the organization from the Nazis themselves. ||The Tesseract makes him vanish at the end, and he reappears in Avengers: Infinity War, now played by Ross Marquand, as guardian of the Soul Stone on Vormir.|| Marquand repeats the part in [Avengers: Endgame](/marvel/vengadores-endgame/) and in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).

### Animation

Liam O'Brien voices him in [Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores-unidos/), where he is a regular villain, and he also turns up in the anime [Marvel Disk Wars: The Avengers](/marvel/marvel-disk-wars-avengers/).
