Bullets cannot get through Luke Cage, and that is almost all the fantasy there is to his story. The rest is an ex-convict, jailed for something he did not do, who only wants a quiet life and ends up defending his neighborhood because nobody else will. He does not bother with a mask: everybody knows where to find him.

## Comics origin

Archie Goodwin, George Tuska, Roy Thomas and John Romita Sr. create him for *Luke Cage, Hero for Hire* #1, from June 1972, at the height of the blaxploitation craze. A prison experiment gives him his strength, and once out he offers his services as a hero for rent. Later he becomes partners with [Iron Fist](/marvel/personajes/iron-fist/) and starts a family with [Jessica Jones](/marvel/personajes/jessica-jones/).

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Mike Colter debuts in [Jessica Jones](/marvel/jessica-jones-t1/) and leads [Luke Cage](/marvel/luke-cage-t1/), a series steeped in Harlem and its music. He joins the rest of the group in [The Defenders](/marvel/the-defenders-t1/). ||The second season ends with him running the Harlem's Paradise club, on a rather ambiguous note.|| Colter returns for a brief appearance in [Daredevil: Born Again](/marvel/daredevil-born-again-t2/).

### Animation

He is a teenager calling himself Power Man in [Ultimate Spider-Man](/marvel/ultimate-spider-man/), voiced by Ogie Banks, and has a supporting part in [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/).
