Carnage is what happens when [Venom](/marvel/personajes/venom/)'s symbiote has an offspring that bonds with a serial killer. Cletus Kasady kills for no reason and enjoys it, so any story with him turns considerably darker. He is red, savage and unpredictable, the very opposite of the antihero Venom tries to be.

## Comics origin

David Michelinie and Mark Bagley introduce him in *The Amazing Spider-Man* #361, from April 1992, after Kasady had appeared unnamed in earlier issues as Eddie Brock's cellmate. He was very popular in the nineties, especially in the *Maximum Carnage* saga.

## On screen

### Live action

Woody Harrelson shows up in the scene after the credits of [Venom](/marvel/venom/), from 2018, in a much-discussed wig, and is the villain of [Venom: Let There Be Carnage](/marvel/venom-habra-matanza/), with Naomie Harris as Shriek, in a short and very manic film.

### Animation

Michael Donovan voices him in [Spider-Man Unlimited](/marvel/spider-man-unlimited/), from 1999.
