Wong is the inseparable companion of [Doctor Strange](/marvel/personajes/doctor-strange/). He is a master of the mystic arts with a dry sense of humor, and his job is often to check Stephen when he gets too clever. Over time he has left the helper image behind and become a character with weight of his own.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Steve Ditko create him for *Strange Tales* #110, from July 1963, the same issue in which Strange debuts. At that point he is a heavily stereotyped manservant, and his role improves with the years.

## On screen

### Live action

Clyde Kusatsu plays him in the 1978 TV film [Dr. Strange](/marvel/dr-strange/).

### Cinematic Universe

Benedict Wong reinvents him in [Doctor Strange](/marvel/doctor-strange/), as a stern librarian with an unexpected fondness for Beyoncé. ||During the five years Strange is missing, between Avengers: Infinity War and Avengers: Endgame, Wong becomes Sorcerer Supreme, a title he keeps afterward.|| He has fun in his cameos in [Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings](/marvel/shang-chi-y-la-leyenda-de-los-diez-anillos/) and [She-Hulk: Attorney at Law](/marvel/she-hulk-abogada-hulka-t1/), and is a key figure in [Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness](/marvel/doctor-strange-en-el-multiverso-de-la-locura/).

### Animation

Paul Nakauchi voices him in [Doctor Strange](/marvel/doctor-strange-el-hechicero-supremo/), and Benedict Wong returns in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
