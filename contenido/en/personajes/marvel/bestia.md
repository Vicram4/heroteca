Hank McCoy has the hands and feet of a primate, colossal strength and agility, and at the same time vast learning and a taste for literary quotation. That contrast is the core of the character: a genius fighting prejudice over his looks while researching how to help mutants. He is also among the kindest members of the team.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby include him in the founding group of *The X-Men* #1, in September 1963. At first he looked human, only with oversized hands and feet. In the early seventies a failed serum gave him the fur he is recognized by today, and soon after he was a member of the [Avengers](/marvel/wiki/vengadores/).

## On screen

### Live action

After a nod on a TV screen in [X2](/marvel/x-men-2/), Kelsey Grammer is an adult, diplomatic Beast in [X-Men: The Last Stand](/marvel/x-men-la-decision-final/). Nicholas Hoult plays the young version from [X-Men: First Class](/marvel/x-men-primera-generacion/) onward, more tormented by his looks. ||Grammer returns in the post-credits scene of The Marvels, in another universe.||

### Animation

George Buza voices him in [X-Men](/marvel/x-men-la-serie-animada/), the animated series, and in [X-Men '97](/marvel/x-men-97/), and he also features in [Wolverine and the X-Men](/marvel/lobezno-y-los-x-men/).
