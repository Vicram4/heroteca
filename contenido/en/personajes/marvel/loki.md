Loki grew up in Asgard in Thor's shadow, knowing or suspecting he was not like the others. His weapon is the lie: he plots, deceives and slips away when everything goes wrong, which is often. The fun of the character is that you can rarely tell whether what he does is betrayal, survival or a clumsy attempt to be loved.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee, Larry Lieber and Jack Kirby adapt him from Norse mythology in *Journey into Mystery* #85, in October 1962, as Thor's stepbrother and enemy. A very different version existed in 1949, but this is the one that lasted. His scheme in *The Avengers* #1 is also what brings the [Avengers](/marvel/wiki/vengadores/) together in the first place.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Tom Hiddleston has played him since [Thor](/marvel/thor/), and he is the main villain of [The Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores/). Audiences grew so fond of him that he stopped being merely an enemy, as seen in [Thor: The Dark World](/marvel/thor-el-mundo-oscuro/) and [Thor: Ragnarok](/marvel/thor-ragnarok/). ||Thanos kills him at the start of Avengers: Infinity War, but in Avengers: Endgame a 2012 version escapes with the Tesseract and opens another timeline.|| That variant leads the series [Loki](/marvel/loki-t1/), a story about fate and second chances that also brings in [Sylvie](/marvel/personajes/sylvie/).

### Animation

He remains a favorite antagonist in [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/) and in [Thor: Tales of Asgard](/marvel/thor-tales-of-asgard/), which shows him young. Hiddleston voices him in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
