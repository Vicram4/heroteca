Gamora loses her people as a child and is taken in by Thanos, who makes her the most dangerous woman in the galaxy. She spends her life trying to escape him, and what sets her apart from other reformed killers is that she knows exactly how much she owes the person who destroyed her. With the Guardians of the Galaxy she finds a family, reluctantly.

## Comics origin

Jim Starlin creates her for *Strange Tales* #180, from June 1975, as a key piece of his cosmic sagas about [Thanos](/marvel/personajes/thanos/) and Adam Warlock. Years later she resurfaces in the 2008 Guardians of the Galaxy.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Zoe Saldana plays her in [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2014/), where her rivalry with her sister [Nebula](/marvel/personajes/nebula/) and her relationship with [Star-Lord](/marvel/personajes/star-lord/) drive the story. ||In Avengers: Infinity War, Thanos sacrifices her on Vormir to obtain the Soul Stone, and in Avengers: Endgame a 2014 version arrives who does not know the Guardians, the same one who returns in Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3.||

### Animation

She appears in the nineties series [Silver Surfer](/marvel/silver-surfer/), in [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2015/) and in the LEGO productions.
