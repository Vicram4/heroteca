Vision is an artificial body with a mind of his own, able to turn intangible or weigh as much as a mountain. Despite his powers, what really interests him is understanding people, and his almost comic calm hides a great many doubts. His relationship with [Wanda Maximoff](/marvel/personajes/bruja-escarlata/) is one of Marvel's strangest and best loved.

## Comics origin

Roy Thomas and John Buscema create him for *The Avengers* #57, from 1968, reviving the name of a hero from the forties. [Ultron](/marvel/personajes/ultron/) builds him to destroy the Avengers, but he rebels and joins the team. He ends up marrying the Scarlet Witch, and his doubts about his own humanity shape many of his best stories.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Paul Bettany had already voiced J.A.R.V.I.S. in the [Iron Man](/marvel/personajes/iron-man/) films, and he takes on the red body in [Avengers: Age of Ultron](/marvel/vengadores-la-era-de-ultron/). He faces his teammates in [Captain America: Civil War](/marvel/capitan-america-civil-war/). ||Thanos rips out the Mind Stone and kills him in Avengers: Infinity War.|| Then comes [WandaVision](/marvel/wandavision-t1/), an oddity that imitates the TV sitcoms of each decade. ||That Vision is a creation of Wanda's magic, and at the end a white version rebuilt by the government appears too.||

### Animation

Peter Jessop voices him in [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/), and Bettany takes up the role again in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/). He was already part of the group in [The Avengers: United They Stand](/marvel/the-avengers-united-they-stand/), from 1999.
