James Rhodes, "Rhodey", is an army pilot and officer and the person who tells Tony Stark what he does not want to hear. His armor, loaded with weaponry, comes from his friend's technology, but his way of thinking is very different: he does believe in the chain of command, and that causes him more than one conflict.

## Comics origin

David Michelinie, John Byrne and Bob Layton create him for *Iron Man* #118, in 1979, as Stark's friend and pilot. In *Iron Man* #169, from 1983, he dons his friend's armor when Stark falls into alcoholism. War Machine's own identity arrives in 1992, with a different, far heavier suit.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Terrence Howard plays him in [Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-2008/). From [Iron Man 2](/marvel/iron-man-2/) on, Don Cheadle replaces him, takes up the armor and stays with him through most of the saga. ||In Captain America: Civil War he suffers a fall that paralyzes him from the waist down, though he stays active with prosthetics.|| He later has a significant part in [Secret Invasion](/marvel/invasion-secreta-t1/).

### Animation

He is a supporting character in [Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-la-serie-animada/), the animated series, where two different actors played him, and in [Iron Man: Armored Adventures](/marvel/iron-man-aventuras-de-hierro/), where he is Tony's teenage friend.
