Otto Octavius is a shy scientist whom an accident leaves with four mechanical arms joined to his body and a changed personality. He has intelligence to spare and no patience for others, and he is often set up as a reflection of what Peter Parker might have been had he used his talent only for himself.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Steve Ditko introduce him in *The Amazing Spider-Man* #3, from July 1963, as the first villain to defeat the hero. He leads the Sinister Six and, decades later, in *The Superior Spider-Man*, even swaps bodies with Peter.

## On screen

### Live action

Alfred Molina gives one of the great villain performances in [Spider-Man 2](/marvel/spider-man-2/), as a tragic scientist whose arms whisper to him. ||He returns in Spider-Man: No Way Home, pulled out of his universe by a spell and given a kinder ending than the one he had.||

### Animation

He appears in [The Spectacular Spider-Man](/marvel/el-espectacular-spider-man/) and [Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man](/marvel/tu-amigo-y-vecino-spider-man/). The most unexpected version is in [Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse](/marvel/spider-man-un-nuevo-universo/), with Kathryn Hahn: a woman named Olivia Octavius, a cheerful and lethal scientist.
