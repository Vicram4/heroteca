Cain Marko does not know how to stop, and that is literally his power. A mystic gem gives him an unstoppable strength that lets him smash through anything, and his helmet shields him from mental attacks. Beneath the armor is a man full of envy toward [Charles Xavier](/marvel/personajes/profesor-x/), his stepbrother, a feeling that has driven him for decades.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby create him for *The X-Men* #12, from July 1965. After years of bitterness toward his father and stepbrother, he finds the Gem of Cyttorak in a temple, and it transforms him. He is no mutant, as some films present him, but someone granted a mystic power.

## On screen

### X-Men saga

Vinnie Jones plays him in [X-Men: The Last Stand](/marvel/x-men-la-decision-final/), where he is a mutant with a line that became very popular online. He also appears in [Deadpool 2](/marvel/deadpool-2/), as a digital giant.

### Animation

Ron Gans voices him in [X-Men: Pryde of the X-Men](/marvel/x-men-pryde-of-the-x-men/), from 1989, and he features in the 1992 [X-Men](/marvel/x-men-la-serie-animada/) series.
