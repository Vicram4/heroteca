Ben Grimm is the pilot who traveled into space with his friends and came back as a pile of orange rock. He is the strongest of the group and the only one the change robbed of his looks for good, so beneath the jokes and the brawls there is a sadness that never quite goes away. His battle cry, "It's clobberin' time!", is among the most famous in Marvel.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby create him for *Fantastic Four* #1, from November 1961. He is a tough guy raised on Yancy Street, on New York's Lower East Side, and a friend of [Reed Richards](/marvel/personajes/mr-fantastico/) since college. Kirby poured much of his own childhood in that neighborhood into him.

## On screen

### Live action

Michael Bailey Smith plays Ben in the unreleased [The Fantastic Four](/marvel/the-fantastic-four-1994/), from 1994. Michael Chiklis wears a heavy rubber suit in [Fantastic Four](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-2005/) and [Fantastic Four: Rise of the Silver Surfer](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-y-silver-surfer/), and Jamie Bell plays him with digital effects in [Fantastic Four](/marvel/cuatro-fantasticos/), from 2015.

### Cinematic Universe

Ebon Moss-Bachrach gives him voice and gesture in [The Fantastic 4: First Steps](/marvel/los-cuatro-fantasticos-primeros-pasos/), a gentler, calmer Ben than usual.

### Animation

Paul Frees voices him in the series [The Fantastic Four](/marvel/the-fantastic-four-1967/), from 1967. The peculiar [Fred and Barney Meet The Thing](/marvel/fred-and-barney-meet-the-thing/), from 1979, makes him a teenager who transforms by means of a pair of rings. Brian Dobson voices him in [Fantastic Four: World's Greatest Heroes](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-2006/), from 2006.
