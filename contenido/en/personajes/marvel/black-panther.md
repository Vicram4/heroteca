T'Challa rules Wakanda, an African nation that has hidden its wealth and technology from the world thanks to vibranium. As Black Panther, he is at once king, scientist and warrior, with a calm that sets him apart from other heroes. His great dilemma is political: whether his nation should stay hidden or open up to others.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby introduce him in *Fantastic Four* #52, from July 1966, an issue in which he even gets the better of the Fantastic Four. He is regarded as the first Black superhero from the big American publishers. He later joins the [Avengers](/marvel/wiki/vengadores/).

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Chadwick Boseman debuts in [Captain America: Civil War](/marvel/capitan-america-civil-war/) and leads [Black Panther](/marvel/black-panther/), a film with an enormous cultural impact that debates heritage, identity and colonialism. Boseman died in 2020, and Marvel chose not to replace him: [Black Panther: Wakanda Forever](/marvel/black-panther-wakanda-forever/) builds his absence into the story.

### Animation

He appears in [Ultimate Avengers 2](/marvel/ultimate-avengers-2-rise-of-the-panther/) and in series such as [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/). [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/) was also Boseman's last work, imagining a T'Challa who becomes Star-Lord.
