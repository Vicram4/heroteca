Drax is a huge warrior seeking revenge for the murder of his family by Thanos. In the films, that fury is mixed with a comic trait: he does not understand metaphors and takes everything literally, which makes him the most unintentionally funny of the Guardians of the Galaxy.

## Comics origin

Jim Starlin creates him, with help from Mike Friedrich, in *The Invincible Iron Man* #55, from February 1973, the same issue in which [Thanos](/marvel/personajes/thanos/) debuts. There he is a murdered human whose spirit is moved into an artificial body made for the sole purpose of killing Thanos.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Dave Bautista has played him since [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2014/), where his literal humor becomes one of the great attractions. He appears in [Avengers: Infinity War](/marvel/vengadores-infinity-war/), with the confrontation he has long awaited, and in [The Guardians of the Galaxy Holiday Special](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-especial-felices-fiestas/). ||In Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 he discovers his true calling is that of a father, not a destroyer.||

### Animation

He appears in the 1998 series [Silver Surfer](/marvel/silver-surfer/) and later in [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2015/), with David Sobolov.
