Foggy Nelson is the person who keeps [Matt Murdock](/marvel/personajes/daredevil/) grounded. He is a lawyer with no powers and no costume, a joker who is braver than he looks, and his friendship, tested many times, gives Daredevil a good part of its heart.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Bill Everett create him for *Daredevil* #1, from April 1964, as Matt's partner at the law firm. At first he is comic relief, in love with [Karen Page](/marvel/personajes/karen-page/). In time he becomes a prosecutor, learns his friend's secret and turns into one of the few people Matt can trust.

## On screen

### Live action

Jon Favreau plays him for laughs in the film [Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil/), from 2003.

### Cinematic Universe

Elden Henson gives him far more weight in the series [Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil-t1/), where finding out who Matt really is puts their friendship at risk. He returns in [The Defenders](/marvel/the-defenders-t1/) and helps other heroes in [Jessica Jones](/marvel/jessica-jones-t2/) and [Luke Cage](/marvel/luke-cage-t2/). ||Daredevil: Born Again opens with his murder at the hands of Bullseye, and everything Matt does afterwards stems from that loss.||
