Yelena Belova comes from the same school of assassins as [Natasha Romanoff](/marvel/personajes/viuda-negra/), and shares both the training and the damage. On screen she is the little sister who mocks Natasha's superhero pose, and her dry humor hides a good deal of loneliness.

## Comics origin

Devin Grayson and J. G. Jones create her for *Inhumans* #5, from January 1999. There she appears as a new Black Widow sent to replace Natasha, a rival she eventually recognizes as better than she thought. In the comics she is not her sister but a competitor.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Florence Pugh appears in [Black Widow](/marvel/viuda-negra/), bringing much life to the film through the makeshift family she forms with Natasha. ||After the credits she is hired to kill Clint Barton, which continues in Hawkeye.|| She leads a group of outcasts in [Thunderbolts*](/marvel/thunderbolts/), where she is the best thing in the film, and returns in [Spider-Man: Brand New Day](/marvel/spider-man-brand-new-day/).

### Animation

Julie Nathanson voices her in [Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores-unidos/).
