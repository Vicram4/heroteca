The Silver Surfer is a silver man who travels the cosmos on a board, an image as strange as it is unforgettable. He gave up his freedom to Galactus to save his planet and seeks out worlds for his master to devour, a guilt that turns the character into the sad philosopher of the comics. Few heroes talk so much about the human condition without being human.

## Comics origin

Jack Kirby draws him on his own initiative for *The Fantastic Four* #48, from March 1966, in the saga of Galactus's arrival, and Stan Lee is so delighted that he gives him a voice and plenty of space. His past as Norrin Radd of the planet Zenn-La, and his love for Shalla-Bal, appear soon after.

## On screen

### Live action

Doug Jones plays him and Laurence Fishburne voices him in [Fantastic Four: Rise of the Silver Surfer](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-y-silver-surfer/), from 2007.

### Cinematic Universe

Julia Garner plays a female version, Shalla-Bal, who serves Galactus in [The Fantastic 4: First Steps](/marvel/los-cuatro-fantasticos-primeros-pasos/). ||She ends up turning against him to save the Earth.||

### Animation

He has his own series, [Silver Surfer](/marvel/silver-surfer/), from 1998, voiced by Paul Essiembre, short-lived and with a very distinctive style. He turns up as well in [The Super Hero Squad Show](/marvel/el-escuadron-de-superheroes/).
