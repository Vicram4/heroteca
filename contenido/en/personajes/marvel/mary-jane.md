Mary Jane is the girl next door who does not fit that label: outgoing, breezy and with more family trouble than she lets on. Her lightness contrasts with Peter Parker's gloom, which is why she is the hero's best-remembered partner.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Steve Ditko mention her in *The Amazing Spider-Man* #15, in 1964, as a neighbor's niece whom May keeps trying to set up with Peter. For years her face stays hidden, until issue 42, in 1966, when John Romita Sr. finally draws her and she delivers her famous "Face it, Tiger, you just hit the jackpot!". She marries Peter in 1987, a marriage the comics undid twenty years later.

## On screen

### Live action

Kirsten Dunst is Mary Jane in the [Spider-Man](/marvel/spider-man-2002/) trilogy, with the famous upside-down kiss in the rain.

### Cinematic Universe

Zendaya plays a high school classmate of Peter's in [Spider-Man: Homecoming](/marvel/spider-man-homecoming/), with another surname (Michelle Jones-Watson) and almost nothing in common with the classic Mary Jane apart from the nickname MJ. She becomes his girlfriend in [Spider-Man: Far From Home](/marvel/spider-man-lejos-de-casa/). ||At the end of Spider-Man: No Way Home, a spell erases all memory of Peter from her mind.||

### Animation

She has a major role in [Spider-Man: The New Animated Series](/marvel/spider-man-la-nueva-serie-animada/) and [The Spectacular Spider-Man](/marvel/el-espectacular-spider-man/). In [Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse](/marvel/spider-man-un-nuevo-universo/), Zoë Kravitz voices the Mary Jane of an alternate universe.
