Norman Osborn is an unscrupulous businessman and the father of Peter Parker's best friend. An experimental formula gives him strength and warps his mind, and behind the mask and glider emerges the Green Goblin, a personality who laughs as he does harm. The personal angle is what makes him so dangerous: he knows exactly where Peter hurts.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Steve Ditko create him for *The Amazing Spider-Man* #14, from July 1964, without revealing who is behind the mask. The secret holds until 1966, and in 1973 he commits his best-known crime, the death of [Gwen Stacy](/marvel/personajes/gwen-stacy/). Decades later he even ends up running national security for the United States.

## On screen

### Live action

Willem Dafoe is the Green Goblin in [Spider-Man](/marvel/spider-man-2002/), with a performance that plays on split personality. In [The Amazing Spider-Man 2](/marvel/the-amazing-spider-man-2-el-poder-de-electro/), Chris Cooper is Norman and Dane DeHaan plays his son Harry, who becomes the Goblin. ||Dafoe returns in Spider-Man: No Way Home as the film's main villain, and his attack costs Aunt May her life.||

### Animation

He is a regular adversary in the hero's series, such as [The Spectacular Spider-Man](/marvel/el-espectacular-spider-man/). [Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man](/marvel/tu-amigo-y-vecino-spider-man/), with Colman Domingo's voice, presents him as Peter's mentor.
