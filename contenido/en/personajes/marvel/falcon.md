Sam Wilson is an ordinary man, feet on the ground even when he flies on mechanical wings, who devotes himself to helping others. He becomes [Captain America](/marvel/personajes/capitan-america/)'s closest ally and someone able to tell him the truth. His story poses an uncomfortable question: what it means to carry an American symbol as a Black man in the United States.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Gene Colan introduce him in *Captain America* #117, from September 1969, at the height of the civil rights struggle. He is the first African American superhero from the big American publishers. For years the title carried his name too, and in the mid-2010s he took on the role of Captain America himself.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Anthony Mackie has played him since [Captain America: The Winter Soldier](/marvel/capitan-america-el-soldado-de-invierno/), where we meet him jogging with Steve Rogers. ||At the end of Avengers: Endgame, Steve hands him the shield.|| [The Falcon and the Winter Soldier](/marvel/the-falcon-and-the-winter-soldier-t1/) deals with his doubts about that inheritance, and [Captain America: Brave New World](/marvel/capitan-america-brave-new-world/) shows him already holding the new title.

### Animation

He has a place in [The Avengers: United They Stand](/marvel/the-avengers-united-they-stand/) and other team series, and Mackie voices him in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
