Venom is what happens when a ruined reporter, Eddie Brock, bonds with an alien suit that hates [Spider-Man](/marvel/personajes/spider-man/). The two share a body and talk to each other like a badly matched married couple, and that dynamic drives the character. Depending on the era, he is a monster who wants to eat Peter Parker or a vigilante with brutal methods.

## Comics origin

Spider-Man's black suit arrives in *The Amazing Spider-Man* #252, from May 1984, and is later found to be alive. David Michelinie and Todd McFarlane bring in Venom proper in #300, from May 1988, when the rejected symbiote finds in Brock someone with the same grudge. He was so successful that he soon stopped being just a villain.

## On screen

### Live action

Topher Grace is Eddie Brock in [Spider-Man 3](/marvel/spider-man-3/), from 2007, a much-debated take.

Tom Hardy leads the Sony trilogy, which goes for humor: [Venom](/marvel/venom/), [Venom: Let There Be Carnage](/marvel/venom-habra-matanza/) and [Venom: The Last Dance](/marvel/venom-el-ultimo-baile/). ||Hardy peeks into the Marvel universe in the post-credits scene of Spider-Man: No Way Home.||

### Animation

He has been a recurring villain in series such as [Spider-Man Unlimited](/marvel/spider-man-unlimited/) and [The Spectacular Spider-Man](/marvel/el-espectacular-spider-man/), where Ben Diskin voices him, and he appears in [Ultimate Spider-Man](/marvel/ultimate-spider-man/).
