Happy Hogan is a former boxer who works as [Tony Stark](/marvel/personajes/iron-man/)'s driver and bodyguard. He complains about everything, but he is one of the few who stick around when things go wrong, and his loyalty offsets his boss's self-absorption.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee, Robert Bernstein and Don Heck create him for *Tales of Suspense* #45, from September 1963. He is a retired boxer whom Tony hires after he saves his life. For years he forms a love triangle with him and [Pepper Potts](/marvel/personajes/pepper-potts/), whom he eventually marries.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Jon Favreau, director of [Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-2008/) and [Iron Man 2](/marvel/iron-man-2/), keeps this role for himself and goes on playing it after leaving the director's chair. ||In Iron Man 3 he is badly hurt in a bombing.|| [Spider-Man: Homecoming](/marvel/spider-man-homecoming/) makes him Peter Parker's reluctant babysitter, which carries on in [Spider-Man: Far From Home](/marvel/spider-man-lejos-de-casa/), where he grows closer to [Aunt May](/marvel/personajes/tia-may/), and in [Spider-Man: No Way Home](/marvel/spider-man-no-way-home/). He also has a comic appearance in [Deadpool & Wolverine](/marvel/deadpool-y-wolverine/).

### Animation

Favreau voices him in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
