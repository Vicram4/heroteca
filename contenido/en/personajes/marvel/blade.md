Blade hunts vampires and is part vampire himself, so he can bear the sun and hates what he carries inside. He is serious, terse and deadly with a sword. Outside the comics he is remembered above all for something else: his film was one of Marvel's first big successes in cinema.

## Comics origin

Marv Wolfman and Gene Colan create him for *The Tomb of Dracula* #10, from July 1973, as a supporting player in a horror title. His mother dies giving birth to him because of a vampire, and that is the root of his mission. It took years for him to get a leading role of his own, and it was the films that made him a front-rank character.

## On screen

### Live action

Wesley Snipes defines him in [Blade](/marvel/blade-1998/), from 1998, and continues in [Blade II](/marvel/blade-ii/), directed by Guillermo del Toro, and [Blade: Trinity](/marvel/blade-trinity/). The rapper Sticky Fingaz plays him in [Blade: The Series](/marvel/blade-la-serie/), from 2006.

### Cinematic Universe

||Mahershala Ali is only heard, as an off-screen voice, in the post-credits scene of Eternals. His solo film spent years in development and Ali eventually left the project. Snipes returned to the role in Deadpool & Wolverine, as a variant from another universe.||

### Animation

He has his own anime, [Blade](/marvel/blade-2011/), from 2011, and Terry Crews voices him in [Hulk and the Agents of S.M.A.S.H.](/marvel/hulk-y-los-agentes-de-smash/).
