Frank Castle has no powers, only an arsenal, a skull on his chest and a single fixed idea. When gang violence takes his wife and children, he resolves to spend the rest of his life wiping out criminals. Other heroes hunt him and sometimes need him, and his best stories leave open whether he is an avenger or a sick man.

## Comics origin

Gerry Conway, John Romita Sr. and Ross Andru create him for *The Amazing Spider-Man* #129, from February 1974, as a mercenary tricked into hunting [Spider-Man](/marvel/personajes/spider-man/). The character caught on so well that he ended up with several titles of his own, especially through the eighties and nineties.

## On screen

### Live action

Dolph Lundgren plays him in [The Punisher](/marvel/the-punisher-1989/), from 1989, without the skull on his chest. Thomas Jane leads another adaptation, [The Punisher](/marvel/the-punisher-2004/), from 2004, and Ray Stevenson takes him to the extreme in the very violent [Punisher: War Zone](/marvel/punisher-war-zone/).

### Cinematic Universe

Jon Bernthal wins everyone over in the [second season of Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil-t2/), and from there moves on to his own series, [The Punisher](/marvel/the-punisher-t1/), more focused on the veteran's trauma. He returns in [Daredevil: Born Again](/marvel/daredevil-born-again-t1/) and jumps to the big screen with [Spider-Man: Brand New Day](/marvel/spider-man-brand-new-day/).

### Animation

He shares the lead with [Black Widow](/marvel/personajes/viuda-negra/) in the anime [Avengers Confidential: Black Widow & Punisher](/marvel/los-vengadores-justicia-y-venganza/).
