Howard Stark is a brilliant inventor and famous businessman, an enormous figure in whose shadow [Tony Stark](/marvel/personajes/iron-man/) grows up. His legacy and absence condition everything, since he is nearly always remembered as a father more attentive to his work than to his son.

## Comics origin

Archie Goodwin and Don Heck introduce him in *Iron Man* #28, from August 1970, in a story exploring Tony's past. He is best remembered for the company he leaves his son and for his death in a car accident.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Two eras get different faces: Dominic Cooper is the young playboy of the forties in [Captain America: The First Avenger](/marvel/capitan-america-el-primer-vengador/) and [Agent Carter](/marvel/agente-carter-t1/), with an almost comic touch, and John Slattery is the mature man of [Iron Man 2](/marvel/iron-man-2/), who leaves a recorded message for his son. ||In Captain America: Civil War it comes out that his accident was actually a murder by the Winter Soldier, and in Avengers: Endgame Tony finds him in 1970 and they finally have an overdue talk.||

### Animation

He has a supporting part in [The Invincible Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-el-invencible/) and returns, voiced by Cooper and Slattery, in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
