Leo Fitz is the Scottish inventor of [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-t1/), so inseparable from [Jemma Simmons](/marvel/personajes/jemma-simmons/) that the team calls them FitzSimmons. He begins as comic relief, shy and full of gadgets, and becomes one of the characters who suffers, and changes, the most in the whole series.

## Comics origin

Like Simmons, he was born on television: Joss Whedon, Jed Whedon and Maurissa Tancharoen create him for the 2013 pilot, and the comics take him up later.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe
