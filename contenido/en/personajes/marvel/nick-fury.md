Nick Fury is a veteran spy who runs S.H.I.E.L.D. and always knows more than he lets on. He has no superpowers, but he handles information and contacts, and he is often the one who talks heroes into working together. His eyepatch and his distrust have become his trademark.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby create him in 1963 as a Second World War sergeant in *Sgt. Fury and his Howling Commandos*. Months later, in *Fantastic Four* #21, he reappears in the present as a CIA agent, and he soon takes charge of S.H.I.E.L.D. In the early 2000s an alternate comics line gave him Samuel L. Jackson's features, years before the actor played the part.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Samuel L. Jackson appears in the post-credits scene of [Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-2008/), and that small cameo becomes the thread that ties the [Avengers](/marvel/wiki/vengadores/) together. He assembles the team in [The Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores/), and [Captain Marvel](/marvel/capitana-marvel/) shows him in the nineties, before he lost the eye. ||In Captain America: The Winter Soldier he fakes his death to escape his own agents.|| Later he leads [Secret Invasion](/marvel/invasion-secreta-t1/), a spy story with an older, wearier Fury.

### Animation

He has had supporting parts since [Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-la-serie-animada/), the animated series, and [X-Men: Evolution](/marvel/x-men-evolucion/). Chi McBride voices him in [Ultimate Spider-Man](/marvel/ultimate-spider-man/) and other shows from that same TV era, and Jackson dubs himself in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
