Ultron is an artificial intelligence created with good intentions who decides that humanity is the problem. He builds himself ever more powerful bodies and is obsessed with his creators. His story is that of a rebellious son with a wish to destroy his father, one of the great nightmares of the [Avengers](/marvel/wiki/vengadores/).

## Comics origin

Roy Thomas and John Buscema introduce him in *The Avengers* #54 and #55, in 1968. There he is created by [Hank Pym](/marvel/personajes/hank-pym/), and soon after Ultron in turn builds [Vision](/marvel/personajes/vision/), in one of the team's best-remembered stories.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

James Spader gives him voice and gestures in [Avengers: Age of Ultron](/marvel/vengadores-la-era-de-ultron/), with a twisted humor nobody expected from a robot. In this version Tony Stark and Bruce Banner create him from the Mind Stone. ||In Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, another universe's Illuminati command an army of Ultron sentries, and in What If...? one version manages to win and lay waste to the universe.||

### Animation

He is a recurring enemy in [The Avengers: United They Stand](/marvel/the-avengers-united-they-stand/) and [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/), and the main villain of [Next Avengers: Heroes of Tomorrow](/marvel/nuevos-vengadores-heroes-del-manana/).
