Michael Morbius is a brilliant scientist with a blood disease that is killing him. His experimental cure saves him but turns him into a being with a thirst for blood, strength and bat-like powers, without being a true vampire. He lives tormented by what he needs in order to survive, and that is where all the character's drama comes from.

## Comics origin

Roy Thomas and Gil Kane create him for *The Amazing Spider-Man* #101, from October 1971, just as the rules forbidding vampires in American comics were relaxed. He served as a Spider-Man enemy and later had his own horror series.

## On screen

### Live action

Jared Leto plays him in [Morbius](/marvel/morbius/), from 2022, a very poorly received Sony film.
