J. Jonah Jameson runs the Daily Bugle and devotes his front pages to painting Spider-Man as a public danger, all while buying photos of the hero from Peter Parker without knowing who he is. He shouts, he rages and he looks like a caricature, but he also has principles as a newspaperman, and that contradiction is what makes him so entertaining.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Steve Ditko create him for *The Amazing Spider-Man* #1, from March 1963. He is Peter's boss for decades. Lee admitted to basing the character partly on himself.

## On screen

### Live action

The first to play him are David White and Robert F. Simon, in the Nicholas Hammond era. J.K. Simmons gives him his definitive form in [Spider-Man](/marvel/spider-man-2002/) and its sequels, in a performance so celebrated that many consider it unbeatable.

### Cinematic Universe

||Simmons makes a surprise return in the post-credits scene of Spider-Man: Far From Home, now the host of an online news channel, and reveals Spider-Man's identity to the world.|| He takes up that role again in [Spider-Man: No Way Home](/marvel/spider-man-no-way-home/).

### Animation

He is a regular voice in the hero's series, from [Spider-Man](/marvel/spider-man-1967/) to [The Spectacular Spider-Man](/marvel/el-espectacular-spider-man/). Simmons also voices him in [Ultimate Spider-Man](/marvel/ultimate-spider-man/) and in [Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse](/marvel/spider-man-cruzando-el-multiverso/).
