Peggy Carter is a British agent who needs no serum to be the most intimidating person in the room. In the war she meets Steve Rogers and sees him for who he is, before anyone else notices him. She then pursues her career, making her way through a sexist environment until she helps found S.H.I.E.L.D.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby create her for *Tales of Suspense* #75, from March 1966, without even a name, as a wartime love of [Captain America](/marvel/personajes/capitan-america/) barely seen in a single panel. For decades she remains a background figure, often tied to [Sharon Carter](/marvel/personajes/sharon-carter/). It was film that gave her the prominence she has today.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Hayley Atwell has played her since [Captain America: The First Avenger](/marvel/capitan-america-el-primer-vengador/). The short [Marvel One-Shot: Agent Carter](/marvel/agente-carter/) went over so well that it led to the series [Agent Carter](/marvel/agente-carter-t1/), set in the forties. She also appears in [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-t2/), and is seen as an old woman in [Captain America: The Winter Soldier](/marvel/capitan-america-el-soldado-de-invierno/). ||Avengers: Endgame finally grants her the dance she was owed with Steve.|| ||In Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, Atwell is the Captain Carter of another universe.||

### Animation

Atwell leads the first episode of [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/), which imagines Peggy receiving the serum instead of Steve.
