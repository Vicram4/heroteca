Sylvie is a version of [Loki](/marvel/personajes/loki/) born in another timeline, whose life was erased by the Time Variance Authority when she was a child. She has spent centuries running through the ends of worlds, and that life on the run has made her tough, wary and very quick with her tricks. Her story with Loki is a game of mirrors, because they understand each other all too well.

## Comics origin

Sylvie was born in the series, although she draws on two figures from the comics: Lady Loki and Sylvie Lushton, an Enchantress created by Loki who appears in *Dark Reign: Young Avengers* #1, from July 2009. The screen version reaches the comics later, in the *TVA* title from 2024.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Sophia Di Martino plays her in the [first season of Loki](/marvel/loki-t1/), where she is revealed as the variant the TVA is chasing. ||In the last episode she kills He Who Remains despite Loki's pleas, unleashing the multiverse.|| In the [second season](/marvel/loki-t2/) she tries to lead a normal life, far from what she has caused, until the consequences catch up with her.
