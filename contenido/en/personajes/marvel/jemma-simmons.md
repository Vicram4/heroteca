Jemma Simmons is the scientific brain of [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-t1/) along with [Leo Fitz](/marvel/personajes/leo-fitz/). She starts out as the show's enthusiastic, charming whiz kid, and over time she grows much tougher and capable of terrible decisions, without ever ceasing to be kind.

## Comics origin

She was born on television: Joss Whedon, Jed Whedon and Maurissa Tancharoen create her for the 2013 pilot. The success of her pairing with Fitz later takes her into the comics, where Mark Waid and Carlos Pacheco bring her in through *S.H.I.E.L.D.* #1, from the 2015 series.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Elizabeth Henstridge plays her across the seven seasons of the series and in the short [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.: Slingshot](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-slingshot/). ||In the third season she spends months stranded on a distant planet. Her love story with Fitz survives distance, death and time, to the point that much of the show's ending revolves around them.||
