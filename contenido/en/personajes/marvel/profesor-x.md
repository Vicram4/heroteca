Charles Xavier is a prodigious telepath in a wheelchair who opens a school for young mutants and leads the X-Men, convinced that humans and mutants can live together. His dream clashes with that of his old friend [Magneto](/marvel/personajes/magneto/). He is a wise teacher and also someone who has hidden things from his students for their own good.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby create him for *The X-Men* #1, from September 1963, as leader and tutor of the first five students. His relationship with Magneto, partly inspired by the civil rights debate, grows over the years to become the core of the franchise.

## On screen

### Live action

Patrick Stewart seems made for the part in [X-Men](/marvel/x-men/) and its sequels. James McAvoy plays him young in [X-Men: First Class](/marvel/x-men-primera-generacion/), more frivolous at first, and they share [X-Men: Days of Future Past](/marvel/x-men-dias-del-futuro-pasado/). ||Stewart gives a devastating farewell in Logan, as an elderly Charles who has lost control of his mind, and returns as a member of the Illuminati in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness.|| Harry Lloyd is the young version in [Legion](/marvel/legion/), where Charles is the lead's father.

### Animation

He is the team's mentor in [X-Men](/marvel/x-men-la-serie-animada/), the animated series, and in [X-Men: Evolution](/marvel/x-men-evolucion/) he runs a school full of teenagers.
