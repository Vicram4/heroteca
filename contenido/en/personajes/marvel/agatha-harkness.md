Agatha Harkness is a witch with several centuries behind her and a very sharp sense of humor. Her specialty is knowing more magic than anyone and using that knowledge to teach or to deceive, as it suits her. On screen she has become one of Marvel's most charismatic villains.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby create her for *Fantastic Four* #94, from January 1970, as Franklin Richards's elderly nanny. Later she mentors [Wanda Maximoff](/marvel/personajes/bruja-escarlata/), teaching her to control her powers, a relationship the screen turns upside down.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Kathryn Hahn is the nosy neighbor of [WandaVision](/marvel/wandavision-t1/), a role that lets her steal the series. ||In the end she turns out to be a witch who has manipulated everything, in the famous song "Agatha All Along".|| She leads her own series, [Agatha All Along](/marvel/agatha-quien-si-no-t1/), a trip down the Witches' Road with a group of hopefuls and Aubrey Plaza as her great antagonist.

### Animation

Hahn voices her in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/), and Pauline Newstone in [X-Men: Evolution](/marvel/x-men-evolucion/).
