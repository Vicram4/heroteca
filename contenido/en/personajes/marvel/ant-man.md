Scott Lang is a thief with more talent than luck, and his motives nearly always involve his daughter Cassie. He inherits a suit that can shrink him to the size of an insect and let him talk to ants, an absurd idea his stories make the most of. Among so many solemn heroes, he is the regular guy who cannot believe what is happening to him.

## Comics origin

David Michelinie, Bob Layton and John Byrne create him in the late seventies. He appears in *The Avengers* #181 and soon after in *Marvel Premiere* #47, from April 1979, where he steals the suit from the first Ant-Man, [Hank Pym](/marvel/personajes/hank-pym/), to save his sick daughter. Pym himself, rather than turning him in, lets him keep using it.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Paul Rudd has played him since [Ant-Man](/marvel/ant-man-2015/), a heist comedy in which Pym recruits him as an apprentice. He joins the fight in [Captain America: Civil War](/marvel/capitan-america-civil-war/) and returns with [Hope van Dyne](/marvel/personajes/avispa/) in [Ant-Man and the Wasp](/marvel/ant-man-y-la-avispa/). ||In Avengers: Endgame he escapes the Quantum Realm after five years and comes up with the idea that makes time travel possible.|| [Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania](/marvel/ant-man-y-la-avispa-quantumania/) returns him to that same realm and pits him against [Kang](/marvel/personajes/kang/).

### Animation

He has his own shorts in [Ant-Man](/marvel/ant-man-2017/), from 2017, and turns up in team series such as [Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores-unidos/).
