Phil Coulson is the friendly face of S.H.I.E.L.D.: discreet, polite and able to treat gods and geniuses with a civil servant's patience. He is an avowed Captain America fan, and precisely because there is nothing extraordinary about him he becomes the human heart of the first stretch of the Cinematic Universe.

## Comics origin

He is one of the rare cases where film comes first: he was created for the 2008 movie and does not reach the comics until *Battle Scars* #6, from April 2012. In that story he is a Ranger who serves with Nick Fury's son.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Clark Gregg has played him since [Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-2008/), and his part grows in [Iron Man 2](/marvel/iron-man-2/) and [Thor](/marvel/thor/). ||In The Avengers he dies at Loki's hands, and that loss is what persuades the team to come together.|| The series [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-t1/) brings him back as the lead and explains how he survived. [Captain Marvel](/marvel/capitana-marvel/) shows him as a rookie in the nineties.

### Animation

Gregg keeps voicing him in [Ultimate Spider-Man](/marvel/ultimate-spider-man/), where he runs the young team of heroes, and in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
