Jessica Jones hung up her costume a while ago and works as a private detective in New York, between sordid cases and too much alcohol. Her powers hardly matter. What her stories are about is how someone carries on after a man with mind control has wrecked her life.

## Comics origin

Brian Michael Bendis and Michael Gaydos create her for *Alias* #1, from November 2001, in an adult imprint. She was later retroactively placed as a classmate of Peter Parker. She marries [Luke Cage](/marvel/personajes/luke-cage/) and together they form one of the most solid families in Marvel comics.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Krysten Ritter plays her in [Jessica Jones](/marvel/jessica-jones-t1/), one of the most respected series of the Netflix era, thanks above all to its first season and David Tennant's Kilgrave. She is part of [The Defenders](/marvel/the-defenders-t1/) and returns years later in [Daredevil: Born Again](/marvel/daredevil-born-again-t2/), now raising a daughter.
