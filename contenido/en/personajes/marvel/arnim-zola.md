Arnim Zola is a Nazi scientist who finds a way to stay alive after losing his body. He is no fighter: his danger lies in what he invents, what he remembers and the patience with which he lays his plans, sometimes over decades. Few Marvel villains look as strange as he does in the comics.

## Comics origin

Jack Kirby creates him for *Captain America and the Falcon* #208, from April 1977, with an unforgettable look: a headless robot body and a face on a screen set in its chest. He is an expert in cloning who builds monsters and copies, one of them tied to [Red Skull](/marvel/personajes/red-skull/).

## On screen

### Live action

Peter Haworth plays a doctor named Arnim Zolo in [Nick Fury: Agent of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/nick-furia-agente-de-shield/), from 1998.

### Cinematic Universe

Toby Jones is Red Skull's nervous scientist in [Captain America: The First Avenger](/marvel/capitan-america-el-primer-vengador/). ||He reappears in Captain America: The Winter Soldier, as a mind stored in old computers, to reveal that Hydra has spent decades embedded in S.H.I.E.L.D.|| He also appears in [Agent Carter](/marvel/agente-carter-t1/).

### Animation

Mark Hamill voices him in [Ultimate Spider-Man](/marvel/ultimate-spider-man/), and Jones returns in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
