Nebula grew up beside [Gamora](/marvel/personajes/gamora/) under the rule of Thanos, who forced her to fight her sister and replaced parts of her body every time she lost. She starts out ready to take revenge on everyone, and her evolution is among the most carefully built in the Marvel films: from bitter villain to survivor who earns a place among the others.

## Comics origin

Roger Stern and John Buscema create her for *The Avengers* #257, from July 1985, as a space pirate claiming to be Thanos's granddaughter. In *The Infinity Gauntlet* she even gets hold of the [Infinity Stones](/marvel/wiki/gemas-del-infinito/).

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Karen Gillan has played her since [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2014/), as a secondary villain. [Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-vol-2/) gives her more depth through her relationship with her sister, and [Avengers: Infinity War](/marvel/vengadores-infinity-war/) sets her against her father. ||In Avengers: Endgame, her 2014 self is exactly what allows Thanos to travel through time, and the adult Nebula has to do away with her past self.||

### Animation

She has minor roles in the series [Silver Surfer](/marvel/silver-surfer/) and in LEGO productions such as [LEGO Marvel Super Heroes: Guardians of the Galaxy - The Thanos Threat](/marvel/lego-guardianes-de-la-galaxia-la-amenaza-de-thanos/). Gillan voices her again in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
