Rogue cannot touch anyone. Her skin absorbs other people's strength, memories and abilities, and prolonged contact could kill them. That loneliness is the foundation of the character, a young woman from the American South with plenty of swagger on the outside and constant fear within.

## Comics origin

Chris Claremont and Michael Golden introduce her in *The Avengers Annual* #10, from October 1981, as a villain under [Mystique](/marvel/personajes/mistica/), her adoptive mother. There she permanently steals the powers of [Carol Danvers](/marvel/personajes/capitana-marvel/). Soon after, frightened by what she has done, she asks the X-Men for help and stays with them.

## On screen

### X-Men saga

Anna Paquin is a runaway teenager called Marie in [X-Men](/marvel/x-men/), seeking the protection of [Wolverine](/marvel/personajes/lobezno/), and continues in [X2](/marvel/x-men-2/). ||In X-Men: The Last Stand she considers taking the cure so she can touch people.|| Her role in [X-Men: Days of Future Past](/marvel/x-men-dias-del-futuro-pasado/) is close to a cameo in the theatrical cut, and grows in the extended version.

### Animation

Lenore Zann voices her in the 1992 series [X-Men](/marvel/x-men-la-serie-animada/), where she is one of the team's most popular members, and returns in [X-Men '97](/marvel/x-men-97/). In [X-Men: Evolution](/marvel/x-men-evolucion/), Meghan Black plays her as a goth girl.
