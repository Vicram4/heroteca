Karen Page has spent years as [Daredevil](/marvel/personajes/daredevil/)'s great love and is much more than that. She is stubborn, has a past she prefers not to talk about and stops at nothing once she smells injustice. She has suffered a lot in nearly every version, and that is exactly why she is so hard to break.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Bill Everett create her for *Daredevil* #1, from April 1964, as secretary to Matt and [Foggy](/marvel/personajes/foggy-nelson/). For years she is little more than the love interest, until Frank Miller brings her back in *Born Again*, from *Daredevil* #227, February 1986, ruined by drugs and capable of selling Matt's identity. That story makes her a real character.

## On screen

### Live action

Ellen Pompeo has a small part in the film [Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil/), from 2003.

### Cinematic Universe

Deborah Ann Woll plays her in the series [Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil-t1/), first as the victim of a fraud and then as a journalist set on going after [Wilson Fisk](/marvel/personajes/kingpin/). ||At the end of the first season she kills James Wesley, a secret that haunts her for years.|| She also appears in [The Defenders](/marvel/the-defenders-t1/) and strikes up an odd friendship with [Frank Castle](/marvel/personajes/punisher/) in [The Punisher](/marvel/the-punisher-t1/). She returns in [Daredevil: Born Again](/marvel/daredevil-born-again-t1/).
