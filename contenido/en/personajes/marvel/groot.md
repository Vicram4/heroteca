Groot is an alien tree who utters only three words, "I am Groot", and still manages to say everything. He can grow, regenerate and run enemies through with his branches, but what stands out is his kindness. Nobody understands him like [Rocket](/marvel/personajes/rocket/), and their friendship is one of the most beloved among the Guardians.

## Comics origin

He is born in *Tales to Astonish* #13, from November 1960, the work of Stan Lee, Larry Lieber and Jack Kirby, before Marvel's great superheroes. That Groot was an invading monster who spoke perfectly normally and wanted to carry off some humans to experiment on them. His present personality emerges in the cosmic adventures of the 2000s.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Vin Diesel voices him in [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2014/) with little more than those three words. ||At the end of that film he sacrifices himself to protect the others, and one of his shoots grows back as a new Groot.|| [Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-vol-2/) gets plenty out of that adorable baby, who by [Avengers: Infinity War](/marvel/vengadores-infinity-war/) is a sulky teenager. The series of shorts [I Am Groot](/marvel/yo-soy-groot-t1/) follows his mischief.

### Animation

Kevin Michael Richardson lends him his voice in [Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores-unidos/) and [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2015/), and he pairs up with Rocket in [Rocket & Groot](/marvel/rocket-y-groot/).
