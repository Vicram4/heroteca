Madame Web is an elderly clairvoyant, blind and ill, who advises the spider heroes from a chair attached to a great web-like machine. Her power is not fighting but seeing what is going to happen. She appears rarely, but when she does she tends to change the course of a story.

## Comics origin

Dennis O'Neil and John Romita Jr. create her for *The Amazing Spider-Man* #210, from November 1980. Her illness forces her to depend on that equipment to live. Later she has been a key piece in several spider-universe crossovers.

## On screen

### Live action

Dakota Johnson plays her in [Madame Web](/marvel/madame-web/), from 2024, a Sony film that turns her into a young New York paramedic in 2003, tasked with protecting three girls. It was very poorly received by critics and audiences.

### Animation

Cree Summer voices her in [Ultimate Spider-Man](/marvel/ultimate-spider-man/).
