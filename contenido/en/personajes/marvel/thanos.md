Thanos is a Titan of colossal strength and terrifying calm, convinced that his crimes make sense. He wants to gather the [Infinity Stones](/marvel/wiki/gemas-del-infinito/) to remake the universe as he sees fit. The frightening part is not only his power but that he sincerely believes he is doing the right thing.

## Comics origin

Jim Starlin creates him for *The Invincible Iron Man* #55, from February 1973, and makes him the great villain of his cosmic sagas. His motive there differs from the films: he kills to win over Death herself. *The Infinity Gauntlet*, from 1991, is his most celebrated story.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Josh Brolin has played him since [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2014/), where he is barely a threat in the background, and after a long wait he takes center stage in [Avengers: Infinity War](/marvel/vengadores-infinity-war/). It is unusual for the villain to end up, in a way, as the heart of the film. ||With a snap of his fingers he erases one in every two living beings, and in Avengers: Endgame a version from the past returns to try again.||

### Animation

Before the films he was already the rival of [Silver Surfer](/marvel/silver-surfer/) in the 1998 series. He later appears in LEGO productions such as [LEGO Marvel Super Heroes: Guardians of the Galaxy - The Thanos Threat](/marvel/lego-guardianes-de-la-galaxia-la-amenaza-de-thanos/), with a far lighter tone, and Brolin voices him in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
