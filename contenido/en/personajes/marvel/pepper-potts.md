Virginia "Pepper" Potts starts out as [Tony Stark](/marvel/personajes/iron-man/)'s efficient secretary and ends up running his company. She has almost endless patience and a judgment he does not always reach. Her relationship with Tony is the human counterpoint to the armor stories.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee, Robert Bernstein and Don Heck create her for *Tales of Suspense* #45, from September 1963, alongside Happy Hogan, whom she would eventually marry. She remains a supporting character for a long time until, in 2009, she dons her own armor as Rescue.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Gwyneth Paltrow has played her since [Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-2008/), with a chemistry with Robert Downey Jr. that holds up much of the films. In [Iron Man 2](/marvel/iron-man-2/) she rises to chief executive, and [Iron Man 3](/marvel/iron-man-3/) puts her at the center of the action. ||In Avengers: Endgame she returns for the final battle in the Rescue armor, and stays with Tony in his last moments.||

### Animation

She appears in [The Invincible Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-el-invencible/) and is Tony's classmate in the teen series [Iron Man: Armored Adventures](/marvel/iron-man-aventuras-de-hierro/).
