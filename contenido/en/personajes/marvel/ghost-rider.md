Johnny Blaze makes a deal with the devil to save someone he loves and pays with a curse: at night he turns into a flaming skeleton on an infernal motorcycle. He punishes the guilty with his penance stare, and his story has a touch of horror few Marvel heroes allow themselves.

## Comics origin

Gary Friedrich, Roy Thomas and Mike Ploog create him for *Marvel Spotlight* #5, from August 1972, at a time when Marvel was exploring horror again. It is eventually revealed that a demon called Zarathos lives inside him, and other characters later inherit the name, such as Danny Ketch and Robbie Reyes.

## On screen

### Live action

Nicolas Cage, a big fan of the character, plays him in [Ghost Rider](/marvel/ghost-rider-el-motorista-fantasma/), from 2007, and in the far wilder sequel, [Ghost Rider: Spirit of Vengeance](/marvel/ghost-rider-espiritu-de-venganza/).

### Cinematic Universe

In the [fourth season of Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-t4/), Gabriel Luna plays another Ghost Rider, Robbie Reyes, with a car instead of a motorcycle. He is a different person, although the listings group him on this page.

### Animation

Fred Tatasciore voices him in [LEGO Marvel Avengers: Mission Demolition](/marvel/lego-marvel-avengers-mision-demolicion/).
