Janet van Dyne is the first Wasp and one of the pillars of the early Avengers. In the comics she is a wealthy young woman, a fashion designer, and far more capable and tougher than others assume. On film she is the missing mother who takes the Pym family's story to a whole new world.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee, Ernie Hart and Jack Kirby create her for *Tales to Astonish* #44, from June 1963, as a partner for [Hank Pym](/marvel/personajes/hank-pym/). She is there from the start of the Avengers, in the title's first issue, and is the one who gives the team its name. In time she leads it and becomes one of its most respected leaders.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Hayley Lovitt plays her in a flashback in [Ant-Man](/marvel/ant-man-2015/). Michelle Pfeiffer takes over in [Ant-Man and the Wasp](/marvel/ant-man-y-la-avispa/), where her family sets out to rescue her from the Quantum Realm. ||She finally gets out after thirty years, and Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania reveals what she never said about her life there and her connection to Kang.||

### Animation

She has a large presence in the anime [Marvel Disk Wars: The Avengers](/marvel/marvel-disk-wars-avengers/) and in [Future Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores-del-futuro/). Other series where the heroine is really Janet appear on the page of [Hope van Dyne](/marvel/personajes/avispa/), because the listings group them under that name.
