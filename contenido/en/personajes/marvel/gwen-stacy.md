Gwen Stacy is Peter Parker's smart, spirited college girlfriend. Her place in comics history owes much to how she ended in her original version, a moment that changed what readers expected of superheroes. More recent versions have given her a life of her own, and today many see her as a heroine in her own right.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Steve Ditko introduce her in *The Amazing Spider-Man* #31, from December 1965. ||In 1973 she dies in issues 121 and 122, after the Green Goblin throws her from a bridge.|| In 2014, *Edge of Spider-Verse* #2 presents a Gwen from another world who is the one bitten by the spider, a version that became a huge success.

## On screen

### Live action

Bryce Dallas Howard plays her in [Spider-Man 3](/marvel/spider-man-3/), as a model and romantic rival of Mary Jane. Emma Stone has a major role in [The Amazing Spider-Man](/marvel/the-amazing-spider-man/) and [The Amazing Spider-Man 2](/marvel/the-amazing-spider-man-2-el-poder-de-electro/), with a chemistry with Andrew Garfield that is what people like most about these films. ||The second film brings about her death, in a scene faithful to the comic.||

### Animation

Hailee Steinfeld voices her as Spider-Gwen in [Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse](/marvel/spider-man-un-nuevo-universo/) and [Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse](/marvel/spider-man-cruzando-el-multiverso/), which gives her its spectacular opening. She is also one of the child leads of [Spidey and His Amazing Friends](/marvel/spidey-y-su-superequipo/).
