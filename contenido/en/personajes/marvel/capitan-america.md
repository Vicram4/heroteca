Steve Rogers is a Brooklyn kid the army turns down again and again until he agrees to try an experimental serum. His body changes overnight, but what defines him came before: his stubbornness and his inability to look the other way. His shield and uniform stand for a country he does not always agree with, as many stories point out.

## Comics origin

Joe Simon and Jack Kirby create him for *Captain America Comics* #1, on sale in December 1940, with a cover showing him punching Hitler before the United States had entered the war. After years of ups and downs, Stan Lee and Kirby revive him in 1964 in *The Avengers* #4: he has spent decades frozen, and he returns as a man out of his time who joins the [Avengers](/marvel/wiki/vengadores/).

## On screen

### Early adaptations

The 1944 serial [Captain America](/marvel/capitan-america-1944/), with Dick Purcell, borrows little beyond the name: its hero is a district attorney called Grant Gardner. Reb Brown plays him in two TV films, [Captain America](/marvel/capitan-america-1979/) and [Captain America II: Death Too Soon](/marvel/capitan-america-ii-muerte-demasiado-pronto/), motorcycle included, and Matt Salinger stars in the modest [1990](/marvel/capitan-america-1990/) film.

### Cinematic Universe

Chris Evans has played Steve since [Captain America: The First Avenger](/marvel/capitan-america-el-primer-vengador/), which takes him to the Second World War. [Captain America: The Winter Soldier](/marvel/capitan-america-el-soldado-de-invierno/) pits him against a conspiracy from within, and [Captain America: Civil War](/marvel/capitan-america-civil-war/) sets him against Tony Stark. ||At the end of Avengers: Endgame he goes back in time to live with Peggy Carter and, by then an old man, hands the shield to Sam Wilson.|| That is why, when the mantle continues in [The Falcon and the Winter Soldier](/marvel/the-falcon-and-the-winter-soldier-t1/), it falls to [Sam Wilson](/marvel/personajes/falcon/).

### Animation

He shows up in [The Avengers: United They Stand](/marvel/the-avengers-united-they-stand/) and is a central figure in [Ultimate Avengers: The Movie](/marvel/ultimate-avengers/). Other films pair him with Tony Stark, such as [Iron Man & Captain America: Heroes United](/marvel/iron-man-y-capitan-america-heroes-unidos/).
