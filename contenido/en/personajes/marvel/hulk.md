Bruce Banner is a brilliant, withdrawn scientist who, after absorbing a massive dose of gamma radiation, turns into a giant whenever he loses his temper. The strength matters less than the cohabitation: Banner and Hulk need and loathe each other in equal measure, and nearly every version asks whether that relationship can ever be mended.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby introduce him in the first issue of *The Incredible Hulk*, in May 1962. That Hulk was gray, a shade Lee picked because he found it unsettling; printing trouble helped push him quickly toward the green everyone now knows.

## On screen

### Live action

Bill Bixby and Lou Ferrigno share the role across two bodies in [The Incredible Hulk](/marvel/el-increible-hulk-1977-serie/), the late-seventies series in which the lead is called David and drifts from town to town running from himself. The story carries on through TV films such as [The Incredible Hulk Returns](/marvel/el-regreso-del-increible-hulk/) and [The Death of the Incredible Hulk](/marvel/la-muerte-del-increible-hulk/).

On the big screen, Eric Bana stars in Ang Lee's [Hulk](/marvel/hulk/), a family drama that treats the character's trauma with great seriousness. Edward Norton then leads [The Incredible Hulk](/marvel/el-increible-hulk-2008/), which is already part of the Cinematic Universe.

### Cinematic Universe

Mark Ruffalo takes over in [The Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores/) with a more ironic, resigned Banner. [Thor: Ragnarok](/marvel/thor-ragnarok/) sends him to a faraway planet as a gladiator, and later he turns up in [She-Hulk: Attorney at Law](/marvel/she-hulk-abogada-hulka-t1/), alongside his cousin, and in [Spider-Man: Brand New Day](/marvel/spider-man-brand-new-day/). ||In Avengers: Endgame, Banner and Hulk finally make peace and share one body, with the mind of one and the muscle of the other.||

### Animation

He has had series since the sixties, including [The Marvel Super Heroes](/marvel/the-marvel-super-heroes/) and the 1982 [The Incredible Hulk](/marvel/el-increible-hulk-1982/). His animated films include [Planet Hulk](/marvel/planet-hulk/), which exiles him to space and makes him an unlikely ruler, while [Hulk and the Agents of S.M.A.S.H.](/marvel/hulk-y-los-agentes-de-smash/) goes for teen comedy.
