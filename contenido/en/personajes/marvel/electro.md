Max Dillon was an ordinary electrician until lightning turned him into a human generator. He can throw bolts, travel through cables and black out a city, but he rarely knows what to do with so much power. That is why he tends to end up as a high-end supporting player in [Spider-Man](/marvel/personajes/spider-man/)'s fights.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Steve Ditko create him for *The Amazing Spider-Man* #9, from February 1964. He turns to ordinary crime with the help of his powers and is part of the Sinister Six, the hero's most famous group of enemies.

## On screen

### Live action

Jamie Foxx plays him in [The Amazing Spider-Man 2](/marvel/the-amazing-spider-man-2-el-poder-de-electro/) as an Oscorp technician, ignored and obsessed with the hero. ||He returns in Spider-Man: No Way Home, arriving from another reality and looking more restrained, without the blue skin.||

### Animation

Ethan Embry voices him in [Spider-Man: The New Animated Series](/marvel/spider-man-la-nueva-serie-animada/). [Spidey and His Amazing Friends](/marvel/spidey-y-su-superequipo/) turns the character into a villainess for children, voiced by Stephanie Lemelin.
