Mantis can sense and manipulate the emotions of anyone she touches, but she barely understands social interaction, which is where much of her humor comes from. Raised far from everyone, she discovers with the Guardians of the Galaxy what it means to have friends.

## Comics origin

Steve Englehart and Don Heck create her for *The Avengers* #112, from June 1973. She is half Vietnamese, half German, trained in martial arts by monks, and turns out to be the "Celestial Madonna" of a prophecy. The comics version has little in common with the one in the films.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Pom Klementieff debuts in [Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-vol-2/) as a servant of Ego, whom she ends up betraying to join the group. She continues in [Avengers: Infinity War](/marvel/vengadores-infinity-war/), [Avengers: Endgame](/marvel/vengadores-endgame/) and [Thor: Love and Thunder](/marvel/thor-love-and-thunder/). ||In The Guardians of the Galaxy Holiday Special she tells Peter Quill that she is his half-sister, and in the third volume she decides to leave and find her own way.||

### Animation

Jennifer Hale voices her in the special [LEGO Marvel Super Heroes: Guardians of the Galaxy - The Thanos Threat](/marvel/lego-guardianes-de-la-galaxia-la-amenaza-de-thanos/).
