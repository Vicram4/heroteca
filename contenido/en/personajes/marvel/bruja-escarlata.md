Wanda Maximoff has a power capable of altering reality, and her struggle to control it is tangled up with a life full of losses. She always wavers between heroine and threat, depending on what is taken from her. With her twin brother, [Quicksilver](/marvel/personajes/mercurio/), she is one of those characters others fear as much as they need.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby introduce her with her brother in *The X-Men* #4, from March 1964, as reluctant members of [Magneto](/marvel/personajes/magneto/)'s Brotherhood of Evil Mutants. In 1965 they switch sides and join the [Avengers](/marvel/wiki/vengadores/). For a long time they were presented as Magneto's own children, a detail later changed.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Elizabeth Olsen first appears in a scene of [Captain America: The Winter Soldier](/marvel/capitan-america-el-soldado-de-invierno/) and joins the main cast in [Avengers: Age of Ultron](/marvel/vengadores-la-era-de-ultron/). [WandaVision](/marvel/wandavision-t1/) is a turning point, blending TV comedy with grief. ||There we learn she has created an entire reality to avoid accepting Vision's death, and in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness that pain turns her into the film's villain.||

### Animation

She appears in [X-Men](/marvel/x-men-la-serie-animada/) and [Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-la-serie-animada/), both animated series, and is part of the team in [The Avengers: United They Stand](/marvel/the-avengers-united-they-stand/). In [X-Men: Evolution](/marvel/x-men-evolucion/) she is a troubled, dangerous teenager.
