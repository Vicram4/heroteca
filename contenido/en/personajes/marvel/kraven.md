Sergei Kravinoff is a Russian aristocrat who hunts out of pride, with a code of honor all his own and a lion's-mane vest. To him, [Spider-Man](/marvel/personajes/spider-man/) is no ordinary enemy but the ultimate proof that he is still the best. He is a villain who wants to prove something as much as to win.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Steve Ditko create him for *The Amazing Spider-Man* #15, from August 1964. For years he is a minor villain, until *Kraven's Last Hunt*, by J. M. DeMatteis and Mike Zeck, published in 1987, makes him the lead of one of the finest Spider-Man stories.

## On screen

### Live action

Aaron Taylor-Johnson plays him in [Kraven the Hunter](/marvel/kraven-the-hunter/), from 2024, a Sony film focused on his origins that paints him as a brutal antihero.

### Animation

Robert Ridgely voices him in [Spider-Man and His Amazing Friends](/marvel/spider-man-y-sus-sorprendentes-amigos/), Michael Dorn in [Spider-Man: The New Animated Series](/marvel/spider-man-la-nueva-serie-animada/), Eric Vesbit in [The Spectacular Spider-Man](/marvel/el-espectacular-spider-man/) and Diedrich Bader in [Ultimate Spider-Man](/marvel/ultimate-spider-man/).
