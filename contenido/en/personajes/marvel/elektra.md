Elektra is a Greek assassin trained by the finest schools of violence, armed with sais and without much remorse. She was [Matt Murdock](/marvel/personajes/daredevil/)'s first love in college and is the person who unsettles him most. Tragic and cold in equal measure, she embodies what Matt could have been.

## Comics origin

Frank Miller creates her for *Daredevil* #168, from January 1981, during his celebrated run on the title. ||Her death at the hands of Bullseye in issue 181 is one of the most shocking moments in eighties comics, although, as usually happens, she ends up coming back.||

## On screen

### Live action

Jennifer Garner plays her in [Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil/), from 2003, and leads her own film, [Elektra](/marvel/elektra/), from 2005, to a rather chilly reception.

### Cinematic Universe

Élodie Yung arrives in the [second season of Daredevil](/marvel/daredevil-t2/), in a seductive, dangerous version, and continues in [The Defenders](/marvel/the-defenders-t1/). ||There she returns from the dead as a weapon of the Hand. Garner, for her part, returns to the role in Deadpool & Wolverine, as a variant from another universe.||
