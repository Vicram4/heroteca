Jennifer Walters is a lawyer who, after a blood transfusion from [Bruce Banner](/marvel/personajes/hulk/), turns green, very tall and very strong. Unlike her cousin, she keeps a cool head and even likes her new form. Her best stories are comedies, often breaking the fourth wall.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and John Buscema create her for *The Savage She-Hulk* #1, from late 1979, partly to protect the name before the Hulk TV series could exploit it. She later joins the Avengers and the Fantastic Four, and in the eighties John Byrne gives her the comic, metafictional tone that defines her today.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Tatiana Maslany plays her in [She-Hulk: Attorney at Law](/marvel/she-hulk-abogada-hulka-t1/), a courtroom comedy in which Jennifer talks to the camera and even argues with the people making the show.

### Animation

Rebecca Shoichet voices her in [Fantastic Four: World's Greatest Heroes](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-2006/), from 2006, and Eliza Dushku in [Hulk and the Agents of S.M.A.S.H.](/marvel/hulk-y-los-agentes-de-smash/).
