Kate Bishop has money, excellent aim and no intention of staying on the sidelines. With no powers of any kind, she earns her place among the heroes through stubbornness and cheek. The best part of her stories is her relationship with [Clint Barton](/marvel/personajes/ojo-de-halcon/), halfway between admiration and constant bickering.

## Comics origin

Allan Heinberg and Jim Cheung create her for *Young Avengers* #1, from April 2005, where she takes the name Hawkeye. The *Hawkeye* title by Matt Fraction and David Aja, from 2012, shares the spotlight between her and Clint, and has done the most to shape how the character is seen today.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Hailee Steinfeld plays her in [Hawkeye](/marvel/ojo-de-halcon-t1/), a Christmas series inspired by that stretch of the comics, where she forces Clint to keep working when he only wants to go home. ||In the final scene of The Marvels, Kamala Khan visits her to recruit her.||

### Animation

Steinfeld voices her in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
