Natasha Romanoff was trained from childhood by a Soviet program to spy and kill. She changes sides without ceasing to be who she is: wary, highly capable and aware she will never finish paying what she owes. With no flashy powers, she stands out for how well she reads people and gets out of any situation.

## Comics origin

She debuts in *Tales of Suspense* #52, in 1964, as an enemy of Iron Man, created by Stan Lee, Don Rico and Don Heck. She reforms in *The Avengers* #30, in 1966, and the black outfit that became her classic look arrives in 1970, in *The Amazing Spider-Man* #86.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Scarlett Johansson has played her since [Iron Man 2](/marvel/iron-man-2/), as a plant inside Tony Stark's company. She gains weight in [The Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores/) and in [Captain America: The Winter Soldier](/marvel/capitan-america-el-soldado-de-invierno/), where she has to face her own past. Her solo film, [Black Widow](/marvel/viuda-negra/), takes her back to the makeshift family she grew up with, including her sister [Yelena Belova](/marvel/personajes/yelena-belova/). ||In Avengers: Endgame she sacrifices herself on Vormir to obtain the Soul Stone.||

### Animation

She is a constant presence in team titles such as [Ultimate Avengers: The Movie](/marvel/ultimate-avengers/) and [Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores-unidos/). Alongside the Punisher, she leads the anime [Avengers Confidential: Black Widow & Punisher](/marvel/los-vengadores-justicia-y-venganza/), which has a more adult tone.
