Shang-Chi is one of the finest fighters in the Marvel universe, raised from childhood to be his father's perfect weapon. When he discovers what his father does, he flees and tries to lead a normal life. His stories combine carefully staged fights with a simple question: whether you can stop being who you were brought up to be.

## Comics origin

Steve Englehart and Jim Starlin create him for *Special Marvel Edition* #15, from December 1973, at the height of the kung fu craze. In his early stories he is the son of the villain Fu Manchu, a literary character whose rights Marvel eventually lost, which is why his origins were gradually rewritten.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Simu Liu plays him in [Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings](/marvel/shang-chi-y-la-leyenda-de-los-diez-anillos/), where he is a San Francisco valet named Shaun pursued by his father, Wenwu, played by Tony Leung. The standouts are the choreography, inspired by Hong Kong martial arts cinema, and Awkwafina's humor. ||Wong takes him after the credits to meet Bruce Banner and Carol Danvers and study the rings.||
