Danny Rand lost his parents as a child and was raised in K'un-Lun, a mystic city where he trained in martial arts until he won the power of the iron fist. When he returns to New York he is both a warrior monk and the heir to a big company, and that double life leaves him rather out of place. He is also one of the best friends [Luke Cage](/marvel/personajes/luke-cage/) could have.

## Comics origin

Roy Thomas and Gil Kane create him for *Marvel Premiere* #15, from May 1974, during the kung fu craze. He later teams up with Luke Cage in *Power Man and Iron Fist*, one of Marvel's best-remembered pairings.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Finn Jones plays him in [Iron Fist](/marvel/iron-fist-t1/), a heavily criticized Netflix series, and joins the group in [The Defenders](/marvel/the-defenders-t1/). He has an episode alongside Luke in [Luke Cage](/marvel/luke-cage-t2/), which was very well received. ||In the second season of his series he gives up the fist, which passes to Colleen Wing.||

### Animation

Loren Lester voices him in [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/). Greg Cipes voices a teenage version in [Ultimate Spider-Man](/marvel/ultimate-spider-man/) and continues in [Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores-unidos/).
