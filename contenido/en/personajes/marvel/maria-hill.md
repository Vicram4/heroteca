Maria Hill is an effective, tough S.H.I.E.L.D. officer with zero patience for nonsense. Her job is to keep everything running and to be the person who is not intimidated by anyone, not even [Nick Fury](/marvel/personajes/nick-fury/) or [Tony Stark](/marvel/personajes/iron-man/).

## Comics origin

Brian Michael Bendis and David Finch create her for *New Avengers* #4, from March 2005. She is far newer than most characters of her stature, and soon after her creation she replaces Nick Fury as director of S.H.I.E.L.D. and clashes with many of the heroes.

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Cobie Smulders has played her since [The Avengers](/marvel/los-vengadores/) and serves as Fury's right hand in [Captain America: The Winter Soldier](/marvel/capitan-america-el-soldado-de-invierno/) and [Avengers: Age of Ultron](/marvel/vengadores-la-era-de-ultron/). She also appears in [Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.](/marvel/agentes-de-shield-t1/). ||In Secret Invasion she dies in the first episode, at the hands of a Skrull posing as Fury.||

### Animation

She has a minor presence in [The Avengers: Earth's Mightiest Heroes](/marvel/los-vengadores-los-heroes-mas-poderosos-del-planeta/), and Smulders voices her in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
