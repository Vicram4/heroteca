Sue Storm can turn invisible, but her force fields are what is truly frightening. Although for years the comic treated her as the group's decorative member, she is the one who holds the family together and perhaps the most powerful of the Fantastic Four. Sister of [Johnny Storm](/marvel/personajes/antorcha-humana/) and wife of [Reed Richards](/marvel/personajes/mr-fantastico/), she is also the one who tells Reed what he does not want to hear.

## Comics origin

Stan Lee and Jack Kirby create her for *Fantastic Four* #1, from November 1961. At first she only turns invisible and needs rescuing, and it is later that the force fields and a firmer personality arrive. In the eighties, John Byrne drops the "Girl" she had been called until then, renames her Invisible Woman and puts her at the front of the title.

## On screen

### Live action

Rebecca Staab plays her in the unreleased [The Fantastic Four](/marvel/the-fantastic-four-1994/), Jessica Alba in [Fantastic Four](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-2005/) and its sequel, and Kate Mara in [Fantastic Four](/marvel/cuatro-fantasticos/), from 2015.

### Cinematic Universe

Vanessa Kirby plays her in [The Fantastic 4: First Steps](/marvel/los-cuatro-fantasticos-primeros-pasos/), where she is the group's public face and carries much of the plot. ||She gives birth to her son Franklin in the middle of a space mission, and Galactus's determination to take him drives the rest of the story.||

### Animation

Lara Gilchrist voices her in [Fantastic Four: World's Greatest Heroes](/marvel/los-4-fantasticos-2006/), from 2006. She is also one of the heroines of [Marvel Super Hero Adventures](/marvel/aventuras-de-los-superheroes-de-marvel/).
