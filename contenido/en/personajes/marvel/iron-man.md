Tony Stark inherits an arms empire from his father, along with a reputation as a genius that he is happy to feed. Everything changes when a kidnapping forces him to build a suit of armor to break out, and to see for himself where his weapons end up. From then on he turns his talent toward helping others, although he never quite learns to leave anything to anyone else.

## Comics origin

He debuts in *Tales of Suspense* #39, cover-dated March 1963. Several hands were involved: Stan Lee had the idea, Larry Lieber worked out the origin and wrote the first story, and Jack Kirby and Don Heck handled the initial design, a gray suit of armor that soon turned gold. Within months he was among the founders of the [Avengers](/marvel/wiki/vengadores/).

## On screen

### Cinematic Universe

Robert Downey Jr. has played him since [Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-2008/), the 2008 film that launched this era. His arc follows someone trying to make up for the harm he has done, who creates new trouble the harder he tries to protect the world: his choices set up [Avengers: Age of Ultron](/marvel/vengadores-la-era-de-ultron/) and pit him against Steve Rogers in [Captain America: Civil War](/marvel/capitan-america-civil-war/). He also mentors Peter Parker in [Spider-Man: Homecoming](/marvel/spider-man-homecoming/). ||In Avengers: Endgame he uses the Infinity Stones to wipe out Thanos and his army, and the strain costs him his life.||

### Animation

Before the films he had already led [Iron Man: The Animated Series](/marvel/iron-man-la-serie-animada/) in 1994. Later came the film [The Invincible Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-el-invencible/), the teen series [Iron Man: Armored Adventures](/marvel/iron-man-aventuras-de-hierro/) and a 2010 anime series, [Iron Man](/marvel/iron-man-2010/), which takes him to Japan. These are separate continuities, each with its own origin.
