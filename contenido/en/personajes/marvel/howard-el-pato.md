Howard is a talking duck who wears a suit, smokes cigars and hates being on Earth, a planet he finds absurd. His stories began as social satire and remain among the oddest things Marvel has published. The idea is that the one who looks like a joke is often the only one with any common sense.

## Comics origin

Steve Gerber and Val Mayerik create him for *Adventure into Fear* #19, from December 1973, as a supporting player in a Man-Thing story. He became such an unlikely success that he got his own title, *Howard the Duck*, where Gerber mocked politics, pop culture and even the medium itself.

## On screen

### Live action

Chip Zien voices him in [Howard the Duck](/marvel/howard-un-nuevo-heroe/), from 1986, produced by George Lucas and famous for its failure.

### Cinematic Universe

||Seth Green voices him in a cameo after the credits of Guardians of the Galaxy, in the Collector's collection, and he pops up again in the two sequels.||

### Animation

Green also voices him in the [Guardians of the Galaxy](/marvel/guardianes-de-la-galaxia-2015/) series, from 2015, and in [What If...?](/marvel/que-pasaria-si/).
