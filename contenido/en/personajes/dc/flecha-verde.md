Oliver Queen is a shallow millionaire until he gets stranded on an island and has to learn to survive with a bow. Back home, he sets out to clean up Star City with sharp aim and arrows of every kind. Powerless and short-tempered, he tends to be the critical voice among heroes, closely attuned to those who have the least.

## Comics origin

Mort Weisinger and George Papp create him for *More Fun Comics* #73, from 1941, the same issue that brings [Aquaman](/dc/personajes/aquaman/). He began as a Batman imitation with a bow; his political edge, and the beard, arrive in the late 1960s.

## On screen

### Series

Justin Hartley plays him in [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t6/), from the sixth season. Stephen Amell leads [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t1/), which starts in 2012 with a very dark tone and gives birth to a whole universe of connected shows: [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/), [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t3/) and [DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t1/). ||Oliver sacrifices himself in the great Crisis on Infinite Earths crossover.||

Joseph David-Jones appears as Connor Hawke, who inherits the mantle in a possible future, in [DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t1/) and [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t7/).

### Animation

Amell repeats by voice in the animated series [Vixen](/dc/vixen-t1/). Chris Diamantopoulos plays him in [Batman Unlimited: Animal Instincts](/dc/batman-unlimited-instinto-animal/) and [Batman Unlimited: Monster Mayhem](/dc/batman-unlimited-monstermania/).
