Steppenwolf is a general of Apokolips serving his nephew [Darkseid](/dc/personajes/darkseid/), a warrior who has conquered worlds in his name. In the comics he is almost a supporting figure, and it was film that pushed him to the front as the enemy who unites the Justice League.

## Comics origin

Jack Kirby creates him in *New Gods* #7, from 1972, in a flashback story where he helps Darkseid kill Highfather's wife. He then appears rarely, until modern versions give him more weight.

## On screen

### Live action

Ciarán Hinds gives him voice and movement in both versions of the same film, with very clear differences. In [Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/) he is a rather flat villain. In [Zack Snyder's Justice League](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-de-zack-snyder/) he changes design, with living armor, and gains a motive: he needs to win back Darkseid's favor.
