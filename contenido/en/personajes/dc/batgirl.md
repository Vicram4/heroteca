Barbara Gordon is the daughter of Commissioner [James Gordon](/dc/personajes/james-gordon/), a brilliant librarian who puts on a cape without asking anyone's permission. After being paralyzed by the [Joker](/dc/personajes/joker/) in a highly influential comic, she does not give up: she becomes Oracle, the computer expert who coordinates heroes from her wheelchair, one of the genre's most important disabled figures.

## Comics origin

Gardner Fox and Carmine Infantino introduce her in *Detective Comics* #359, from January 1967. The idea stems from the Batman TV series, whose producers wanted a new heroine, which is why comic and show launch her at almost the same time.

## On screen

### Films

Alicia Silverstone leads, in [Batman & Robin](/dc/batman-y-robin/), a reinvented version, Alfred's niece. In Nolan's trilogy, [Batman Begins](/dc/batman-begins/) and [The Dark Knight](/dc/el-caballero-oscuro/), the name belongs only to relatives of the commissioner, with no suit.

### Series

Dina Meyer appears as Oracle through archive footage in [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t6/), in the Crisis crossover.

### Animation

Tara Strong voices her in [Batman: The Killing Joke](/dc/batman-la-broma-asesina/), which recounts that attack. Melendy Britt does so in [The New Adventures of Batman](/dc/las-nuevas-aventuras-de-batman/) and Peyton List in [Batman: Hush](/dc/batman-hush/).
