Jonathan Crane is a psychology professor, humiliated in youth, who devotes himself to studying and provoking fear. With his sack mask and his toxin, he makes his victims hallucinate what they fear most. He is one of the [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/) foes who best reflects that world: a hero who uses fear as a weapon facing someone who uses it as science.

## Comics origin

Bill Finger and Sheldon Moldoff create him for *World's Finest Comics* #3, from 1941. After two appearances he falls into oblivion until 1967, when Gardner Fox brings him back in *Batman* #189 and gives him the fear gas.

## On screen

### Films

Cillian Murphy is a cold, corrupt psychiatrist in [Batman Begins](/dc/batman-begins/), making short appearances in [The Dark Knight](/dc/el-caballero-oscuro/) and [The Dark Knight Rises](/dc/el-caballero-oscuro-la-leyenda-renace/), which makes him the only villain present in all three films.

### Series

In [Gotham](/dc/gotham-t1/) he is a teenager, first with Charlie Tahan and then with David W. Thompson.

### Animation

Corey Burton voices him in [Batman: Gotham Knight](/dc/batman-guardian-de-gotham/), and Christian Lanz in [Batman: Assault on Arkham](/dc/batman-el-asalto-de-arkham/).
