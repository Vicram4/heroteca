Homelander leads the Seven, the star team of the Vought company, and has everything Superman has: flight, strength, eye beams, a flag on his back. What he lacks is any trace of conscience. He smiles for the cameras, needs to be loved and is frightening precisely because nobody can stop him, not even his bosses.

## Comics origin

Garth Ennis and Darick Robertson create him for *The Boys* as a poisonous parody of the great patriotic heroes. In the comic, his link to the crime that obsesses [Billy Butcher](/dc/personajes/billy-butcher/) differs from the one the show tells.

## On screen

### Live action

Antony Starr plays him in [The Boys](/dc/the-boys-t1/), mostly through small gestures: the trembling lip, the look that lasts one second too long. The show peels back his layers, from his upbringing in a lab to his need for adoration. ||In season two he joins forces with Stormfront, and in season three he learns that Soldier Boy is his father.||
