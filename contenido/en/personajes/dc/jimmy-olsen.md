Jimmy is the Daily Planet's young photographer, eager and somewhat clumsy, always ready to follow Clark and Lois into any mess. He acts as the reader's eyes: he admires [Superman](/dc/personajes/superman/) with the excitement of any kid reading the comic. Recent versions have made him more grown-up and more independent.

## Comics origin

After several unnamed appearances, he gets his name in a Jerry Siegel and Joe Shuster tale in *Superman* #13, from late 1941. Readers grew so fond of him that he ended up with his own title for years.

## On screen

### Live action

Marc McClure is the most constant: he appears in all four Christopher Reeve films and in [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl/). Sam Huntington plays him in [Superman Returns](/dc/superman-returns/) and Michael Cassidy in [Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice](/dc/batman-vs-superman-el-amanecer-de-la-justicia/). ||There he is a CIA agent and dies right away.||

On television, Aaron Ashmore is in [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t6/). Mehcad Brooks is, in [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t1/), a very different James Olsen, adult and self-assured, who ends up as Guardian. Azie Tesfai, who also shows up in his titles, plays his sister Kelly in that series.

### Animation

He has minor roles in many Superman films: David Kaufman in [Superman vs. The Elite](/dc/superman-vs-la-elite/), Max Mittelman in [The Death of Superman](/dc/la-muerte-de-superman/) and Matthew Gray Gubler in [All Star Superman](/dc/superman-viaja-al-sol/).
