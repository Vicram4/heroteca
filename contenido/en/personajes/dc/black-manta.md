Black Manta is the great enemy of [Aquaman](/dc/personajes/aquaman/), and it is personal. Under the huge red-eyed helmet that fires beams is a sea mercenary who does not want to rule the oceans but to collect on a debt. The debt changes with the version; the grudge does not.

## Comics origin

Bob Haney and Nick Cardy create him in *Aquaman* #35, from 1967. For a long time he was a villain with neither name nor face, and only later does he receive a concrete past tied to his father's death.

## On screen

### Live action

Yahya Abdul-Mateen II is David Kane in [Aquaman](/dc/aquaman/), a pirate who blames the hero for his father's death and builds his suit with Atlantean technology. In [Aquaman and the Lost Kingdom](/dc/aquaman-y-el-reino-perdido/) he returns as the engine of the story, with a far darker power in his hands.

### Animation

Harry Lennix voices him in [Justice League: Throne of Atlantis](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-el-trono-de-atlantis/) and Kevin Michael Richardson in [Attack of the Legion of Doom!](/dc/lego-dc-comics-super-heroes-la-liga-de-la-justicia-el-ataque-de-la-legion-del-mal/).
