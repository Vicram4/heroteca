Hal Jordan is a daredevil test pilot to whom a dying alien hands a ring that materializes whatever its bearer imagines. That makes him a member of the Green Lantern Corps, a space police force with an officer for each sector of the galaxy. His weapon runs on willpower, so his powers depend on his character. Several characters share the title, which is why different Lanterns are gathered here.

## Comics origin

The first Green Lantern is Alan Scott, from 1940, with a magical origin. Hal Jordan arrives in *Showcase* #22, from October 1959, by John Broome and Gil Kane, with science fiction now at the forefront. Other bearers follow, such as John Stewart and Jessica Cruz.

## On screen

### Films

Ryan Reynolds leads [Green Lantern](/dc/linterna-verde/), from 2011, a costly bet that did not meet expectations.

### Animation

Animation is where he has worked best. Christopher Meloni leads [Green Lantern: First Flight](/dc/linterna-verde-primer-vuelo/), and Nathan Fillion [Green Lantern: Emerald Knights](/dc/linterna-verde-caballeros-esmeralda/), an anthology with several members of the galactic police. Fillion repeats in several League films, such as [Justice League: Doom](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-perdicion/). David Boreanaz does so in [Justice League: The New Frontier](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-la-nueva-frontera/).

Other titles feature other bearers: Roger Cross is John Stewart in [Justice League Dark: Apokolips War](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia-oscura-la-guerra-apokolips/), and Diane Guerrero is Jessica Cruz in [Justice League vs. the Fatal Five](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-vs-los-cinco-fatales/).
