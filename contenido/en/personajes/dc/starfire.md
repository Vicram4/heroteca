Koriand'r is a princess of the planet Tamaran, sold into slavery by her own people and arriving on Earth as a fugitive. She flies, fires energy and learns fast, and against her past she has a joy of living that spreads through the group. With the Titans she finds what she never had, and a long history with Dick Grayson.

## Comics origin

Marv Wolfman and George Pérez create her in the same story as [Raven](/dc/personajes/raven/) and [Cyborg](/dc/personajes/cyborg/), inside *DC Comics Presents* #26, from 1980, as part of the relaunch of the New Teen Titans.

## On screen

### Live action

Anna Diop plays her in [Titans](/dc/titans-t1/), where at first she has lost her memory and is much more serious and aggressive than in other versions.

### Animation

Jennifer Hale voices her in [Superman/Batman: Public Enemies](/dc/superman-batman-enemigos-publicos/). Kari Wahlgren is the voice in [Justice League vs. Teen Titans](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-contra-los-jovenes-titanes/) and [Teen Titans: The Judas Contract](/dc/jovenes-titanes-el-contrato-de-judas/).
