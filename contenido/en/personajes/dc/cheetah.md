Cheetah is the most persistent enemy of [Wonder Woman](/dc/personajes/wonder-woman/). Strong, incredibly fast and clawed, she is part victim as well as villain: nearly always someone who wanted to be more than she was and paid for it. The best-known version is Barbara Minerva, an archaeologist trapped by a curse.

## Comics origin

William Moulton Marston and H. G. Peter create the first one in *Wonder Woman* #6, from 1943: Priscilla Rich, a jealous society lady. Barbara Minerva arrives in 1987, with the heroine's relaunch led by George Pérez.

## On screen

### Live action

Kristen Wiig is Barbara Minerva in [Wonder Woman 1984](/dc/wonder-woman-1984/), a clumsy, kind scientist who admires Diana. ||A wish grants her everything she envies and strips away her humanity until she turns feline.||

### Animation

Claudia Black voices her in [Justice League: Doom](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-perdicion/), Cree Summer in [Attack of the Legion of Doom!](/dc/lego-dc-comics-super-heroes-la-liga-de-la-justicia-el-ataque-de-la-legion-del-mal/), Laura Bailey in [Batman Unlimited: Animal Instincts](/dc/batman-unlimited-instinto-animal/) and Kimberly Brooks in [Wonder Woman: Bloodlines](/dc/wonder-woman-linaje/).
