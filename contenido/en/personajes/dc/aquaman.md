Arthur Curry is the son of a lighthouse keeper and an Atlantean queen, and he inherits the throne of an undersea realm that sees him as half a foreigner. He breathes underwater, talks to sea life and has colossal strength. For decades he was the League's easy joke, until film and recent comics turned him into an imposing hero.

## Comics origin

Paul Norris and Mort Weisinger create him for *More Fun Comics* #73, from 1941, the same issue in which [Green Arrow](/dc/personajes/flecha-verde/) debuts. Geoff Johns gives him new life in 2011 with a run in which the script itself laughs at the joke.

## On screen

### Live action

Alan Ritchson plays a young Arthur in [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t5/). Jason Momoa appears for a moment in [Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice](/dc/batman-vs-superman-el-amanecer-de-la-justicia/), joins [Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/) and leads [Aquaman](/dc/aquaman/) and [Aquaman and the Lost Kingdom](/dc/aquaman-y-el-reino-perdido/). His version is more rebellious and irreverent than earlier ones. ||Momoa also shows up in the last episode of the first season of Peacemaker and in The Flash.||

### Animation

Matt Lanter voices him in [Justice League: Throne of Atlantis](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-el-trono-de-atlantis/), which adapts the Johns run. Cary Elwes makes a very violent Aquaman in [Justice League: The Flashpoint Paradox](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-la-paradoja-del-tiempo/).
