Lois Lane is the Daily Planet's star reporter: stubborn, brave to the point of recklessness and a better journalist than her colleague Clark Kent, whom she barely takes seriously in her early stories. For years she chased [Superman](/dc/personajes/superman/)'s secret identity without seeing what stood beside her, and that game is part of the character's charm.

## Comics origin

Jerry Siegel and Joe Shuster create her alongside Superman, and she appears in the same story, the one in *Action Comics* #1, from 1938. She is therefore as old as the hero himself and one of the genre's first strong female leads.

## On screen

### Live action

Phyllis Coates comes first in the collection, in [Superman and the Mole Men](/dc/superman-y-los-hombres-topo/). Margot Kidder defines her opposite Christopher Reeve, in the saga that opens with [Superman](/dc/superman/). Kate Bosworth plays her in [Superman Returns](/dc/superman-returns/) and Amy Adams in [Man of Steel](/dc/el-hombre-de-acero/) and the League films.

On television, Erica Durance joins in the fourth season of [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t4/). Elizabeth Tulloch plays her in the crossovers between series, such as those in [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t4/) and [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t5/).

### Animation

Rebecca Romijn repeats her voice in [The Death of Superman](/dc/la-muerte-de-superman/) and other films of that continuity. Anne Heche acts in [Superman: Doomsday](/dc/superman-doomsday/), Christina Hendricks in [All Star Superman](/dc/superman-viaja-al-sol/) and Amy Acker in [Superman: Red Son](/dc/superman-hijo-rojo/).
