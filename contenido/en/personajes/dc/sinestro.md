Thaal Sinestro was the most respected Green Lantern of all and Hal Jordan's teacher. He was also the dictator of his planet, Korugar, convinced that order was worth any price. When he is expelled from the Corps, he keeps the idea that fear works better than will, and founds his own army of yellow rings to prove it.

## Comics origin

John Broome and Gil Kane create him in *Green Lantern* #7, from 1961, and he becomes the series' great enemy. His prominence grows greatly with Geoff Johns's stories of the 2000s, especially *The Sinestro Corps War*.

## On screen

### Live action

Mark Strong plays him in [Green Lantern](/dc/linterna-verde/), impeccable as the stern mentor of [Hal Jordan](/dc/personajes/linterna-verde/). ||A mid-credits scene shows him putting on the yellow ring, setting up a sequel that never came.||

### Animation

Victor Garber voices him in [Green Lantern: First Flight](/dc/linterna-verde-primer-vuelo/), Jason Isaacs in [Green Lantern: Emerald Knights](/dc/linterna-verde-caballeros-esmeralda/) and Mark Hamill in [Attack of the Legion of Doom!](/dc/lego-dc-comics-super-heroes-la-liga-de-la-justicia-el-ataque-de-la-legion-del-mal/).
