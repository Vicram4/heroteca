Grodd is a gorilla smarter than almost any human, able to get inside other people's minds. He escapes Gorilla City, a hidden civilization of advanced apes, convinced that humanity owes him the planet. His image is absurd, but it works because he acts with total cruelty and seriousness. He is an old enemy of the [Flash](/dc/personajes/flash/).

## Comics origin

John Broome and Carmine Infantino create him in *The Flash* #106, from 1959, right when a gorilla on the cover sold more copies.

## On screen

### Live action

A digital creature voiced by David Sobolov appears in [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/), from season one, the product of S.T.A.R. Labs experiments and with a bond with Caitlin. He is among the show's most ambitious effects.

### Animation

Kevin Michael Richardson voices him in [Justice League vs. Bizarro League](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-contra-la-liga-de-bizarro/) and [Attack of the Legion of Doom!](/dc/lego-dc-comics-super-heroes-la-liga-de-la-justicia-el-ataque-de-la-legion-del-mal/). Takehito Koyasu voices him in [Batman Ninja](/dc/batman-ninja/), moved to feudal Japan.
