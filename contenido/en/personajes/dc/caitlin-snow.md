Caitlin is the medic of [Flash](/dc/personajes/flash/)'s team, reserved and brilliant, and from a certain point she lives with a second personality: Killer Frost, able to freeze everything she touches. The struggle between the two defines the character. In the comics, on the other hand, Killer Frost is a villain's name held by several women.

## Comics origin

Killer Frost has existed since the 1970s as an enemy of [Firestorm](/dc/personajes/firestorm/), first Crystal Frost and then Louise Lincoln. Caitlin Snow arrives much later, in 2013, during DC's New 52 era.

## On screen

### Series

Danielle Panabaker plays her in [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t2/) and above all in [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/), and appears in crossovers with [DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t2/) and [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t3/). ||The show's other Caitlin, a villain from another Earth, foreshadows her transformation.||

### Animation

The animated voices in these titles belong to other versions of Killer Frost, not to Caitlin: Jennifer Hale in [Superman/Batman: Public Enemies](/dc/superman-batman-enemigos-publicos/) and [Batman: Assault on Arkham](/dc/batman-el-asalto-de-arkham/), and Kristin Bauer in [Suicide Squad: Hell to Pay](/dc/escuadron-suicida-consecuencias-infernales/).
