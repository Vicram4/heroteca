Amanda Waller is a United States government official with no superpower at all, and yet she makes the most dangerous tremble. She heads the Suicide Squad: she gathers convicted criminals, sends them on impossible missions and keeps them in line through threats. Tough, smart and remorseless, she truly believes she is doing the right thing.

## Comics origin

She appears in *Legends* #1, from 1986, created by John Ostrander, Len Wein and John Byrne. Ostrander and Kim Yale develop her right away in the Squad series. Seeing a middle-aged Black woman, not remotely glamorous, giving orders in a superhero comic was rare then, and that made her memorable.

## On screen

### Films

Angela Bassett plays her in [Green Lantern](/dc/linterna-verde/). Viola Davis gives her definitive shape in [Suicide Squad](/dc/escuadron-suicida/) and [The Suicide Squad](/dc/el-escuadron-suicida/), and also appears in [Black Adam](/dc/black-adam/).

### Series

Pam Grier appears in [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t9/), and Cynthia Addai-Robinson in [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t2/), as head of a secret unit. Davis repeats in [Peacemaker](/dc/el-pacificador-t1/), where her shadow hangs over the whole plot.

### Animation

CCH Pounder plays her in [Superman/Batman: Public Enemies](/dc/superman-batman-enemigos-publicos/) and [Batman: Assault on Arkham](/dc/batman-el-asalto-de-arkham/), and Vanessa Williams in [Suicide Squad: Hell to Pay](/dc/escuadron-suicida-consecuencias-infernales/).
