Bane is born and raised in a prison, serving his father's sentence, and he walks out as an immensely strong man and far more educated than he looks. He uses a drug called venom that multiplies his strength. What sets him apart from other thugs is that he plans: he wears [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/) down before facing him.

## Comics origin

Chuck Dixon, Doug Moench and Graham Nolan create him in *Batman: Vengeance of Bane* #1, from 1993. That same year, the *Knightfall* saga gives him his most famous moment: he frees Arkham, waits until Batman is exhausted and snaps his spine.

## On screen

### Live action

His debut is almost a caricature: in [Batman & Robin](/dc/batman-y-robin/) he is a giant with hardly any dialogue, played by Jeep Swenson, with Michael Reid MacKay as the man before the change. Tom Hardy gives him his full dimension in [The Dark Knight Rises](/dc/el-caballero-oscuro-la-leyenda-renace/), with a mask, an unmistakable voice and a plan to take Gotham. ||That film later reveals he is not the heir of the League of Shadows but the protector of the real one.|| Shane West plays him in the final season of [Gotham](/dc/gotham-t5/).

### Animation

Carlos Alazraqui voices him in [Justice League: Doom](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-perdicion/), John DiMaggio in [Batman: Assault on Arkham](/dc/batman-el-asalto-de-arkham/), Kenta Miyake in [Batman Ninja](/dc/batman-ninja/) and Adam Gifford in [Batman: Hush](/dc/batman-hush/).
