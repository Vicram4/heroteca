Darkseid rules Apokolips, a planet turned into both factory and hell, and he does not conquer worlds just to own them: he wants the Anti-Life Equation, which would erase the will of every living being. He speaks little, moves slowly and fires beams from his eyes that chase down their target. For the [Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/) he is the threat standing behind nearly all the others.

## Comics origin

Jack Kirby creates him for his Fourth World saga. He peeks in at *Superman's Pal Jimmy Olsen* #134, from 1970, and is properly introduced in *Forever People* #1, from 1971. His son Orion, raised by the opposing side, is one of his great conflicts.

## On screen

### Live action

His only version outside animation is the one in [Zack Snyder's Justice League](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-de-zack-snyder/), with Ray Porter providing voice and movement. His presence there is mostly a promise of what would come later.

### Animation

Andre Braugher voices him in [Superman/Batman: Apocalypse](/dc/superman-batman-apocalipsis/), where he wants to turn [Supergirl](/dc/personajes/supergirl/) into his weapon, and Steve Blum in [Justice League: War](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-guerra/), as the villain who brings the team together. Tony Todd is the most frequent voice, in two LEGO films, in [Reign of the Supermen](/dc/la-muerte-de-superman-parte-2-el-reinado-de-los-superhombres/) and in [Apokolips War](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia-oscura-la-guerra-apokolips/). Bruce Thomas voices him in [Gods and Monsters](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-dioses-y-monstruos/).
