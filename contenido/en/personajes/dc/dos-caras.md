Harvey Dent is the honest prosecutor all of [Gotham](/dc/wiki/gotham/) trusts until an acid attack destroys half his face and breaks his mind. From then on he makes every decision by flipping a coin with one side scarred. He is one of the most tragic of [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/)'s foes, because what is lost with him is a city's hope.

## Comics origin

Bob Kane introduces him in *Detective Comics* #66, from August 1942, with Bill Finger's collaboration, in a story influenced by *Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde*.

## On screen

### Films

Billy Dee Williams is Harvey Dent in [Batman](/dc/batman-1989/), from 1989, without ever transforming. Tommy Lee Jones is an exaggerated, colorful Two-Face in [Batman Forever](/dc/batman-forever/). Aaron Eckhart hits on the most painful version in [The Dark Knight](/dc/el-caballero-oscuro/). ||Harry Lawtey plays a still unscarred Dent in Joker: Folie à Deux, until its final stretch.||

### Series

Nicholas D'Agosto is a young prosecutor Dent in [Gotham](/dc/gotham-t1/), before his fall.

### Animation

William Shatner voices him in [Batman vs. Two-Face](/dc/batman-vs-dos-caras/), which brings back the tone of the 1960s series. Wade Williams does so in [Batman: The Dark Knight Returns](/dc/batman-el-regreso-del-caballero-oscuro/) and Robin Atkin Downes in [Batman: Year One](/dc/batman-ano-uno/).
