Talia is the daughter of [Ra's al Ghul](/dc/personajes/ras-al-ghul/), and for years she lives between loyalty to her father and love for [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/). Raised among assassins, she is clever and capable of anything, and with Bruce Wayne she has a son, [Damian](/dc/personajes/damian-wayne/). Nobody ever quite knows whose side she is on, and she herself rarely does.

## Comics origin

Dennis O'Neil and Bob Brown create her in *Detective Comics* #411, from 1971, shortly before introducing her father. Her story with Batman is among the longest in comics, and Damian's birth gives her new importance from 2006.

## On screen

### Live action

Lexa Doig plays her in [season five of Arrow](/dc/arrow-t5/), as Oliver Queen's teacher in the flashbacks. ||In The Dark Knight Rises, Marion Cotillard seems to be a businesswoman named Miranda Tate, and at the end she is revealed as Talia.||

### Animation

Morena Baccarin voices her in [Son of Batman](/dc/el-hijo-de-batman/) and [Batman: Bad Blood](/dc/batman-mala-sangre/).
