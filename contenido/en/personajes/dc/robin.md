Dick Grayson is a circus acrobat who loses his parents to a sabotage during the show and is taken in and trained by [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/). Robin brings color and laughs to a somber hero, and over time becomes a title handed from one kid to another. Dick, once older, leaves the post and carries on alone under the name Nightwing.

## Comics origin

Bill Finger, Bob Kane and Jerry Robinson introduce him in *Detective Comics* #38, from April 1940, and sales nearly doubled. Other Robins follow, such as Jason Todd and Tim Drake, and later [Damian Wayne](/dc/personajes/damian-wayne/), who has his own entry.

## On screen

### Live action

Burt Ward forms the campy duo with Adam West in [Batman](/dc/batman/), from 1966, and returns briefly as a cameo in [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t5/). Chris O'Donnell is a rebellious Dick Grayson in [Batman Forever](/dc/batman-forever/) and [Batman & Robin](/dc/batman-y-robin/).

Brenton Thwaites is an adult Dick trying to leave Batman behind in [Titans](/dc/titans-t1/), where Curran Walters plays Jason Todd.

### Animation

Sean Maher voices Nightwing in several films, such as [Son of Batman](/dc/el-hijo-de-batman/) and [Teen Titans: The Judas Contract](/dc/jovenes-titanes-el-contrato-de-judas/). Jason Todd drives [Batman: Under the Red Hood](/dc/batman-capucha-roja/), in which Neil Patrick Harris voices Dick. Ariel Winter plays Carrie Kelley, a teenage Robin, in [Batman: The Dark Knight Returns](/dc/batman-el-regreso-del-caballero-oscuro/). Burt Ward speaks for him again in [Batman vs. Two-Face](/dc/batman-vs-dos-caras/).
