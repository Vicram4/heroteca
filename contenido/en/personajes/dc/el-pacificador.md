Chris Smith loves peace so much he is willing to kill men, women and children to keep it. That line sums up both the joke of the character and his tragedy: shiny helmet, muscles and glam music, and underneath, a man raised by a monstrous father. He is ridiculous, dangerous and, as his story moves on, surprisingly moving.

## Comics origin

Joe Gill and Pat Boyette create him in *Fightin' 5* #40, from 1966, for Charlton, as a pacifist diplomat who does not hesitate to use force. DC later buys him and makes him darker, tormented by what he has done.

## On screen

### Live action

John Cena plays him in [The Suicide Squad](/dc/el-escuadron-suicida/), by James Gunn. ||There he ends up killing Rick Flag on Amanda Waller's orders, and what looks like his death in the collapse is not, as the post-credits scene reveals.|| The [Peacemaker](/dc/el-pacificador-t1/) series, also by Gunn, makes him the lead and dares to take him seriously, with [Amanda Waller](/dc/personajes/amanda-waller/) still pulling strings from afar.
