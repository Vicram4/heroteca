The name Blue Beetle has passed through three pairs of hands, and the ones that matter here belong to Jaime Reyes, a teenager who finds a mysterious scarab. It fuses with his spine, wraps him in armor and also talks to him. He has a huge family who end up mixed up in everything, and much of the charm lies with them.

## Comics origin

The first Blue Beetle dates from the 1930s, at Fox Comics, and the second, Ted Kord, comes from Charlton. Keith Giffen, John Rogers and Cully Hamner create Jaime, a kid from El Paso who appears in *Infinite Crisis* #3, from 2006, and inherits the first one's scarab.

## On screen

### Live action

Jaren Brandt Bartlett plays him in an episode of [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t10/). Xolo Maridueña leads [Blue Beetle](/dc/blue-beetle/), DC's first film with a Latino hero at the front, closely focused on his home and his grandmother, with Ted Kord as a ghost everyone talks about.

### Animation

Jake T. Austin voices him in [Justice League vs. Teen Titans](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-contra-los-jovenes-titanes/) and [Teen Titans: The Judas Contract](/dc/jovenes-titanes-el-contrato-de-judas/).
