Slade Wilson is a soldier put through an experiment that multiplies his strength, reflexes and capacity for calculation. Turned hired killer, with a half-black, half-orange mask and a single eye, he works for contracts rather than out of evil, which makes him more unsettling. He also has a disastrous relationship with his own children.

## Comics origin

Marv Wolfman and George Pérez introduce him in *The New Teen Titans* #2, from 1980, as an enemy of the young heroes. His best-known saga is *The Judas Contract*, in which he infiltrates them with the help of a teenage spy.

## On screen

### Series

Manu Bennett is the Slade of [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t1/): first Oliver's friend on the island and then his worst enemy, in one of the show's best storylines. Michael Hogan had played him in [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t10/), and Esai Morales does so in [Titans](/dc/titans-t2/), with his family at the heart of the story.

### Films

Joe Manganiello shows up after the credits of [Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/) and repeats in [Zack Snyder's Justice League](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-de-zack-snyder/). He is among the characters that film left half-finished.

### Animation

Miguel Ferrer voices him in [Teen Titans: The Judas Contract](/dc/jovenes-titanes-el-contrato-de-judas/), which adapts that saga. Ron Perlman does so in [Justice League: The Flashpoint Paradox](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-la-paradoja-del-tiempo/) and Thomas Gibson in [Son of Batman](/dc/el-hijo-de-batman/).
