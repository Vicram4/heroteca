Courtney Whitmore is a high school girl who stumbles on a cosmic staff, that of the old hero Starman, and starts using it out of pure rebellion, to annoy her stepfather, once a heroes' sidekick. What is special about her is her spirit: she throws herself into heroism with an enthusiasm the veterans had lost.

## Comics origin

Geoff Johns and Lee Moder create her in *Stars and S.T.R.I.P.E.* #0, from 1999. Johns gives her the name and looks of his sister Courtney, who died in the 1996 explosion of TWA Flight 800. She starts as Star-Spangled Kid and soon takes the name Stargirl.

## On screen

### Series

Britt Irvin plays her in two seasons of Smallville, [the ninth](/dc/smallville-t9/) and [the tenth](/dc/smallville-t10/), and Sarah Grey in [Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t2/). Brec Bassinger makes her the lead of [Stargirl](/dc/stargirl-t1/), with Johns running the show, in a family tone very close to the comic. She also appears in the [Legends](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t5/) crossover.
