Luthor is the smartest man in Metropolis and, depending on the era, either a mad scientist or a businessman spotless outside and rotten within. He has no powers: his war on [Superman](/dc/personajes/superman/) springs from pride and from the conviction that a human like him should hold that place. That makes him the most human foe of the most powerful hero.

## Comics origin

Jerry Siegel and Joe Shuster introduce him in *Action Comics* #23, from April 1940. Oddly, he starts out red-haired, and a drawing slip left him bald shortly afterward. The origin tying him to a young Superboy, and the name Lex, come in 1960.

## On screen

### Live action

Gene Hackman plays him as roguish and theatrical in [Superman](/dc/superman/) and its sequels. Kevin Spacey revives that tone in [Superman Returns](/dc/superman-returns/) and Jesse Eisenberg offers a young, jittery Luthor in [Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice](/dc/batman-vs-superman-el-amanecer-de-la-justicia/).

Michael Rosenbaum delivers perhaps the fullest version in [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t1/): the friend of Clark who slowly turns into his enemy. Jon Cryer takes him up with dark humor in [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t4/).

### Animation

Clancy Brown makes him president of the United States in [Superman/Batman: Public Enemies](/dc/superman-batman-enemigos-publicos/), and Rainn Wilson voices him in [The Death of Superman](/dc/la-muerte-de-superman/) and related films. In [Superman: Red Son](/dc/superman-hijo-rojo/) he is played by Diedrich Bader.
