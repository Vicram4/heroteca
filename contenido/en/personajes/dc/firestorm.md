Firestorm is born of a nuclear accident that fuses two people in one body: young athlete Ronnie Raymond and Professor Martin Stein. The kid drives the body, while the scientist talks in his head with the know-how, and between them they transform matter. That pair of opposites, forced to get along, is good for both comedy and drama.

## Comics origin

Gerry Conway and Al Milgrom introduce him in *Firestorm, the Nuclear Man* #1, from March 1978. The idea has been renewed since: in 2004 another bearer appears, Jason Rusch.

## On screen

### Series

The fusion arrives in [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/), with Victor Garber as Stein and Robbie Amell as Ronnie, tied to the particle accelerator accident. Franz Drameh takes over as Jefferson Jackson in [the second season](/dc/the-flash-t2/), and he and Garber are part of the original group of [DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t1/). ||Stein dies in the Crisis on Earth-X crossover, seen in Arrow and Supergirl.||

### Animation

Cedric Yarbrough voices him in [Justice League: Crisis on Two Earths](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-crisis-en-dos-tierras/).
