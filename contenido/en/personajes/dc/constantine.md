John Constantine is a Liverpool magician with a trench coat and a cigarette, better at conning demons than beating them up. He has enemies to spare in hell and his friends rarely end well, something he carries with guilt and black humor. He is a true antihero: selfish, clever and, when the time comes, capable of sacrifice.

## Comics origin

Alan Moore, Stephen R. Bissette and John Totleben create him for *Swamp Thing* #37, from 1985, partly because they wanted to draw someone who looked like Sting. What began as a whim led to *Hellblazer*, one of DC's longest horror series.

## On screen

### Films

Keanu Reeves plays him in [Constantine](/dc/constantine/), from 2005, with black hair and in Los Angeles. It strays far from the comic, but it has its defenders.

### Series

Matt Ryan is the most faithful Constantine: blond, British and brash in [Constantine](/dc/constantine-t1/), a one-season series. The role stuck and gave him a second life: he visits [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t4/) and joins [DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t3/). [The Sandman](/dc/sandman/) also features Johanna Constantine, a female version and his ancestor, played by Jenna Coleman.

### Animation

Ryan keeps voicing him in [Justice League Dark](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-oscura/), [Constantine: City of Demons](/dc/constantine-ciudad-de-demonios/) and [Justice League Dark: Apokolips War](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia-oscura-la-guerra-apokolips/).
