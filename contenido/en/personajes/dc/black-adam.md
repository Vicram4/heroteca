Teth-Adam received the same powers as [Shazam](/dc/personajes/shazam/) in ancient Egypt, and used them for revenge. That is why he was removed from the world for millennia. Depending on who tells it, he is a tyrant or the liberator of his homeland, Kahndaq, and his appeal lies right there: a hero with no problem killing.

## Comics origin

Otto Binder and C. C. Beck introduce him in *The Marvel Family* #1, from 1945, then published by Fawcett. He dies in that same story and spends decades out of the picture. DC brings him back and, over time, turns him into a more complex character.

## On screen

### Live action

Dwayne Johnson plays him in [Black Adam](/dc/black-adam/), a project he had pushed for years, in which the people of Kahndaq awaken him five thousand years later to defend them. The film pits him against the Justice Society, far more worried about his methods than about his powers.
