Cisco is the technical brain and comic relief of [Flash](/dc/personajes/flash/)'s team: he designs the suits, fixes the impossible and names every villain with a nickname drawn from pop culture. Over time he finds that he himself can see and open breaches between realities, which makes him Vibe. His humor counterbalances the more dramatic storylines.

## Comics origin

Vibe is born in *Justice League of America Annual* #2, from 1984, with Gerry Conway among his creators. There he is called Paco Ramone, a young man from Detroit who shakes things with vibration waves, and he goes down in history as the first League member killed in action. Today's Cisco owes nearly everything to television.

## On screen

### Series

Carlos Valdes is the only live-action performer: he debuts in [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t2/), is a regular on [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/) and visits [DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t1/) and [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t2/). He even has a small digital series of his own, [The Flash: Chronicles of Cisco](/dc/the-flash-chronicles-of-cisco-t1/). ||In time he gives up his powers and leaves the team.||

### Animation

Valdes also voices him in [Vixen](/dc/vixen-t1/).
