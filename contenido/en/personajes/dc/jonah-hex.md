Jonah Hex is an American Civil War veteran, a bounty hunter and one of the worst people to run into in the West. The scar warps half his face, he has a foul temper and a twisted sense of honor to which he always ends up loyal. His territory is the hard western, with the odd trip into the supernatural.

## Comics origin

John Albano and Tony DeZuniga create him in the second series of *All-Star Western*, in issue 10, from 1972. The title is renamed *Weird Western Tales* soon afterward, with him in the lead.

## On screen

### Live action

Josh Brolin plays him in [Jonah Hex](/dc/jonah-hex/), from 2010, a film with a supernatural tone, Megan Fox at his side and a poor reception. Johnathon Schaech fares much better in [Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t1/), where he appears over several seasons thanks to trips to the West, and is also listed in [Batwoman](/dc/batwoman-t1/).
