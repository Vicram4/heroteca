Dr. Harrison Wells runs S.T.A.R. Labs and guides Barry Allen in his early days as the [Flash](/dc/personajes/flash/). He then becomes the show's great running joke: each season brings another Wells from another Earth, with the same face and a different personality. Tom Cavanagh has a great time with all of them.

## Comics origin

He does not come from the comics. Geoff Johns, Greg Berlanti and Andrew Kreisberg create him for the TV show, without basing him on any DC character.

## On screen

### Series

Tom Cavanagh plays him in every season of [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/). ||The Wells of season one is really Eobard Thawne, who killed the genuine one and stole his identity.|| Then come Harry, a surly scientist from Earth-2, in [season two](/dc/the-flash-t2/); H.R., an enthusiastic novelist, in [season three](/dc/the-flash-t3/); Sherloque, a French detective, in [season five](/dc/the-flash-t5/), and Nash, an explorer, in [season six](/dc/the-flash-t6/). Cavanagh also appears in the Crisis on Earth-X crossover, which runs through [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t6/), [Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t3/) and [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t3/).
