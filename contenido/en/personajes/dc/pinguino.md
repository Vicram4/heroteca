Oswald Cobblepot is a criminal in a tuxedo with a monocle and rigged umbrellas, scorned since childhood for his figure. Unlike other [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/) foes, he is not mad: he is a ruthless businessman who tends to run [Gotham](/dc/wiki/gotham/)'s underworld from his club. He craves the respect of the high society that laughs at him.

## Comics origin

Bob Kane and Bill Finger introduce him in *Detective Comics* #58, from December 1941. Kane said the idea came from a cigarette brand's penguins and from men in suits who reminded him of those animals.

## On screen

### Films

Burgess Meredith has him squawking in [Batman](/dc/batman/), from 1966. Danny DeVito offers the most grotesque version in [Batman Returns](/dc/batman-vuelve/), turned into a sewer monster. Colin Farrell, unrecognizable under makeup, is a second-rate gangster in [The Batman](/dc/the-batman/).

### Series

Robin Lord Taylor plays the character's rise over five seasons of [Gotham](/dc/gotham-t1/), from errand boy to king of crime, and is among the show's most remembered elements.

### Animation

Tom Kenny plays him in [Scooby-Doo! & Batman: The Brave and the Bold](/dc/scooby-doo-y-el-intrepido-batman/), and Nolan North in [Batman: Assault on Arkham](/dc/batman-el-asalto-de-arkham/). In [Batman vs. Two-Face](/dc/batman-vs-dos-caras/) he has the voice of William Salyers.
