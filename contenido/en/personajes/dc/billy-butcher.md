Billy Butcher hates superheroes, and one in particular. He blames [Homelander](/dc/personajes/patriota/) for what happened to his wife, Becca, and has turned that rage into a trade: he gathers a small group to expose and eliminate the "supes". He is charming, foul-mouthed and able to sacrifice any of his own, and the show never makes clear where his cause ends and his obsession begins.

## Comics origin

Garth Ennis and Darick Robertson create him for *The Boys*, a series born in 2006 at Wildstorm, a DC imprint, which later moves on to Dynamite. In the comic, the hand pushing the Boys is the CIA's.

## On screen

### Live action

Karl Urban plays him in [The Boys](/dc/the-boys-t1/) with a London accent and a dangerous cheerfulness, and the role is among the show's most praised. ||At the end of season one he learns that Becca is alive and raising a son of Homelander's.|| In [season three](/dc/the-boys-t3/) he crosses a line he had sworn not to: ||taking a temporary version of the Compound V that gives powers to those he hates.||
