Mera commands water, shaping it and hurling it as a weapon. She is a queen in her stories, a partner to [Aquaman](/dc/personajes/aquaman/), and she does not merely stand beside him: she is often the one who makes the hard decisions and leads him to accept his destiny.

## Comics origin

Jack Miller and Nick Cardy create her in *Aquaman* #11, from 1963, as the queen of Dimension Aqua. She marries Arthur soon after, in one of the first great superhero weddings, and more recent versions make her a native of the kingdom of Xebel.

## On screen

### Live action

Elena Satine plays her in [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t10/). Amber Heard is a princess of Xebel, Orm's betrothed and the hero's ally in [Aquaman](/dc/aquaman/). Before that she has a small role in [Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/), repeated in [Zack Snyder's version](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-de-zack-snyder/), and in [Aquaman and the Lost Kingdom](/dc/aquaman-y-el-reino-perdido/) she is far less present.

### Animation

Sumalee Montano voices her in [Justice League: Throne of Atlantis](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-el-trono-de-atlantis/).
