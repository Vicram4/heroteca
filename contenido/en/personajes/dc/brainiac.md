Brainiac hoards. He roams the worlds storing their knowledge, shrinks their cities into bottles and then eliminates what remains, so that what he has is unique. He is cold, nearly always half machine, and among his trophies is Kandor, the Kryptonian city [Superman](/dc/personajes/superman/) dreams of restoring to full size.

## Comics origin

Otto Binder and Al Plastino create him in *Action Comics* #242, from 1958. In his early stories he is a green-skinned alien; later he is explained as a computer shaped like a man, and new versions keep changing him.

## On screen

### Live action

James Marsters is Milton Fine in season five of [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t5/), a professor with other plans. Blake Ritson turns him into the great threat of [Krypton](/dc/krypton-t1/). He should not be confused with his descendant Brainy, a Legion hero played by Jesse Rath in [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t4/), or with the Brainiac 5 in [Justice League vs. the Fatal Five](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-vs-los-cinco-fatales/).

### Animation

John Noble voices him in [Superman: Unbound](/dc/superman-sin-limites/) and Paul Williams in [Superman: Red Son](/dc/superman-hijo-rojo/).
