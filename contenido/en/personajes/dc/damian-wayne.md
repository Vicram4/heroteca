Damian is the son of [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/) and [Talia al Ghul](/dc/personajes/talia-al-ghul/), schooled from childhood by the League of Assassins. He enters his father's life at ten, arrogant, extremely dangerous and with no idea what not killing means. Becoming [Robin](/dc/personajes/robin/) is his way of learning to be something else, with a temper that makes it easy for nobody.

## Comics origin

A baby son of Batman and Talia already appears in 1987, in a standalone story. Grant Morrison and Andy Kubert turn him into Damian and make him part of the regular stories with *Batman* #655, from 2006, at the start of *Batman and Son*.

## On screen

### Animation

In the collection he exists only in animation, nearly always voiced by Stuart Allan: [Son of Batman](/dc/el-hijo-de-batman/), where he is introduced, [Batman vs. Robin](/dc/batman-contra-robin/), [Batman: Bad Blood](/dc/batman-mala-sangre/), [Justice League vs. Teen Titans](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-contra-los-jovenes-titanes/), [Teen Titans: The Judas Contract](/dc/jovenes-titanes-el-contrato-de-judas/), [Batman: Hush](/dc/batman-hush/) and [Apokolips War](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia-oscura-la-guerra-apokolips/). Yuki Kaji voices him in [Batman Ninja](/dc/batman-ninja/).
