Victor Fries suffers an accident while trying to save Nora, his wife, terminally ill and frozen while awaiting a cure. Since then he can only live at subzero temperatures, inside a suit that keeps him alive. His crimes fund his research, which makes him one of the saddest villains [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/) faces.

## Comics origin

Dave Wood, Sheldon Moldoff and Bob Kane create him in *Batman* #121, from 1959, under the name Mr. Zero and without much depth. The 1960s TV series gives him his current name. His love story does not arrive until the 1990s animated series, and from there it moves to the comics.

## On screen

### Live action

Arnold Schwarzenegger plays him in [Batman & Robin](/dc/batman-y-robin/), with enormous armor and an endless collection of ice jokes, though Nora's story is still there. Nathan Darrow makes him far more serious in [Gotham](/dc/gotham-t2/), from season two.

### Animation

Jim Meskimen voices a suitless Victor Fries in [Justice League: Gods and Monsters](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-dioses-y-monstruos/), one of the scientists of that alternate world.
