Zod is a general from Krypton condemned to the Phantom Zone for trying to seize power. When he manages to escape and reaches Earth, he has the same gifts as [Superman](/dc/personajes/superman/), but with a military discipline and a hunger for command that make him fearsome. He is what Superman might have been without the Kents: someone sure he was born to rule.

## Comics origin

Robert Bernstein and George Papp introduce him in *Adventure Comics* #283, from April 1961. For years he was just another villain, and he owes much of his fame to film.

## On screen

### Films

Terence Stamp plays him in the prologue of [Superman](/dc/superman/) and above all in [Superman II](/dc/superman-ii-la-aventura-continua/), with the line that made him famous: kneeling before Zod. Michael Shannon is a Kryptonian patriot in [Man of Steel](/dc/el-hombre-de-acero/), with an almost understandable motive: saving his people at any cost. He returns briefly in [The Flash](/dc/flash/). ||In Batman v Superman, his corpse is used to create Doomsday.||

### Series

Callum Blue is a young Zod in [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t9/), Mark Gibbon appears in [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t2/) and Colin Salmon plays him in [Krypton](/dc/krypton-t1/), set on the planet long before its destruction.

### Animation

René Auberjonois voices him in the series [Superman](/dc/superman-1988/). Bruce Thomas does so in [Justice League: Gods and Monsters](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-dioses-y-monstruos/), which gives him an unexpected role in the origin of that Superman.
