Rachel Roth has for a father Trigon, a demon able to destroy worlds. She feels other people's emotions and holds a dark power she can never fully let go of, because when she loses control her father gains ground. That is why she lives contained and quiet, and why the Titans are the only place she fits.

## Comics origin

Marv Wolfman and George Pérez create her in a preview story inside *DC Comics Presents* #26, from 1980, along with the new Titans. She is the one who gathers the team, to help her against her father.

## On screen

### Live action

Teagan Croft plays her in [Titans](/dc/titans-t1/), where the show opens with her, a girl hunted for reasons she does not know who seeks help from Dick Grayson. The Raven listed in [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t2/) with Viv Leacock only shares her name.

### Animation

Taissa Farmiga voices her in [Justice League vs. Teen Titans](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-contra-los-jovenes-titanes/), [Teen Titans: The Judas Contract](/dc/jovenes-titanes-el-contrato-de-judas/) and [Apokolips War](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia-oscura-la-guerra-apokolips/).
