Sara Lance starts out as Laurel's younger sister, the one who vanishes in a shipwreck. She returns as a hardened fighter, and over the years goes from supporting player in [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t1/) to leading the ragtag heroes of *Legends of Tomorrow*. She is one of the few openly bisexual leads of these shows, treated quite naturally.

## Origin

She does not come from the comics, although she takes her name from them. Sara was created for the pilot of [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t1/), in 2012, and her surname connects her to [Black Canary](/dc/personajes/black-canary/).

## On screen

### Series

Jacqueline MacInnes Wood plays her only in the first episode of [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t1/). Caity Lotz takes over from [the second season](/dc/arrow-t2/). ||Sara dies in the third, and coming back to life and moving on takes its toll.||

Lotz then heads the cast of [DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t1/), much more comedic in tone, and appears in crossovers with [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t3/), [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t3/) and [Batwoman](/dc/batwoman-t1/).
