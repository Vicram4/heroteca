In a rotten [Gotham](/dc/wiki/gotham/), Jim Gordon is the cop who won't sell out. He agrees to work with a masked vigilante, knowing the law can't reach everything, and that uneasy alliance is the basis of many [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/) stories. He is usually a tired man with a mustache and a trench coat, held up by sheer stubbornness.

## Comics origin

Bill Finger and Bob Kane create him alongside Batman: he appears in the very first panel of the very first story, in *Detective Comics* #27, from 1939. That makes him the hero's oldest supporting character. The most influential version is Frank Miller's *Year One*, in which he reaches Gotham at the same time as Bruce.

## On screen

### Live-action films

Neil Hamilton appears in [Batman](/dc/batman/), from 1966. Pat Hingle plays him in all four Burton and Schumacher films, and Gary Oldman raises the bar in Nolan's trilogy, from [Batman Begins](/dc/batman-begins/) to [The Dark Knight Rises](/dc/el-caballero-oscuro-la-leyenda-renace/). ||In The Dark Knight he even fakes his death.||

J. K. Simmons takes over in [Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/), and Jeffrey Wright plays an investigative partner in [The Batman](/dc/the-batman/).

### Series

Ben McKenzie leads [Gotham](/dc/gotham-t1/) as a young Gordon just joining the police.

### Animation

Bryan Cranston voices him in [Batman: Year One](/dc/batman-ano-uno/), Ray Wise in [Batman: The Killing Joke](/dc/batman-la-broma-asesina/) and David Selby in [Batman: The Dark Knight Returns](/dc/batman-el-regreso-del-caballero-oscuro/).
