Iris is a journalist with a nose for tough stories and the great love of Barry Allen, [Flash](/dc/personajes/flash/). In the comics she was born as the hero's typical girlfriend, and television has made her much more: in the show she also coordinates the team from base.

## Comics origin

Robert Kanigher and Carmine Infantino create her alongside Barry, and she appears from his debut in *Showcase* #4, from 1956. Over the years she was given a stranger past tied to the future and an important place in the Flash family, since her nephew Wally West ends up inheriting the suit.

## On screen

### Series

Paula Marshall plays her in the pilot of [The Flash](/dc/the-flash/), from 1990, before the character vanished from the story. Candice Patton leads [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/) as the daughter of [Joe West](/dc/personajes/joe-west/), in whose home Barry grows up. She also appears in crossovers with [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t3/) and [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t6/).

### Films

Kiersey Clemons has a short scene in [Zack Snyder's Justice League](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-de-zack-snyder/) and a small part in [The Flash](/dc/flash/).

### Animation

Vicki Lewis voices her in [Justice League: The New Frontier](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-la-nueva-frontera/).
