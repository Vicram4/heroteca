Waylon Jones has a condition that gives him a reptilian look and enormous strength, and he grew up rejected by everyone. In Gotham he has carved out a place as an underworld thug and, depending on the writer, also as a beast who barely reasons. For [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/) he is mainly a physical rival.

## Comics origin

Gerry Conway, Don Newton and Gene Colan introduce him in *Detective Comics* #523, from 1983. He starts as a gangster with a skin problem, and later stories make him more and more animal.

## On screen

### Live action

Adewale Akinnuoye-Agbaje plays him in [Suicide Squad](/dc/escuadron-suicida/), under heavy makeup and with short lines, as one of the members who win the group's sympathy.

### Animation

Fred Tatasciore voices him in [Son of Batman](/dc/el-hijo-de-batman/) and John DiMaggio in [Batman Unlimited: Animal Instincts](/dc/batman-unlimited-instinto-animal/).
