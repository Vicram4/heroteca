Doomsday neither negotiates nor explains anything: he advances and destroys. Each time he dies he returns immune to whatever killed him, which is why he is so hard to stop. His reason for existing comes down to one fight, the one that leaves [Superman](/dc/personajes/superman/) lifeless before all of Metropolis.

## Comics origin

Dan Jurgens creates him for that story. He peeks in at *Superman: The Man of Steel* #17, from 1992, and fully appears in the next issue. *The Death of Superman* becomes one of the biggest comics events of its time, even outside specialist shops.

## On screen

### Live action

In season eight of [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t8/), Dario Delacio is the monster. ||Carrying it inside him unknowingly is Davis Bloome, the paramedic played by Sam Witwer.|| Robin Atkin Downes gives him voice and movement in [Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice](/dc/batman-vs-superman-el-amanecer-de-la-justicia/). ||There Lex Luthor builds him from Zod's body, and the final fight repeats the outcome of the comics.||

### Animation

The two adaptations of the original story are [Superman: Doomsday](/dc/superman-doomsday/), from 2007, and [The Death of Superman](/dc/la-muerte-de-superman/), from 2018, which is much more faithful.
