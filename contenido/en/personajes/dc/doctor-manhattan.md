Jon Osterman was a physicist when an accident disintegrated him. He pieced himself back together as a nearly omnipotent blue being, able to see his past and future at once. The United States uses him as a weapon, but he cares less and less about humans, and that distance is the heart of the character: what does life matter to someone who can remake matter?

## Comics origin

Alan Moore and Dave Gibbons create him for *Watchmen*, published in 1986 and 1987. He is based on a Charlton character, Captain Atom, like nearly the whole cast of the work. He is the only one with real powers in that story.

## On screen

### Live action

Billy Crudup gives him voice and movement in [Watchmen](/dc/watchmen/), with a digital body very faithful to the art and an unsettling calm. In the [Watchmen](/dc/watchmen-2019/) series, set decades later, everybody believes he is on Mars. ||Cal Abar, the protagonist's husband, played by Yahya Abdul-Mateen II, is him, with his memory deliberately erased.||
