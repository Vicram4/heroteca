Hawkgirl flies with wings of special metal and fights with a mace. Her story is famous for its tangles, because DC has told it in several ways: a woman reincarnated again and again alongside her eternal love, Hawkman, or a police officer from the planet Thanagar. Both ideas are still around.

## Comics origin

Gardner Fox and Dennis Neville create Shiera Sanders in *Flash Comics* #1, from 1940, and in 1941 she finally puts on the costume. Shayera Hol, the alien officer, arrives in 1961, and Kendra Saunders, the version television uses, in the late 1990s.

## On screen

### Series

Ciara Renée is Kendra Saunders, a barista with no memory of her past lives, introduced in [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t2/) and [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t4/). She moves on to the first season of [Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t1/), with the couple's eternal enemy, Vandal Savage, and with Falk Hentschel as Hawkman. She leaves when that season ends.
