As a child, Bruce Wayne watches his parents being killed on their way out of a show. As an adult he turns that trauma into a method: years of training, a fortune spent on equipment and a costume designed to frighten the criminals of [Gotham](/dc/wiki/gotham/). He has no superpowers and, in most versions, refuses to kill, two traits that define him almost as much as the cape does.

## Comics origin

He first appears in *Detective Comics* #27, in 1939. For decades only Bob Kane was credited as creator, although Bill Finger, the writer, is now recognized too; the cowl, the gloves and much of what makes the character recognizable came from him.

## On screen

### Live-action films

Michael Keaton opens the character's modern film era with Tim Burton in [Batman](/dc/batman-1989/) and [Batman Returns](/dc/batman-vuelve/). Val Kilmer follows in [Batman Forever](/dc/batman-forever/) and George Clooney in [Batman & Robin](/dc/batman-y-robin/), both far more colorful.

Christian Bale leads Christopher Nolan's more realistic trilogy: [Batman Begins](/dc/batman-begins/), [The Dark Knight](/dc/el-caballero-oscuro/) and [The Dark Knight Rises](/dc/el-caballero-oscuro-la-leyenda-renace/). ||At the end of the trilogy he fakes his death and leaves Gotham in the hands of others.||

Ben Affleck is a weary veteran Batman in [Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice](/dc/batman-vs-superman-el-amanecer-de-la-justicia/) and [Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/). He and Keaton share [The Flash](/dc/flash/), and Robert Pattinson plays a young, more detective-minded Batman in [The Batman](/dc/the-batman/), unconnected to the earlier films.

### Series

[Gotham](/dc/gotham-t1/) follows his teenage years, before the suit, with David Mazouz as Bruce. In [Titans](/dc/titans-t1/) and [Batwoman](/dc/batwoman-t1/) he is mostly a shadow that shapes everyone else.

### Animation

He is the DC character with the most animated titles in the collection. Standouts include adaptations of classic comics such as [Batman: Year One](/dc/batman-ano-uno/), [Batman: The Dark Knight Returns](/dc/batman-el-regreso-del-caballero-oscuro/) and [Batman: The Killing Joke](/dc/batman-la-broma-asesina/), which do not form a single continuity.
