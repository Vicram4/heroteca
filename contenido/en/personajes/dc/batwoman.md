Kate Kane, Bruce Wayne's cousin, leaves the army after refusing to hide that she is a lesbian and finds another way to serve: patrolling [Gotham](/dc/wiki/gotham/) in a red and black suit. Her visibility made her one of DC's most talked-about heroines this century. She is a more military and less solemn take on the [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/) myth.

## Comics origin

The name dates from 1956, when Kathy Kane appears, largely created to give Batman a love interest amid the controversy over comics. The current version, Kate Kane, is introduced in 2006 in the series *52*.

## On screen

### Series

Ruby Rose first plays her in the crossover running through [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t5/), [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t7/) and [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t4/), then leads the first season of [Batwoman](/dc/batwoman-t1/). Rose left after that season, and in the [second](/dc/batwoman-t2/) Javicia Leslie is Ryan Wilder, a new heroine who picks up the suit. ||Kate later returns with another face, that of Wallis Day.||

### Animation

Yvonne Strahovski voices her in [Batman: Bad Blood](/dc/batman-mala-sangre/).
