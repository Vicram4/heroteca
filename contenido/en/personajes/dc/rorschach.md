Walter Kovacs chases criminals under a mask whose ink blots keep shifting, and in a journal he writes what he thinks of the city, which is not kind. He admits neither gray areas nor compromise. He is an uncomfortable character, attractive and frightening at once, and *Watchmen* does not make it easy for the reader to decide.

## Comics origin

Alan Moore and Dave Gibbons create him in *Watchmen*, from 1986, based on The Question, a Charlton hero by Steve Ditko. In *Doomsday Clock*, much later, another person inherits his mask.

## On screen

### Live action

Jackie Earle Haley plays him in [Watchmen](/dc/watchmen/), with the rough voice and the journal that narrates much of the film. ||In the end he refuses to keep the truth quiet and forces Doctor Manhattan to kill him.|| In the [Watchmen](/dc/watchmen-2019/) series, set decades later, his mask has become the symbol of a racist group, the Seventh Kavalry. Trey Butler appears in the cast under his name.
