Eobard Thawne is a man from the future so obsessed with [Flash](/dc/personajes/flash/) that he copies his powers and sets out to destroy his life, traveling back in time. He wants more than to beat him: he wants him to suffer, which is why he targets those around him. He dresses as an inverted Flash, in yellow and red, and he is Barry Allen's quintessential enemy.

## Comics origin

John Broome and Carmine Infantino introduce him in *The Flash* #139, from September 1963. Other speedster enemies have held the title, such as Daniel West, but Thawne remains the main one.

## On screen

### Series

In [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/) he is the mystery that sets the whole series in motion, a man in yellow tied to the death of Barry's mother. Matt Letscher plays him with Thawne's real face, also in [DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t2/). ||During the first season he hides behind the identity of Harrison Wells, which is why Tom Cavanagh plays him much of the time, including in the Arrow and Supergirl crossovers.||
