Ray Palmer is a scientist who finds a way to shrink himself down to microscopic size while keeping all his strength. On television he is also a friendly, chatty good guy with a new idea every minute, wearing a suit he builds himself. In a genre full of tormented heroes, his enthusiasm is welcome.

## Comics origin

There was an earlier Atom, without powers, in the 1940s. Ray Palmer's debuts in *Showcase* #34, from October 1961, as part of the Silver Age renewal, drawing on the science of the time.

## On screen

### Series

Brandon Routh plays him in [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t3/), where he comes to own a major company, and is one of the founders of [DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t1/). He also passes through [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/) and [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t5/). Routh had been [Superman](/dc/personajes/superman/) on film, a wink the show itself enjoys.

### Animation

Routh continues by voice in [Vixen](/dc/vixen-t2/). Dee Bradley Baker voices an alternate version in [Justice League: Gods and Monsters](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-dioses-y-monstruos/).
