Ray Terrill can turn into light and fly at high speed, a power that also puts him at risk if he does not control it. On screen he is a shy, kind, gay man who inherits his powers from a double from another universe, on an Earth where the Nazis won the war.

## Comics origin

The first Ray is Happy Terrill, created by Lou Fine in *Smash Comics* #14, from 1940, for Quality Comics. DC later buys the publisher. His son Ray arrives through Jack C. Harris and Joe Quesada in *The Ray* #1, from 1992.

## On screen

### Live action

Russell Tovey plays him in the Crisis on Earth-X crossover, on [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t4/) and [Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t3/), and makes a brief appearance in [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t5/).

### Animation

Tovey himself voices [Freedom Fighters: The Ray](/dc/the-ray-t1/), the animated series in which the character leads his own story.
