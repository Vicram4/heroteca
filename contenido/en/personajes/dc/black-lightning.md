Jefferson Pierce generates electricity with his body, but what truly defines him is his job: running a school in a tough neighborhood. He is a hero with his feet firmly in his community, and the stories give him more to do as a father and teacher than most superheroes get.

## Comics origin

He is created by Jenny Blake Isabella and artist Trevor Von Eeden in *Black Lightning* #1, from 1977. He was DC's first Black superhero with his own series, and his daughters Anissa and Jennifer later become heroes too.

## On screen

### Live action

Cress Williams plays him in [Black Lightning](/dc/black-lightning-t1/), which opens with him nine years into retirement and a gang threatening his family. His daughters are central to the show from the start. Williams also appears in the big Crisis on Infinite Earths crossover, in [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t6/) and [Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t5/).

### Animation

LeVar Burton voices him in [Superman/Batman: Public Enemies](/dc/superman-batman-enemigos-publicos/) and Cedric Yarbrough in [Justice League: Crisis on Two Earths](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-crisis-en-dos-tierras/).
