Alec Holland was researching a formula to make plants grow in impossible places when he was murdered and his lab blew up. From the swamp came a plant monster. He is sad, peaceful most of the time and terrifying when someone threatens nature or Abby, the woman he loves.

## Comics origin

Len Wein and Bernie Wrightson create him in *House of Secrets* #92, from 1971, as a standalone horror tale from another era, and a year later they give Holland his series. Alan Moore reinvents it in the 1980s with a famous idea: ||he is not Holland turned into a plant but a plant that believes it is Holland.||

## On screen

### Live action

Wes Craven directs [Swamp Thing](/dc/la-cosa-del-pantano/), from 1982, with Ray Wise as Holland and Dick Durock inside the suit, and Durock returns in [The Return of Swamp Thing](/dc/el-regreso-de-la-cosa-del-pantano/), a much more lighthearted sequel. [Swamp Thing](/dc/swamp-thing-t1/), from 2019, is serious horror with Andy Bean as Holland and Derek Mears as the creature, canceled after a single season.

### Animation

Roger Cross voices him in [Justice League Dark](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-oscura/) and [Apokolips War](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia-oscura-la-guerra-apokolips/).
