Billy Batson is a boy without a family who receives an enormous gift from an old wizard: by saying "Shazam!" he turns into an adult hero with the powers of six legendary figures. Inside he is still a kid, and that is the charm of the character. He is a superhero who has fun being one, very unlike the grave tone of other DC colleagues.

## Comics origin

Bill Parker and C. C. Beck create him for Fawcett in *Whiz Comics* #2, from 1940, under the name Captain Marvel, and during the 1940s he outsells Superman. A long lawsuit by DC ends his adventures in the 1950s. DC brings him back in the 1970s and, in the 2010s, officially names him Shazam. His rival is [Black Adam](/dc/personajes/black-adam/).

## On screen

### Live action

Two actors share him in [Shazam!](/dc/shazam/) and [Shazam! Fury of the Gods](/dc/shazam-la-furia-de-los-dioses/): Zachary Levi is the adult hero and Asher Angel is Billy. The films are family comedies centered on a foster family.

### Animation

Sean Astin is the hero in [Justice League: War](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-guerra/), with Zach Callison as Billy, and returns in [Throne of Atlantis](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-el-trono-de-atlantis/) and [Apokolips War](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia-oscura-la-guerra-apokolips/). Under the name Captain Marvel, Corey Burton voices him in [Superman/Batman: Public Enemies](/dc/superman-batman-enemigos-publicos/).
