A baby reaches Earth in a ship from a planet about to explode and is adopted by the Kents, a farming couple from Smallville. As an adult, Clark Kent works as a reporter in Metropolis and, under the yellow sun, wields nearly limitless powers. The interesting part has never been his strength, but the fact that someone who could do anything chooses to behave well.

## Comics origin

Jerry Siegel and Joe Shuster, two young men from Cleveland, launch him in *Action Comics* #1, on sale in April 1938. That issue opens the superhero comic as we know it, and DC bought the rights for a mere 130 dollars.

## On screen

### Live-action films

George Reeves plays him in the modest [Superman and the Mole Men](/dc/superman-y-los-hombres-topo/), from 1951. Christopher Reeve makes him an icon in [Superman](/dc/superman/), [Superman II](/dc/superman-ii-la-aventura-continua/), [Superman III](/dc/superman-iii/) and [Superman IV: The Quest for Peace](/dc/superman-iv-en-busca-de-la-paz/). Brandon Routh picks up that legacy in [Superman Returns](/dc/superman-returns/).

Henry Cavill is a gloomier Superman in [Man of Steel](/dc/el-hombre-de-acero/) and [Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice](/dc/batman-vs-superman-el-amanecer-de-la-justicia/), and continues in [Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/) and [Zack Snyder's Justice League](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-de-zack-snyder/). ||Cavill returns briefly in the post-credits scene of Black Adam.||

### Series

Tom Welling plays a teenage Clark over ten seasons of [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t1/), with no suit until the very end. Tyler Hoechlin is Superman from the second season of [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t2/) on. In [Batwoman](/dc/batwoman-t1/), [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t6/) and [DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t5/), the *Crisis on Infinite Earths* crossover brings Hoechlin, Routh and Welling together.

### Animation

Along with Batman, he has the most animated films in the collection. Highlights include [All Star Superman](/dc/superman-viaja-al-sol/), [Superman: Red Son](/dc/superman-hijo-rojo/) and [The Death of Superman](/dc/la-muerte-de-superman/), where Jerry O'Connell repeats a voice he had used in other films. In 1988 he also leads the series [Superman](/dc/superman-1988/).
