Pamela Isley is a botanist left by an experiment with poisoned blood and the power to control plants. With her pheromones and her deadly kiss, she defends nature by any means, which makes her more a radical ecologist than a simple [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/) villain. In recent years, her friendship and romance with [Harley Quinn](/dc/personajes/harley-quinn/) have made her very popular.

## Comics origin

Robert Kanigher and Sheldon Moldoff, with Carmine Infantino, introduce her in *Batman* #181, from June 1966, as a seductress with little backstory. Her ecological side develops later.

## On screen

### Live action

Uma Thurman plays her with great pomp in [Batman & Robin](/dc/batman-y-robin/). In [Gotham](/dc/gotham-t4/), Peyton List is the last of several actresses to play her, an adult version of the girl from the early seasons.

### Animation

Paget Brewster is her voice in [Batman and Harley Quinn](/dc/batman-y-harley-quinn/), Tara Strong in [Scooby-Doo! & Batman: The Brave and the Bold](/dc/scooby-doo-y-el-intrepido-batman/) and Kari Wührer in [Batman: Gotham by Gaslight](/dc/batman-gotham-a-luz-de-gas/). Peyton List returns to the role by voice in [Batman: Hush](/dc/batman-hush/).
