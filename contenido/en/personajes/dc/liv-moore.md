Olivia Moore had a perfect life as a medical resident in Seattle until a party on a boat turned her into a zombie. To keep her mind she needs to eat brains, so she takes a job at the morgue. Each brain gives her the dead person's memories and personality for a while, which is as useful for solving crimes as it is absurd for her life. Her name is a joke: Liv Moore sounds like "live more".

## Comics origin

The show is loosely based on *iZombie*, by Chris Roberson and Michael Allred, published by Vertigo from 2010. Liv does not come from there: the comic's lead is someone else, a gravedigger named Gwen. The premise of eating brains and inheriting memories does remain.

## On screen

### Series

Rose McIver plays her in all five seasons of [iZombie](/dc/izombie-t1/), created by Rob Thomas and Diane Ruggiero-Wright. The best part of the role is being someone else each week: the actress changes habits and even her walk according to the brain of the moment, while helping a detective who does not know what she is. Each [season](/dc/izombie-t2/) widens the world of the zombies, with an ever more complicated Seattle.
