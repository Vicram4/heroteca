Victor Stone was a sports prospect until an accident in his father's lab nearly killed him. To save him, his father implants machine parts, and Victor wakes with enormous strength and access to networks, but also with the impression of having stopped being a person. That is his conflict: what remains human in him.

## Comics origin

Marv Wolfman and George Pérez create him in a preview inside *DC Comics Presents* #26, from 1980, and he is a pillar of the New Teen Titans. Since 2011 he has also belonged to the Justice League.

## On screen

### Live action

Lee Thompson Young plays him in [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t5/). Ray Fisher appears for a moment in [Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice](/dc/batman-vs-superman-el-amanecer-de-la-justicia/) and is one of the heroes of [Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/). [Zack Snyder's Justice League](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-de-zack-snyder/) gives him much more weight and tells his full story.

Joivan Wade is Cyborg in [Doom Patrol](/dc/doom-patrol-t1/), returning briefly in [DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t5/).

### Animation

Shemar Moore voices him in [Justice League: War](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-guerra/) and the films that follow, up to [Justice League Dark: Apokolips War](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia-oscura-la-guerra-apokolips/). Michael B. Jordan does so in [Justice League: The Flashpoint Paradox](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-la-paradoja-del-tiempo/).
