Leonard Snart is a planning kind of thief, and he figured out that cold was the best way to slow somebody fast. He is the boss of the Rogues, a gang of crooks with their own code: no killing without a reason. That is why it is never fully clear whether he is the enemy or an ally of the [Flash](/dc/personajes/flash/).

## Comics origin

John Broome and Carmine Infantino create him in *Showcase* #8, from 1957. He is Barry Allen's first great villain, and the group he eventually gathers becomes a hallmark of the Flash comics.

## On screen

### Live action

Wentworth Miller plays him from season one of [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/), very ironic and drawling, paired with Dominic Purcell, his *Prison Break* co-star. He becomes a lead in [Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t1/). ||He sacrifices himself at the end of that first season, though it will not be the last time we see Miller, thanks to time travel and other universes.||

### Animation

Kevin Michael Richardson voices him in two LEGO films, [Justice League vs. Bizarro League](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-contra-la-liga-de-bizarro/) and [Attack of the Legion of Doom!](/dc/lego-dc-comics-super-heroes-la-liga-de-la-justicia-el-ataque-de-la-legion-del-mal/).
