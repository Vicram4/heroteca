Ra's al Ghul has lived for centuries thanks to the waters of the Lazarus Pits and leads the League of Assassins, a global network that obeys his orders. His aim is to save the planet, even if it costs millions of lives. He is one of the few foes who treats [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/) as an equal, and he even wants him as his heir, with the help of his daughter [Talia](/dc/personajes/talia-al-ghul/).

## Comics origin

Dennis O'Neil and Neal Adams, with editor Julius Schwartz, introduce him in *Batman* #232, from June 1971, in a story that gave Batman back his side as an international detective and took him away from the legacy of the 1960s show.

## On screen

### Films

Ken Watanabe plays the leader of the League of Shadows in [Batman Begins](/dc/batman-begins/). ||He is in fact a decoy: the real Ra's is Ducard, Bruce's mentor, played by Liam Neeson, who appears in a vision in the third film of the trilogy.||

### Series

Matt Nable plays him in [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t3/), as a leader who tests Oliver, and returns briefly in [DC's Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t1/). Alexander Siddig plays him in [Gotham](/dc/gotham-t3/), where he connects with young Bruce.

### Animation

Jason Isaacs voices him in [Batman: Under the Red Hood](/dc/batman-capucha-roja/), Giancarlo Esposito in [Son of Batman](/dc/el-hijo-de-batman/) and Terrence C. Carson in [Justice League vs. Teen Titans](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-contra-los-jovenes-titanes/).
