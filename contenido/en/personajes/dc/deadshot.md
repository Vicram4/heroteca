Floyd Lawton never misses a shot and charges dearly for it. Powerless, with a sight on his eye and guns on his wrists, he works for the highest bidder, and only the daughter he barely sees keeps him tied to something like a life. He is a regular of the Suicide Squad, where he busies himself with surviving and asking to be left alone.

## Comics origin

David Vern Reed and Lew Sayre Schwartz create him in *Batman* #59, from 1950, as a fake vigilante trying to replace the hero. He spends decades forgotten until the 1970s revive him with the red suit he still wears.

## On screen

### Live action

Bradley Stryker appears in [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t10/). Michael Rowe plays him in [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t1/), with an important role in John Diggle's life. Will Smith, in [Suicide Squad](/dc/escuadron-suicida/), is one of the film's leads.

### Animation

Jim Meskimen voices him in [Batman: Gotham Knight](/dc/batman-guardian-de-gotham/), Neal McDonough in [Batman: Assault on Arkham](/dc/batman-el-asalto-de-arkham/) and Christian Slater in [Suicide Squad: Hell to Pay](/dc/escuadron-suicida-consecuencias-infernales/).
