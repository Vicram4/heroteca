Lightning strikes the police lab and douses Barry Allen in chemicals: from then on he runs so fast he can cross dimensions and travel through time. He is an upbeat hero, always in a hurry, but he usually carries a wound behind him, the murder of his mother, for which his father is blamed. That guilt feeds nearly all his storylines.

## Comics origin

The first Flash is Jay Garrick, from 1940. Barry arrives in *Showcase* #4, from October 1956, by Robert Kanigher and Carmine Infantino. His success convinced DC to revive its old heroes, which is why he is seen as the start of the so-called Silver Age.

## On screen

### Series

John Wesley Shipp plays him in the 1990 series, which yields three films: [The Flash](/dc/the-flash/), [The Flash II: Revenge of the Trickster](/dc/the-flash-ii-la-venganza-del-mago-asesino/) and [The Flash III: Deadly Nightshade](/dc/the-flash-iii-la-sombra-mortal/). Grant Gustin debuts in the second season of [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t2/) and then leads [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t1/), from which he visits the other series of the same universe. Shipp returns in that show as Henry Allen, Barry's father, and also as Jay Garrick.

### Live-action films

Ezra Miller appears for a moment in [Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice](/dc/batman-vs-superman-el-amanecer-de-la-justicia/) and joins [Justice League](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia/) before leading [The Flash](/dc/flash/), from 2023, in which Barry tries to save his mother by traveling to the past.

### Animation

Christopher Gorham voices him in several League films, such as [Justice League: War](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-guerra/). Justin Chambers plays him in [Justice League: The Flashpoint Paradox](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-la-paradoja-del-tiempo/), adapting the same comic that inspires the 2023 film.
