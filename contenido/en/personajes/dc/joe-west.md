Joe West is a detective in Central City and the father of [Iris West](/dc/personajes/iris-west/). When Barry Allen's father goes to prison, he takes the boy in and raises him as a son. He is the person the [Flash](/dc/personajes/flash/) turns to when he needs someone to tell him the truth, and much of the show's warm tone comes from him.

## Comics origin

He is essentially a creation of the TV show. In the classic comics, Iris has a different family, and this police father is designed for the screen.
