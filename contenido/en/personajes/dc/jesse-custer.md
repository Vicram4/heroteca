Jesse Custer is a preacher in a Texas town, short on faith and with a violent past, when something called Genesis gets inside him: the child of an angel and a demon, which lets him force anyone to obey what he says. He decides to use it to find God, who has abandoned heaven, and demand answers. With him go his ex-girlfriend, Tulip, and an Irish vampire, Cassidy.

## Comics origin

Garth Ennis and Steve Dillon create him in *Preacher*, published from 1995 by Vertigo, DC's mature imprint. The work is known for being blasphemous and brutal, and also for how it talks about friendship.

## On screen

### Live action

Dominic Cooper plays him in [Preacher](/dc/preacher-t1/), developed among others by Seth Rogen and Evan Goldberg. Season one takes place almost entirely in his town, Annville, and differs greatly from the comic. From [season two](/dc/preacher-t2/) the search begins, and [season three](/dc/preacher-t3/) takes him to where he grew up, Angelville.
