Black Canary is one of DC's best hand-to-hand fighters and, in many versions, has an ultrasonic scream that can bring down walls. The title has passed from mother to daughter in the comics and been shared by several women on television, so different characters are gathered here. She is often tied to [Green Arrow](/dc/personajes/flecha-verde/), forming one of the genre's classic couples.

## Comics origin

Robert Kanigher and Carmine Infantino create Dinah Drake in *Flash Comics* #86, from 1947. The second, Dinah Laurel Lance, enters in *Justice League of America* #75, from 1969, by Dennis O'Neil and Dick Dillin, and brings the sonic scream as a fixed trait.

## On screen

### Series

Alaina Huffman is Dinah Lance in [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t7/). In [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t1/), Katie Cassidy plays Laurel, a lawyer who ends up taking the name, and Juliana Harkavy plays Dinah Drake, a cop who arrives in later seasons. Alex Kingston, also in these titles, is the Lance sisters' mother. The other sister has her own entry: [White Canary](/dc/personajes/white-canary/). ||Laurel dies in Arrow, and Cassidy returns as an evil Laurel from another Earth.||

### Films

Jurnee Smollett-Bell is a club singer who discovers her power in [Birds of Prey](/dc/aves-de-presa-y-la-fantabulosa-emancipacion-de-harley-quinn/), alongside [Harley Quinn](/dc/personajes/harley-quinn/).

### Animation

Katie Cassidy repeats by voice in [Vixen](/dc/vixen-t2/). Grey DeLisle plays her in [Scooby-Doo! & Batman: The Brave and the Bold](/dc/scooby-doo-y-el-intrepido-batman/) and Kari Wührer in [Justice League: Crisis on Two Earths](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-crisis-en-dos-tierras/).
