Edward Nygma is a criminal with an obsession: he has to prove he is smarter than anyone, so he leaves riddles at every crime scene, even if it gives him away. He wears green with question marks, and his war with [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/) is above all one of wits. In his mind, victory is worth nothing if no one knows he won.

## Comics origin

Bill Finger and Dick Sprang introduce him in *Detective Comics* #140, from October 1948. He appeared little in his first decades, and it was 1960s television that made him one of the main foes.

## On screen

### Films

Frank Gorshin, histrionic and giggling, plays him in [Batman](/dc/batman/), from 1966. Jim Carrey takes that energy to the extreme in [Batman Forever](/dc/batman-forever/). Paul Dano offers a radical turn in [The Batman](/dc/the-batman/): a serial killer inspired by the Zodiac.

### Series

Cory Michael Smith plays, in [Gotham](/dc/gotham-t1/), a shy, brilliant police forensic technician who slowly falls apart over the show.

### Animation

Matthew Gray Gubler voices him in [Batman: Assault on Arkham](/dc/batman-el-asalto-de-arkham/) and Wally Wingert in [Batman vs. Two-Face](/dc/batman-vs-dos-caras/). Geoffrey Arend plays him in [Batman: Hush](/dc/batman-hush/).
