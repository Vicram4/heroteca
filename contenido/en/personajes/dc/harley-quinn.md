Harleen Quinzel is a psychiatrist at Arkham Asylum who falls in love with her most dangerous patient, the [Joker](/dc/personajes/joker/), and abandons her career to become his partner in crime. Cheerful, violent and very smart under the clown disguise, she has gradually broken free of that toxic relationship to become a lead in her own right, and sometimes a heroine.

## Comics origin

Interestingly, she does not come from the comics: Paul Dini and Bruce Timm create her for *Batman: The Animated Series*, and she debuts in a September 1992 episode. Conceived as a minor character, her popularity soon took her to the comics and later everywhere.

## On screen

### Films

Margot Robbie has defined her for the wider public in [Suicide Squad](/dc/escuadron-suicida/), [Birds of Prey](/dc/aves-de-presa-y-la-fantabulosa-emancipacion-de-harley-quinn/), in which she breaks up with the Joker, and [The Suicide Squad](/dc/el-escuadron-suicida/).

### Animation

Hynden Walch voices her in [Batman: Assault on Arkham](/dc/batman-el-asalto-de-arkham/), [Batman: Hush](/dc/batman-hush/) and [Justice League Dark: Apokolips War](/dc/liga-de-la-justicia-oscura-la-guerra-apokolips/). Tara Strong plays her in [Suicide Squad: Hell to Pay](/dc/escuadron-suicida-consecuencias-infernales/) and Melissa Rauch stars in the rowdy [Batman and Harley Quinn](/dc/batman-y-harley-quinn/). In [Batman Ninja](/dc/batman-ninja/), Rie Kugimiya voices her in Japanese.
