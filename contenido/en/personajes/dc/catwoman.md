Selina Kyle steals jewels across [Gotham](/dc/wiki/gotham/)'s rooftops with a whip, claws and a cat suit, and depending on who writes her she is a villain, an antiheroine or the love of [Batman](/dc/personajes/batman/)'s life. She has no interest in destroying anything, only in living her own way and protecting her own. In parts of Latin America she is known as Gatúbela.

## Comics origin

Bill Finger and Bob Kane introduce her in *Batman* #1, from 1940, then called just the Cat, as a thief the hero lets slip away. From day one, the game of seduction is part of the deal.

## On screen

### Films

Lee Meriwether plays her in [Batman](/dc/batman/), from 1966. Michelle Pfeiffer delivers a legendary, tormented version in [Batman Returns](/dc/batman-vuelve/). Anne Hathaway is an elegant thief in [The Dark Knight Rises](/dc/el-caballero-oscuro-la-leyenda-renace/), and Zoë Kravitz a woman with scores to settle in [The Batman](/dc/the-batman/).

Halle Berry leads [Catwoman](/dc/catwoman/), from 2004, with a new character, Patience Phillips, unrelated to Batman. The film was a resounding flop.

### Series

Camren Bicondova plays her as a teen, a street kid, in [Gotham](/dc/gotham-t1/). Lili Simmons is the adult Selina of the show's ending.

### Animation

Julie Newmar, the Catwoman of the 1960s series, voices her in [Batman vs. Two-Face](/dc/batman-vs-dos-caras/). Jennifer Morrison does so in [Batman: Hush](/dc/batman-hush/) and Jennifer Carpenter in [Batman: Gotham by Gaslight](/dc/batman-gotham-a-luz-de-gas/).
