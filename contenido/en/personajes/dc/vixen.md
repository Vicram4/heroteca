Mari McCabe was born in Zambesi, a fictional African country, and inherits a family totem, the Tantu, which gives her the abilities of any animal: the strength of an elephant, the speed of a cheetah. She earns her living as a model when she debuts in the comics, and in time she reaches the Justice League.

## Comics origin

She was going to be DC's first African superheroine to headline a comic, but that debut was canceled in the great publishing crisis of 1978. Gerry Conway and Bob Oksner finally bring her out in *Action Comics* #521, from 1981, in a story with Superman.

## On screen

### Live action

Megalyn Echikunwoke moves from voice to live action in an episode of [Arrow](/dc/arrow-t4/). [Legends of Tomorrow](/dc/dc-s-legends-of-tomorrow-t2/) introduces another person with the same totem: her grandmother Amaya Jiwe, played by Maisie Richardson-Sellers, a World War II heroine.

### Animation

Megalyn Echikunwoke voices Mari in [Vixen](/dc/vixen-t1/), the animated series with which the character debuts in this universe.
