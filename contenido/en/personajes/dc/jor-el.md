Jor-El is the scientist who discovers Krypton is going to explode and whom nobody heeds. Unable to save his world, he puts his son in a ship and sends him to Earth. He dies early in almost every story, yet stays present as a wise figure and as the conscience of [Superman](/dc/personajes/superman/), in the form of memories, crystals or holograms.

## Comics origin

Jerry Siegel and Joe Shuster introduce him in the Superman newspaper strip, in January 1939, with his name then spelled Jor-L. The Krypton origin later expanded over many stories.

## On screen

### Films

Marlon Brando plays him in [Superman](/dc/superman/), with the highest salary of its day for a few minutes of film. Footage of him is reused in [Superman Returns](/dc/superman-returns/). Russell Crowe has a far more active role in [Man of Steel](/dc/el-hombre-de-acero/), with action in the opening, and keeps guiding his son as a hologram. He reappears in [Zack Snyder's Justice League](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-de-zack-snyder/).

### Series

In [Smallville](/dc/smallville-t2/) he is an imposing voice, that of Terence Stamp, who had been [General Zod](/dc/personajes/zod/) on film. Julian Sands plays him in person in [the ninth season](/dc/smallville-t9/).
