Gar Logan can turn into any animal, always green, and he is the group's clown, the one who cracks a joke when things get serious. Underneath is a kid who lost his parents and found in the Titans the family he lacked. He has a well-known soft spot for [Raven](/dc/personajes/raven/).

## Comics origin

Arnold Drake and Bob Brown create him in *Doom Patrol* #99, from 1965, before he is a Titan. A strange virus turns him green and the cure gives him his powers. His jump to the New Teen Titans of the 1980s, under the name Changeling, makes him far more popular.

## On screen

### Live action

Ryan Potter plays him in [Titans](/dc/titans-t1/), a more serious take than usual, without green skin most of the time, who joins the group in season one.

### Animation

Brandon Soo Hoo voices him in [Justice League vs. Teen Titans](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-contra-los-jovenes-titanes/) and [Teen Titans: The Judas Contract](/dc/jovenes-titanes-el-contrato-de-judas/).
