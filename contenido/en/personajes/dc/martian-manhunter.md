J'onn J'onzz reaches Earth by accident or fleeing the extinction of his people, depending on the version. He is one of DC's most powerful heroes, with telepathy, invisibility and shapeshifting, but what defines him is the loneliness of the last of his kind. He lives among humans with a borrowed face, patiently watching.

## Comics origin

Joseph Samachson and Joe Certa create him in a story in *Detective Comics* #225, from November 1955: a scientist accidentally brings him to Earth with an experiment. He is part of the first Justice League lineup.

## On screen

### Series

David Harewood plays J'onn in [Supergirl](/dc/supergirl-t1/) and, from there, visits [The Flash](/dc/the-flash-t3/). ||In the show he hides for a while behind the identity of Hank Henshaw, a senior government official.||

### Films

Harry Lennix plays, in [Zack Snyder's Justice League](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-de-zack-snyder/), a general already seen in [Man of Steel](/dc/el-hombre-de-acero/). ||At the end of the film he is revealed as J'onn in disguise.||

### Animation

Miguel Ferrer voices him in [Justice League: The New Frontier](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-la-nueva-frontera/), and Carl Lumbly, his voice on television in the 2000s, returns in [Justice League: Doom](/dc/la-liga-de-la-justicia-perdicion/). Nyambi Nyambi plays him in [The Death of Superman](/dc/la-muerte-de-superman/).
